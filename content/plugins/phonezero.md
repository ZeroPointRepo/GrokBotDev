## What it does

PhoneZero enables any scenario where you need Grok Bot to pick up the phone and call someone to complete a task. Restaurant reservation in a foreign country and language? Need to check operating hours for a business? Using PhoneZero, Grok Bot can trigger an active call via Telnyx and your Grok Voice Agent to complete the task.

There's nothing to host. You just need Telnyx and an xAI developer account. Grok Bot does most of the setup with a little help from you.

## Use it in Grok Bot

Paste https://github.com/function1st/PhoneZero into this chat and ask it to help you set PhoneZero up. If you don’t have an existing Telnyx or SpaceXAI dev account, ask Grok Bot to walk you through the prerequisites as well.

A six-minute call is usually about $0.40 to $0.55 on Telnyx and xAI.
