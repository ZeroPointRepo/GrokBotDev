## What it does

Serves one chosen quotation a day that stays put until tomorrow, and searches the rest by idea rather than exact wording, so you can find a line you only half remember. Every result carries a link back to where it came from, and it will test a shaky attribution before you repeat it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
