## What it does

Share a few details about yourself, pick a country, and it invents a fitting fictional identity: a given name, a surname, plus a nickname for cultures that use them, rendered in the local script when there is one. The framing is travel and fiction, an alias for a trip or a character, not impersonation. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
