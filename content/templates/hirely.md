## What it does

Job hunting with a teammate rather than a job board. It gathers openings from employers' own careers pages, shortlists the ones that fit, and helps you put an application together — without submitting until you have reviewed it and said go. After that it tracks your follow-ups so nothing goes quiet. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
