## What it does

Runs a weekday pass across every channel in the workspace, reading it from leadership's vantage point to pull out problems needing a decision and wins worth naming. Congratulation posts reach the company-wide channel only once a human has approved the wording, and vendor signatures stay out of whatever it writes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
