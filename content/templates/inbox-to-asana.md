## What it does

Mail arrives as a wall of messages; work arrives as a list. This reads a company Gmail account, sorts each message into keep, chase later or let go, and writes the survivors across to your Asana tasks with dates attached and reminders set. It never replies and never sends anything out from your address.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @wikiwayne shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
