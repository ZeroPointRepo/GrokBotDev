## What it does

Each morning this bot works through what is climbing GitHub's trending list and picks out a small number of projects to write up in Chinese, with a plain summary of the problem each one actually solves. The notes double as raw material for tech posts or a personal reading archive. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
