## What it does

Aimed at codebases that gather small messes faster than anyone gets round to clearing them. While you sleep it works through the project to build a picture of how the parts fit together, then commits to one narrow, contained improvement in each area it studied: visible by morning, small enough to review without dread. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
