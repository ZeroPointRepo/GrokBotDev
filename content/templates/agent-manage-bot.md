## What it does

Sits above a two-tier setup where Grok bots handle the thinking and Mac command-line tools do the executing. Work is sorted into separate research and coding rooms, briefs that missed their slot get chased, and a recurring lab session keeps the rig exercised. Terminal sessions underneath stay attached to each layer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
