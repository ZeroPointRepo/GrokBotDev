## What it does

Report your walking total in a message and Loaf answers in character, reacting to how active the day was and checking in again each evening. The tone is deliberately gentle: a day spent resting is treated as part of looking after the creature, not as a failure to make up for. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
