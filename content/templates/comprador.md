## What it does

Hand it a product link and it keeps watch on launches and restocks, joins the queue at the right moment, and carries the order through to checkout on a connected agent virtual card with a spending cap. Every single purchase stops for your approval before any payment is released. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
