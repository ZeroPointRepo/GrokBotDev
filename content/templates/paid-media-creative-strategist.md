## What it does

Reviews early creative performance and calls the winners before the data makes it obvious, pairing each pick with a hypothesis about why it works and the next test worth running. It refuses to invent numbers to fit a story, so the reasoning stays honest even when the sample is still small. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
