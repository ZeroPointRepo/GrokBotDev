## What it does

Its focus is short vertical talking-head video: hand over raw footage and it picks the strongest takes, then builds the edit with subtitles, cutaway clips, motion graphics and a music track. Nothing is delivered unreviewed — each cut is checked first, so what arrives is a finished short, not an assembly to repair. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
