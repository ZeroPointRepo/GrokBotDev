## What it does

Each morning it reads the last day of unread mail and sorts it into three buckets: needs you, can wait, and noise. Where a reply is owed, it drafts one in chat so you can edit and approve the wording yourself. Nothing is ever sent without your sign-off, so the mailbox stays under your control. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
