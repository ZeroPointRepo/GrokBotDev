## What it does

Give it your ideal-customer profile and it scores Instagram creators against that bar, so your outreach list starts qualified instead of guessed. For each one it hands you a hook drawn from that creator's own recent feed plus a ready-to-edit DM you send yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
