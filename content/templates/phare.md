## What it does

Starts as a tutor: it walks you through generative-AI foundations using official coursework rather than random blog posts, at whatever pace you set. As it learns how you work it takes on a second job, watching your priorities and following up on what you said you would do. The tone stays calm rather than pushy. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
