## What it does

Rather than a single assistant trying to cover everything, this is a front desk with staff behind it — someone taking notes, someone on the inbox, a writer, a scheduler, a calendar keeper and a deliberate sceptic. Work is passed along through written briefs and shared files, with mail handled by an agent mailbox. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
