## What it does

Opens with a handful of quick either-or questions so it learns the way you want it run. From then on it takes whatever loose notes have piled up, works them into a single written brief, and hands back an ordered list of the decisions genuinely waiting on you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @mjjefford shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
