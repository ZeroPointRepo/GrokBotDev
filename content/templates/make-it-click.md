## What it does

A study companion that begins with the knowledge you already hold rather than a textbook chapter. Give it a subject and it builds the explanation out of ideas you are comfortable with, so each new concept has something to attach to. Aimed at anyone who bounced off a topic once and wants it to land this time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
