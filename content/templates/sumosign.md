## What it does

Turns a plain conversation into a signing desk. You connect the credentials from your own vendor account, hand over the file, mark the spots where each party has to sign or initial, and it dispatches the request to them. Registering an account gets you a starter key at no charge. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SumoSign shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
