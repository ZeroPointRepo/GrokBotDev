## What it does

Works as the mail desk for a shared household: it reads the USPS Informed Delivery summary each morning, records every letter and parcel listed, and files bills under whoever in the house pays them. The result is one running log of everything headed to your door, so items stop going missing and due dates stop slipping. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
