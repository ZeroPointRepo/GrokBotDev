## What it does

Replaces a four-bot X workflow with a single template that plays every role itself. It scouts real sources for material, drafts a few opening hooks that sound like you, and attaches fact-check notes before anything goes out. Reference material on the algorithm and direct-message handling round out the kit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
