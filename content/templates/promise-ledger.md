## What it does

Paste the notes from a customer call and it hands back three things: a short recap, a list of every commitment made during the conversation on both ends, and a dated list of what to chase next. It was built by a home-services owner, so it reads like the paperwork an HVAC or plumbing shop actually needs after a job. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
