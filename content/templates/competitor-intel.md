## What it does

Aim it at your local market and it researches the service companies you are up against, then files a weekly report of the moves they made, with a citation for each claim. It stays strictly read-only: no outreach on your behalf, no posting anywhere, and no advice on what you should charge. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
