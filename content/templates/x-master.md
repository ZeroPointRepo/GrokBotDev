## What it does

Runs a light desk for one professional X account: quick skims of the day, wry draft posts that cost nothing in API fees by default, and optional watches on contest or marketplace threads you follow. Every post waits for an explicit human go-ahead, and it will never auto cross-post to other accounts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ladybarrelmaker shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
