## What it does

Explains the load arithmetic an electrician runs before a Level 2 charger joins a household panel, following the National Electrical Code's Article 220 method. Use it to check an installer's quote before you commit money to the work. It doubles as the free education arm of a paid assessment service. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
