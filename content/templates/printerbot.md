## What it does

Tell it what a bot is called and roughly what that bot does, and it renders a figure in the house style: a smooth pale head, dark capsule eyes, props hinting at the job, and a backdrop themed to the domain. The result is a consistent cast of avatars rather than a pile of mismatched images. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @viticci shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
