## What it does

While you sleep it reads through your inbox and the day's appointments, chooses what matters, and typesets it as a small personal newspaper. Then it sends the layout to your printer, so the first thing you pick up in the morning is a paper edition of your own agenda rather than a phone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
