## What it does

Feed it the tools, launches and articles piling up in your reading list and it scores each one against the goals you have written down. Every item comes back filed as keep, park or kill, maybes get a revisit date, and it stays silent unless something clears the bar. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @the_Arow_H shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
