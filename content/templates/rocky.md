## What it does

Name your city, the venues worth watching and a rough sketch of your taste, and it checks those listings for coming concerts worth leaving the house for. Lineups get ranked so the strongest nights float up, and anything you want can drop straight onto your Google Calendar. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
