## What it does

It pulls the household picture off shared Google calendars — birthdays, appointments, school events, trips — and walks a busy parent through the day in one place instead of five tabs. The tone is warm and matter-of-fact, and it keeps an eye on what is coming rather than only what is next. One owner, one morning brief. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
