## What it does

For people running tracked vehicles on OneStepGPS, it answers the questions you would otherwise dig through the app for and carries out the changes you ask for — but only after you have confirmed that specific change. Knowing the product inside out turns a lookup into one message instead of a session. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
