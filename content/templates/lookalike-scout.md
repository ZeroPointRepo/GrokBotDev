## What it does

Start from a single account that fits your market and it fans out to businesses of the same shape, then works out who inside each one would actually sign off on a purchase and confirms those contacts. The roster keeps refreshing rather than sitting there as an export that goes stale in a week. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
