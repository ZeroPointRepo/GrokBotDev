## What it does

Answer a short survey about where you stand on the issues and it builds a walkthrough of your actual ballot: every race and measure, explained plainly. Alongside each one it notes where your stated views line up with a choice and where they clash, so you can see the full picture without being told how to vote. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
