## What it does

Paste a customer email, text or public review and it drafts a reply a local service business can send as-is. It is barred from inventing prices, dates or promises, so a draft reflects only what you have told it. You review and send everything; nothing goes out on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @lonzom10 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
