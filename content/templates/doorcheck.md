## What it does

Hand it a template you are about to add and it reads the recipe — instructions, requested skills, plugin asks — as material to weigh, not orders to obey. The verdict comes back as block, allow with limits, or clear. Green means no significant install-time findings, which is narrower than calling it safe. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @the_Arow_H shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
