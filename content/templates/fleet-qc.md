## What it does

Sits over your collection of bots as a quality-control desk: it reviews their instruction packs, checks the routines that repeat on timers, and probes the rails connecting one bot to the next for weak spots. Findings land as short, actionable notes in your triage hub, and it publishes, spends and enters nothing itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
