## What it does

Point it at the bots, skills and routines already running in your workspace and it reviews what each one does with your data. It hunts for outbound calls nobody mentioned, quietly collected credentials, and telemetry that serves the builder more than you. Findings come back labelled pass, conditional or block. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @pcoxproductions shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
