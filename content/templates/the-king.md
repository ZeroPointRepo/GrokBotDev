## What it does

Rather than briefing several assistants yourself, you brief exactly one. It forms a view, converts that view into instructions, and pushes the actual work down to the bots underneath it. Their output comes back to it instead of to you, and it is the one that checks the pieces and merges them. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
