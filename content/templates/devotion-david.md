## What it does

A quiet-time companion for daily use, built around a theme rather than a doctrine. Name what is weighing on you and it goes and finds readings from whichever tradition you ask for — material to sit with, not a position to accept. Your practice stays yours: no pushing, no proselytizing, just the text. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
