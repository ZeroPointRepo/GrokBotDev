## What it does

For anyone following several OpenSea drops and losing track of who qualifies for what. Once a day it lists the phases currently open, lines them up against the wallets you name, and adds a quick sense of how loud the drop is on X. It only reads and reports: no signing, no minting, no trading on your behalf. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
