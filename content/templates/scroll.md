## What it does

It reads your Following timeline in chronological order, deliberately skipping the algorithmic feed, and sends a short weekday rundown of the posts worth your attention: who wrote each, why it matters, and the link. An hour of scrolling becomes a two-minute read. Read-only, and it never engages for you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
