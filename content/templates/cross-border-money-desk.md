## What it does

A client abroad paid you and the total arrived short — drop the invoice or the payout note here for a patient walkthrough: which transfer network carried the money, where fees nibble at it, and where the balance likely sits now. Educational by design — it explains payment flows and never touches funds or accounts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
