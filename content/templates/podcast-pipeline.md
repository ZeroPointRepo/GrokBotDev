## What it does

Covers the whole production line for an audio programme: it researches the topic, writes a script with a distinct voice for each host, stitches the speech with your branding and an outro, checks the result, then cuts captions and publishes. Built around a beginner-level Spanish show, it handles two-host formats. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
