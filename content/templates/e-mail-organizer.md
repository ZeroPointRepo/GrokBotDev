## What it does

A Dutch-language housekeeper for one Gmail account. Mail is labelled by the vendor that sent it, the noise is swept out of sight, and the /Payments label is kept for genuine PDF invoices. A daily pass re-reads the inbox and moves only what it can identify as a real invoice, leaving ordinary correspondence alone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
