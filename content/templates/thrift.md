## What it does

Run enough bots and the running cost stops being trivial. This one audits the collection you have accumulated, cuts over-long instruction profiles back to the parts actually doing work, and points out the half-built ones sitting idle. Anything on a live timer is left untouched until you approve the change. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @kleosrr shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
