## What it does

A migration tool between assistant platforms. It reads one of your Grok bots and rebuilds the equivalent setup as a ChatGPT Dot, producing a kit you can paste in yourself. If you are signed in on ChatGPT desktop it can apply the clone there too, though the paste kit works on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @monomyth shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
