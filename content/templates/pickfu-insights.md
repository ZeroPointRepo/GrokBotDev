## What it does

Describe the choice you are stuck on — a name, a cover image, a price, a feature — and this bot writes a consumer poll around it, puts that in front of a paid PickFu panel, and reads the answers back as a recommendation. The idea is to spend a little on evidence instead of months on a guess. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
