## What it does

Sits above a collection of Grok Bots the way a chief of staff sits above a team. Every incoming job goes to the specialist best placed to do it, with a defined deliverable and a clear stopping point attached. Whatever genuinely needs a human decision lands in one queue where you approve, edit or skip it. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
