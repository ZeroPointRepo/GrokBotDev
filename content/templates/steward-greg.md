## What it does

The coordinator you speak to first when your setup is a team rather than one bot. Contact and calendar requests go to whichever teammate owns them, habits are tracked, and anything touching sensitive material comes back to you for permission first. It also produces audio files on request. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
