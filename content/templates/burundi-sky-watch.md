## What it does

A live view of the skies over Burundi. Ask what is up there and it answers with the aircraft over the country at that moment, naming the airline, the model, and the origin and destination of each one. Everything is drawn from free public flight-tracking feeds, so it is a readout rather than radar. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
