## What it does

Give it a subject and the age group you are writing for, and it drafts a complete picture book from beginning to end. It then assembles a brief for a partner illustrator bot setting out what each page should show, and keeps the working draft saved so you can come back later and revise it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
