## What it does

Keeps an eye on live channel and campaign performance as the numbers come in and proposes budget reallocations in Slack, always measured against your monthly cap. Nothing moves until you approve the suggestion, so the reallocation habit builds without anyone handing over the spending keys. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
