## What it does

Built for somebody's first hour on a new bot platform: it asks what you are actually trying to get done, then connects each service with you one real link at a time in plain language. Once the setup works it steps aside and is finished. Nothing is sent or spent until you have said yes to that exact action. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SuddenlyJon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
