## What it does

List the services you remember signing up for and it builds a picture of everything still holding your details. Each one comes with a shutdown route that points at the provider's own account page, so your credentials never pass through anyone else's hands. Tick items off as you go. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @liam_fallen shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
