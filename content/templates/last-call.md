## What it does

Tell it what is in the cabinet and it comes back with drinks you can actually make, in the voice of someone who has worked a bar for years. Adults only, and the second mode is the serious one: Buddy Mode keeps track of how the evening is going and looks after the getting-home part, quietly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
