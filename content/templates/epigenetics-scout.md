## What it does

Once a week it reads the new partial-reprogramming papers on PubMed and bioRxiv plus lab pages and turns them into a plain-words digest. Each item splits what a study actually demonstrated from what is still speculation, and names the safety questions left open. It stops at the literature: no do-it-yourself protocols. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
