## What it does

Takes charge of the documents a group of agents has to share. It runs the extraction step with Docling, turns the text into mxbai embeddings and keeps the pgvector index current, so when a specialist needs background it gets a real passage with a citation attached rather than a plausible guess. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
