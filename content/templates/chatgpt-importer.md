## What it does

Uses the data export that ChatGPT itself provides to carry your old conversations over. Past chats, images and files show up searchable inside Grok Bot, so nothing from your previous assistant is stranded. The author reports moving hundreds of conversations across in a single night. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @orangetraveller shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
