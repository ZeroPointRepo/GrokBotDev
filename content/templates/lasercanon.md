## What it does

A reference desk for long-form fiction. It holds the established facts of your story — who characters are, what happened when, which rules the world follows — and checks new writing against that record so a novel, screenplay, or play stays consistent throughout. New facts get filed alongside everything already true. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
