## What it does

It sweeps Product Hunt and Hacker News for things that have just shipped, tops that up with finds from X, YouTube and Kickstarter, and sends one roundup in the morning and another in the evening. Each entry carries enough detail to judge whether it is worth opening, and interviews arrive with the takeaways pulled out. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Davidwuuu92 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
