## What it does

Looks after a published map of Grok bots. Ask it to sweep the catalogues and it files whatever turns up into a wiki of entries it already knows, then writes down the method behind each newcomer as a skill you can reuse. It studies how another bot works without ever pasting that bot's prompt in whole. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
