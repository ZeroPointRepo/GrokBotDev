## What it does

Solo operators get pulled apart by requests arriving from five directions at once. Paste them here - leads, jobs, favors, whatever came in - and you get one sorted queue, ranked, with a single sensible next step attached to every item. The point is not elaborate workflow; it is knowing what to touch first.
