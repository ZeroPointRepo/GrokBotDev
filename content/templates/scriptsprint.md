## What it does

Pulls transcripts from sources like Otter, whether voice memos, meetings or rants, and distills each into its speakers, a clean summary and the action items hiding inside. The output lands on a companion note in an Obsidian-style vault, with the original transcript left exactly as it was. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @the_simonjester shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
