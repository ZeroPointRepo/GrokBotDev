## What it does

A research assistant for X that stays on the reading side only: it maps who talks to whom inside a circle and how a topic develops over time. It never publishes, likes, follows or messages anyone, so the account it watches is never touched. Setup means your own SocialData key plus a spend limit agreed on the first run. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GrokBotRadar shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
