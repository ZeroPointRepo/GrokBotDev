## What it does

Reads a dossier on one local business and writes up where it is exposed across standard search, answer engines and map listings. The output stays in draft. It does not email anyone, pitch anyone, or hunt for fresh prospects — the work ends at the written brief on the desk. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
