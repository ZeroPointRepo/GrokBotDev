## What it does

Each weekday it compiles a short brief on every small online operation you run: traffic, sales, whatever is blocking progress, and the next move worth making. Before a launch it walks a pre-flight checklist with you, so nothing ships half-configured. It reads and reports; decisions stay yours. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @CorlettScott shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
