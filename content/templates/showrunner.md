## What it does

The lead role in a five-part setup for making music videos. It settles the look, produces the reference frames, and spells out what every shot needs from Grok Imagine — feed those in yourself or let it render them — after which the finished clips are cut together against the track. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
