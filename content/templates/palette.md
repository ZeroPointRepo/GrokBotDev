## What it does

Feed it a picture and it reads the colours back out as a working scheme, split into four jobs: a dominant shade, two supporting tones and one accent. The split is drawn as a proportion bar at 55/20/20/5, and every band carries the hex value lifted from the shot, ready to paste into a design file. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
