## What it does

Aimed at anyone whose Monday-to-Friday is really a queue of appointments. Early each morning it lays the day's schedule out in a single read, and the evening before it gathers background on whoever you are meeting from outside the company. Everything lands as a draft; nothing irreversible happens without your say-so. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
