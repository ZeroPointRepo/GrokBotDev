## What it does

Feed it a single idea, or a chore you keep repeating every day, and it drafts the entire bot around it: the concept, a quick market scan, the persona, the skills, the routines and even a test plan. It will write candidate launch posts too, so the announcement is not a blank page. The publish button stays in your hand. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
