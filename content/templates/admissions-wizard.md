## What it does

It works like a counselor who knows your file: interview practice in a realistic mode, mining your history for essay material and recommender briefs, and a tracker per application you switch on when one goes live. The aim is fewer generic essays and fewer missed deadlines across a whole application season. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
