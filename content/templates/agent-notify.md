## What it does

A notification sidekick for people running agents and automations: it sends an email only when an event genuinely needs a human, instead of streaming every status line at you. The mail flow is one-way out, so nothing in your mailbox is ever read. Setup is a one-click affair on Cloudflare, costing minutes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
