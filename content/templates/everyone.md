## What it does

Keeps a quiet, personal record of the people around you: their story, the way you first met, and the small details that slip between conversations. Built for recollection rather than selling, so there are no pipelines or deals here, just a place to remember someone properly before you next meet. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
