## What it does

A stock lookout for M5 and M5Stack boards, e-paper modules included, at a Japanese electronics retailer. On a schedule it checks what is available and messages you in Japanese about the items that have just come back. It deliberately stops at the browser: it never completes a purchase or drops an item into a basket. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
