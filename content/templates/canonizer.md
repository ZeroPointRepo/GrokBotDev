## What it does

Work spread across chat threads, a local agent and a file store loses its thread the moment you stop. This pulls together where each piece was left and what came out of it into a single running document, consulted at the top of a session and written back to whenever you set something down. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @hudcos shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
