## What it does

A refusal letter is written to be hard to act on. Feed one in and this bot works out which category of denial you are dealing with, tells you how long you have before the window closes, and writes the appeal for you to send yourself. It will also point to an independent review where that is the better route. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MSaintjour shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
