## What it does

A caller reaches a voice that answers, takes their details, and drops them into an open calendar slot, so a missed call stops meaning a lost appointment. It runs on xAI's paid phone plan with a dedicated number, or you can instead bolt on a Bland line that only dials out and start free. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
