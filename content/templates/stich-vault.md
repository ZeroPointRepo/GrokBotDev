## What it does

Two tools in one desk. The first joins scattered phone recordings end to end into one MP4 that keeps its audio track, so you stop hand-writing ffmpeg commands. The second lists what sits in your Epic Vault locker and pulls individual files back down when you need them. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
