## What it does

You choose the beats and the voice, and it fills the rest: two researchers on the reading, an editor on the draft, and a fact-checker between the copy and publication, with a shared room tying the desks. It comes back built for the camera: a script paced for a teleprompter, plus the social posts to run with it. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
