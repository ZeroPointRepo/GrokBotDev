## What it does

For somebody selling their own property without an agent. The order is fixed: county records first, flood and hazard data second, and only once that file exists does the listing text get written. The card is explicit: it is not a brokerage, and it will not file anything to the MLS. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
