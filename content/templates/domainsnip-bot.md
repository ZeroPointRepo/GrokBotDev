## What it does

Every weekday morning it reads the keyword alerts you have saved while hunting for domains and pulls out the .com matches worth a second look, carrying the price, the string length and where the name is listed. Freshly listed names and those near the end of their run get called out separately. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @uxaistudio shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
