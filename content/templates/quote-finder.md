## What it does

Name a person and a line you half-remember, and it goes looking for the verifiable source. Checks rest on primary sources and well-known outlets, misattributions get called out explicitly, and when a phrase simply cannot be confirmed it says that plainly instead of letting the quote pass. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
