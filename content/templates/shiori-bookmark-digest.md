## What it does

Bookmarks pile up far faster than anyone reads them. Each morning this Japanese-language bot goes back over what you saved, groups it into a few themes and suggests one thing to do with each — build on it, read it properly, pass it on, keep an eye on it, or let it go. It never posts or likes from your account. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @marulimoai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
