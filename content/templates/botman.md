## What it does

A detective-themed guard for your social accounts. It reviews new followers and message requests, flags likely scams, bot accounts and impersonators, and writes a short case file for each suspect. It also summarizes publicly reported local crime activity, and carries a seasonal Halloween mode for parents. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
