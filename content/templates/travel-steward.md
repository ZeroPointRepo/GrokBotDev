## What it does

Once a trip is booked, it runs a daily pass that turns the plan into practical checks — what to confirm, what to prepare, what usually gets missed. On travel days it stays close, and along the way it suggests where to eat well or find an airport lounge. One steward per traveler, not a feed of alerts. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
