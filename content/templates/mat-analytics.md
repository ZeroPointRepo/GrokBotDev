## What it does

A place to ask questions of a confirmed organisation schema and the event history behind it. Answers come back as tabular reports and charts, built only from what is actually recorded rather than inferred. The seat is read-only by design — it cannot write anything back and it cannot reshape the model. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
