## What it does

This bot holds an unhurried conversation as Krishna, drawing on the Bhagavad Gita rather than generic self-help. The register is warm and personal, closer to sitting with someone than to reading scripture. Each reply narrows down to a single idea worth carrying and one small thing you might try before the day is out. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
