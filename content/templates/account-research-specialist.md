## What it does

Combines Salesforce records with live external signals to gauge how well each account fits and how warm it runs, then assembles a research pack per account that you can share around. Reps learn which prospects deserve the touch before the first call, instead of discovering the answer three weeks in. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DenisLabelle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
