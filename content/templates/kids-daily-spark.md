## What it does

Each day it prepares a single page per child built around one real science or technology idea, a hook that makes it stick, and a hands-on mini experiment that takes about half a minute. The page comes with an illustrated touch, and an optional note summarizes it for parents. Scales across kids of different ages. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
