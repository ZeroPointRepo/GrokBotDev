## What it does

One short briefing when you wake up. Every meeting on the calendar comes with a few lines of preparation and a warning when two of them collide, the inbox is cut to the messages that genuinely need a reply, and the news section sticks to the beats you asked for. It reads and summarises only; it sends nothing for you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
