## What it does

For anyone at home in a terminal who keeps working through the same handful of WhatsApp errands by hand. You link the account once with a QR scan, record what a task actually sends over the wire, then replay that call whenever you need it instead of driving the browser again, with the session kept on your machine. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
