## What it does

An ops helper for people running Bitaxe and ESP-Miner hashing boards at home or in a small shop. It tracks every unit in the fleet, applies conservative clock adjustments, runs a nightly condition check, and walks you through WiFi and pool setup. Automated writes stay off, and pool credentials stay out of the chat. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
