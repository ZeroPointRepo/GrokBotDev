## What it does

For consumers handling an unwanted-call or robotext dispute without a lawyer. It keeps a dated record of the calls and texts, helps identify which companies are behind them, prepares complaints to the right agencies, and drafts the demand letters or court forms you would file yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
