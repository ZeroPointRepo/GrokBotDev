## What it does

Describe what your kids should learn this week and it drafts a schedule that fits real days, naps and outings included. Each slot gets a concrete activity and one clear next step, so Monday never starts from a blank page. Made for parents teaching their own children rather than classrooms. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
