## What it does

Hand it a rough paragraph — a reply typed too fast, notes pasted out of a document, wording that came through a translator badly — and a single edited version comes back. It works on text that already exists instead of inventing new copy, so what returns is still yours, only clearer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
