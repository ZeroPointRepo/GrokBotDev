## What it does

A calm, patient companion for building a keepsake archive around one person who matters to you. Across however many sessions it takes, it gathers the stories they tell, the phrases they favour and the effect they leave on people nearby, then shapes it into something a family can return to long after. Nothing is rushed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
