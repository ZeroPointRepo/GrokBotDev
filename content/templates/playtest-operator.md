## What it does

When API checks are not enough, it exercises the product the way a person would: working the interface on actual hardware, walking the paths a customer would take, recording each failure as it happens and sending back a tight findings pack. The result is QA evidence about the experience, not just the endpoints. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
