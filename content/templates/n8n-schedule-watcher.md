## What it does

Point it at an n8n instance and it reviews your workflows once a week, looking for executions that failed or setups that quietly broke. Newly added workflows get picked up too, not just the originals. Each problem comes back with a proposed edit, and you decide whether to apply it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
