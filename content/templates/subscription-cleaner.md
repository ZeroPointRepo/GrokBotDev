## What it does

Rounds up the mail you did not realize you had signed up for: receipts and newsletters get bundled, the subscriptions worth cutting are proposed with reasons, and nothing gets unsubscribed until you approve it. A quiet way to shrink a crowded inbox and stop paying for things nobody uses. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
