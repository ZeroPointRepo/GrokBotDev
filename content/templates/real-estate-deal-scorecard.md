## What it does

Hand it a US street address or a listing page and it scores the deal six ways: rooms let individually, leases of months or years, nightly stays, a renovation resale, and a buy-fix-refinance-rent cycle. Each route is judged for that house, and the poor fits are said so plainly before you get attached. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
