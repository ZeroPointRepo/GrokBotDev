## What it does

A development partner that hands coding jobs to a set of automated build labs instead of grinding through them in one thread. Whatever is in flight gets mirrored onto a Linear board, so the issue tracker stays the one place you look to see status. After a change ships it keeps watching how the live product behaves. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
