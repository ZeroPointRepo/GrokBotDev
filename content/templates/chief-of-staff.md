## What it does

A morning desk that sits at the top of the list. Each weekday opens with a short brief on what matters now, and from there it delegates — individual pieces go out to the specialist bots working underneath it. Mail is drafted rather than sent, and no handoff clears until you say it can. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
