## What it does

Reads one public website the way an AI crawler would and hands over what to fix: an llms.txt draft, additions for robots.txt, notes on schema, and a patch list of five items. Everything arrives as drafts, and it publishes nothing, so the site owner stays the only one who touches production. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
