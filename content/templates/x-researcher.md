## What it does

A general-purpose desk for working the AI-coding conversation on X. It researches a topic across threads before you post, drafts posts and replies in your register, and keeps a shortlist of recent posts worth studying for the patterns that got them shared. One place for the whole loop: read, write, learn what lands. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
