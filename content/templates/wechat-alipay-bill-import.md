## What it does

Chinese payment apps export a month of activity as a spreadsheet, and plenty of people only have a screenshot. This one reads either, sorts each line into a category, drops the duplicates that come from overlapping exports, and shows you the result before a single row is saved. It never asks for a password. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
