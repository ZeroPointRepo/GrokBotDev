## What it does

It surveys how exposed your household's information is: email addresses found in known breaches, profiles on people-search sites, and entries held by data brokers. Results come back ranked, highest risk first, each with practical next steps. The audit is read-only, runs with your consent, and never asks for a password. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
