## What it does

Some documents cannot reasonably be pasted into a general assistant, so the analysis simply never gets done at all. This carries out the work inside a sealed hardware enclave instead: the material is encrypted on your own side, and the environment proves what it is running before a result is accepted. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
