## What it does

Hand it a link to a post, a whole thread or a screenshot and it answers one question straight: is this idea worth more of your time, or should it die here. The evaluation is deliberately blunt rather than encouraging, because polite feedback already surrounds you. It judges and says so; it does not rewrite. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @TNVOLMAN shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
