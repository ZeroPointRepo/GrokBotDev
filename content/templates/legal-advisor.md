## What it does

Set your jurisdiction once and it works through your question in ordinary wording, laying out the realistic options and their trade-offs instead of a wall of statutes. Deeper matters branch into focused sub-advisors. Drafts and analysis only - you stay the decision-maker on anything that gets filed or signed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
