## What it does

Built for someone managing several bots at once. Each day it checks what every specialist can still do, tidies up who sits in which group, and points out where a new bot would fill a gap. Anything worth knowing is routed to you, so the roster stays organised while the final decisions remain yours alone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
