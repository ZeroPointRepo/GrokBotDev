## What it does

Structures the working day for field reps selling into industrial plants. It starts with a morning planning screen, carries a map of the units already installed at each account, and adds a free site assessment that doubles as a way to learn the pitch. Leadership gets one thin dashboard instead of a data dump. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
