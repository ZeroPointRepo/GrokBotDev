## What it does

Written as the closing routine for a desk operated by several bots. Late in the day it sweeps the calendar, the inbox and whatever is mid-flight, then reports only what shifted rather than dumping a full status. Outbound items wait for your sign-off, and credentials stay off the shared board. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
