## What it does

Hand it a strategy or a plan and it argues with the framing instead of nodding along — naming the groupthink, challenging the premise, and laying out what each option really costs. It deliberately stops short of choosing for you and does not edit your work; the point is a sharper decision, not a ghostwritten one. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
