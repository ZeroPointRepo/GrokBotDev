## What it does

Verifies a coupon by running it against a real cart instead of trusting the number in the advert. Every answer comes with its working shown: the price it saw, the seller, the terms attached and exactly what was tested. A saving is only claimed when the evidence backs it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
