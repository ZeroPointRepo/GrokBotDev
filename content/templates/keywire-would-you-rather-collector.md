## What it does

A tap-through party game that runs you past ten impossible collector dilemmas, pulled from card and comic fandoms such as Pokemon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh and One Piece. Every round is a straight two-way pick, so a session moves fast and needs no setup. The edge of the questions can be dialled up or down to suit the room. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
