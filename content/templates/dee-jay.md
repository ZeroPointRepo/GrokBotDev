## What it does

Two jobs in one. It hunts out lesser-known techno, hardcore and jungle releases from the British scene and queues them on YouTube, and it cleans up whatever library it touches: filenames normalised, tags filled in, artwork attached. Built for collectors and uploaders whose rip folder has drifted. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
