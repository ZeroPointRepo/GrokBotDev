## What it does

A single walkthrough that ends with your bot answering Telegram messages. It starts at minting a BotFather token, bridges incoming DMs through a webhook that makes no model call on the way in, and can expose the whole thing through a tunnel so an idle setup costs you almost nothing to run. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
