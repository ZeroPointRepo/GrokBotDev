## What it does

Each day it sweeps the previous day's AI, technology and policy developments for the few items worth your attention, then writes them up as a script in neutral wire style. That draft moves through voiceover and a two-step review before anything is cleared to publish. A small newsroom in one bot. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
