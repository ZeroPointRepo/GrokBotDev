## What it does

A shared browser session collects debris fast. This bot takes stock of everything currently open, proposes a cleanup without touching a thing on the first pass, respects a list of tabs the owner has marked untouchable, and shuts only what is plainly finished with. Then it reports what it did. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
