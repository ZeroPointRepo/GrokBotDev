## What it does

Built around the reality of running a channel. It bundles YouTube-comment reports summarised in Italian and English and read through research, advisory and resolution angles, with a daily pass over an Outlook inbox for junk. Nothing is removed without asking, so the sweep stays reviewable. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
