## What it does

Pick a topic and it runs the whole explainer pipeline for one video at a time: research, script, voice track, hand-drawn-style animation, edit, and a QA pass, ending with an unlisted YouTube upload for you to review. It spins up helper bots so several stages run side by side instead of in a queue. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
