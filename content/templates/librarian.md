## What it does

Point a camera at the books you already own and it identifies each spine, then assembles the whole collection into a private site you can browse. Along the way it lifts passages worth keeping, notes where two authors take opposing positions, and points you toward the titles to read next. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ShaneMac shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
