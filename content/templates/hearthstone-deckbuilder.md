## What it does

Point it at footage of your own Hearthstone games and it weighs the cards, the format and the list you brought for strengths and gaps. Expect an argument about the current meta, including a case against the pet card you refuse to cut. A budget build comes with it for players who would rather not craft the latest set. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Wardonis shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
