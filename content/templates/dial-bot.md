## What it does

Wires chat up to a voice-calling service, so asking it to ring a number and put a question to whoever answers actually dials. Once the call is over you get a written account of what happened. The first run collects the key and the voice it should use, and after that it simply works. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
