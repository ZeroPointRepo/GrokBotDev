## What it does

Watches your symbols for momentum-cross formations and drafts a full ticket for each one, rather than acting. Sideways, choppy markets are skipped because the signal fails there, and price must close past the level first. Each ticket needs 1.5-to-1 reward-to-risk, and no order is ever sent anywhere.
