## What it does

You tell it a goal you care about, then throw everyday decisions at it, large or small. Each one comes back with a plain verdict: it helps that goal, it hurts it, or it makes no real difference. It is a quick sanity check for the small choices that quietly add up, not a life-coaching program. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
