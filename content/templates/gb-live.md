## What it does

Watches a live broadcast on X and captures a screenshot whenever the picture changes, with a short Japanese note on what just happened. The notes build into an hourly digest that only arrives if something moved, so a quiet stream stays quiet. Nothing is posted publicly; the owner sees it first. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
