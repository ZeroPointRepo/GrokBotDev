## What it does

Handles the swag pipeline from first message to dispatch: prospect outreach goes out, the redemption form is watched, each approve-or-reject call arrives as a chat ping, and the box ships once you sign off. Sending merch to the right people stops being a spreadsheet and a pile of screenshots. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
