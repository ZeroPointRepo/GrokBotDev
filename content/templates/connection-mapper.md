## What it does

Name two people, two companies, or a person and an organization, and choose which kinds of ties matter to you, such as employment, investment or membership. It then maps the direct links between them plus every friend-of-a-friend route one step removed, working from what is publicly findable. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
