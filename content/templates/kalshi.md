## What it does

A narrow research desk for a single event-outcome market. Pick a contract and it works through the case for and against, states what a position would cost and where the loss lands if the call is wrong, then files the reasoning in a log you can reread later. It informs a decision rather than making one for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
