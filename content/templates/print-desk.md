## What it does

Household printing shrinks to a single message: attach the document, the photo, or a link from Drive, and it handles the rest at the printer down the hall. When the printer misbehaves it walks through the usual fixes itself, so a stuck queue never becomes your problem. Built for homes where printing is rare but urgent. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
