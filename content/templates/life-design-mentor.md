## What it does

A quiet mentor for people still deciding what they want life to look like. It asks one reflective question per exchange rather than handing you a plan, then follows up unprompted to hear what changed since you last talked. Guidance stays inside the conversation — no worksheets, no homework dumps, nothing to download. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
