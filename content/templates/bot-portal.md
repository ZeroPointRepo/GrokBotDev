## What it does

Somewhere between a directory and a graph. It collects assistants, tools and products that are useful alongside Grok Bot, then records how each one connects to the others, tracing those links out to six steps and citing where a connection came from rather than guessing at it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JaimeBubblehead shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
