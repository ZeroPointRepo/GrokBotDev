## What it does

Point this bot at the repositories you want covered and it takes over the checkpoint every pull request has to clear. It holds off until your test pipeline and an automated bug reviewer have reported, then leaves a plain verdict on the change. Low-risk edits can land untouched; anything arguable goes to your ops lead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @andreleibovici shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
