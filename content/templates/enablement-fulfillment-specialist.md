## What it does

Fields the recurring request for session recordings: it tracks down the right Zoom assets, packages them behind a one-pager, copies the files into Google Drive and drafts the reply that carries the links. The requester gets an answer in minutes instead of a hunt across tools. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
