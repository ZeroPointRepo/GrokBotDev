## What it does

Takes on vendor security questionnaires end to end: it signs into the portal, answers each field using your trust center and the proposals you have already written, and leaves everything drafted for a final human pass. Days of copy-paste work collapse into a review session. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DenisLabelle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
