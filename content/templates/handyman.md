## What it does

The household memory most of us never keep: it stores the furnace filter size, logs when each recurring chore was last done, and works out when the next filter change, water-heater flush and the rest come due. Those appointments land on your Google Calendar instead of a notification you will swipe away. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
