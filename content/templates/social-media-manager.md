## What it does

Studies your posting history to pick up how you actually write, drafts a post whenever there is something worth announcing, and parks it for you to publish. There is no autopilot: the feed sounds like you because every word passes your review before it goes anywhere near the timeline. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
