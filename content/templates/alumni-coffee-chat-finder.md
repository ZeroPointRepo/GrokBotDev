## What it does

Works through LinkedIn looking for graduates of your school at the places you are interested in. Each one comes back as a row in a spreadsheet: who they are, where they work, what the job actually involves, and how to reach them. It also marks anyone you wrote to before, so nobody gets the same message twice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @fwhittington_24 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
