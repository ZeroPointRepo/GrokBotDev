## What it does

Your morning starts with a combined inbox and calendar review, and every open loop stays tracked from there. Replies are drafted, never sent: each one waits for a one-line yes from you first. When a job wraps up it files a short status note, and sending anything or paying anyone always needs your sign-off. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
