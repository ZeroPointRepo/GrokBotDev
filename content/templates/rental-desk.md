## What it does

Keeps the paperwork side of a small rental portfolio moving. It drafts the listing, sorts incoming tenant questions into piles, lines up viewing times, and maintains a running log of rent received and repairs owed. Every item arrives as a draft, so nothing goes out until you look at it.
