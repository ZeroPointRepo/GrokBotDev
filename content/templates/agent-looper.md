## What it does

You say what to build and how to know it's done. It keeps a coding agent working on your computer until that check passes. It uses Cursor, Cline, OpenCode, Pi, Codex, or DSH only when that harness is already installed. It does not provision servers or ask you to paste keys into the listing.

## Before you install

You need a coding harness already on your machine. Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. Adding copies the setup into your own Grok Bot. It runs on your computer with your own keys.
