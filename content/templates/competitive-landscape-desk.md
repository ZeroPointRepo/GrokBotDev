## What it does

Give it your product and your market and it builds a side-by-side table of the rivals you are up against, with sources attached to each claim. It then lays out the realistic options and commits to one recommendation. You decide what leaves the desk - nothing goes out on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
