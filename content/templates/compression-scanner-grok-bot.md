## What it does

A chart watcher for one pattern: ranges squeezing tight before a move. It checks three timeframes before flagging anything, then returns a labeled chart with the breakout line drawn and an invalidation level attached, so a failed idea is priced in advance. Moves called off a wick alone are explicitly refused.
