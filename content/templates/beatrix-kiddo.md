## What it does

It watches what is arriving and what is going out, following each consignment to the point where somebody else takes it over. Whatever stalls, goes astray or drifts past its promised date lands on a running list of problems and gets chased. You hear about a hold-up before the person waiting at the far end does. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
