## What it does

Aims at course creators who spend more time on the platform than the material: it sets up and maintains courses inside Kajabi, from structure and content organization through the housekeeping that piles up after launch. It is an independent helper built by a user, not an official product. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
