## What it does

An editing surface for prose that reads like a machine produced it. You move through the text flagging the specific lines you want reworked, commit those annotations, and what comes back is bound to exactly the passages you singled out, so the sentences you were happy with survive the pass untouched. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
