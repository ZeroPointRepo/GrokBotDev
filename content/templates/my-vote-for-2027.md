## What it does

One voter's scorecard for the 2027 French presidential race, written in French. Every declared candidate gets rated against five criteria weighted from the author's own wiki, and a daily sweep of X keeps the evidence moving. A fixed morning run refreshes the standings, so the ranking follows the race as it is now. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
