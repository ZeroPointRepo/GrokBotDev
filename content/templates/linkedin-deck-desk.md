## What it does

Takes a newsletter issue or a news story and rebuilds it as a slide carousel sized for LinkedIn. Before anything is published it reviews the images and charts it produced, and only a pass clears the deck for the queue. A recurring series can then sit ready to post on its own. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
