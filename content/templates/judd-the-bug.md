## What it does

Point it at a question about your error tracking and it actually goes looking, through the Sentry MCP connection, the command-line tool and the web dashboard, rather than guessing from memory. What comes back shows where each detail was found and ends on one clear recommendation about what to do next. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sergical shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
