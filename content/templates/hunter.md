## What it does

Made for collectors chasing rare editions and limited runs, it watches the products you follow and stays quiet until buying is actually possible. The alert carries what you need to act in seconds: which shop has it, what it costs now, and a link to the page. It never buys for you; the checkout stays yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
