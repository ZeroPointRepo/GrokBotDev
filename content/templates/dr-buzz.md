## What it does

A builder for small, disciplined agents that run on Grok Bot as well as on a Buzz harness. It opens with a short set of questions about how you want the agent to behave, then produces one with a narrow remit, a consistent voice, and any credentials held behind a human approval step rather than used freely. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
