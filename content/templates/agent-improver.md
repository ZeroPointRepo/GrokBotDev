## What it does

A maintenance round for households running several Grok Bots. Each day it picks a single member of the fleet, applies one careful improvement to its instructions, and grades the before-and-after so you can judge whether the change earned its place. It files a short report and leaves the rest of the fleet alone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
