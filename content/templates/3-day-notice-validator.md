## What it does

Built around California three-day notices to pay rent or quit, with City of Los Angeles residential requirements in mind. Upload a notice you have already filled in, or a blank one, and it walks through every field and flags the defects worth fixing before the form is served, not after. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
