## What it does

Paste in the link to a newsletter issue and it pulls out the parts worth promoting, which you approve before anything is designed. Those highlights become social graphics and carousel slides through a branded template, either yours or one of Contentdrips' own. Output arrives sized for posting, not rebuilding. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
