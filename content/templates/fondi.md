## What it does

Aimed at people building alone. It works through whatever your company already publishes about itself, forms a picture of the business, then creates specialist assistants to match, standing in for the heads of function a one-person outfit cannot afford to hire. The bench is shaped around your company, not generic. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
