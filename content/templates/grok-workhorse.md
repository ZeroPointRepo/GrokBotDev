## What it does

Breaks a coding job into bounded tasks and hands each one to a sandboxed coding agent running on its own machine, with Kilo and OpenCode as the supported workers. It then reads the diffs and the test results before anything reaches you, so what lands in your lap has already survived a review pass. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
