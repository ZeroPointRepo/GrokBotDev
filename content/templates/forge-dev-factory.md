## What it does

The work it picks up is whatever you have marked as settled, so nothing starts on a brief that is still being argued over. From there it drafts an approach, writes the code, reads its own output back critically and opens the change for review — unattended, through the small hours, waiting for you at your desk. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
