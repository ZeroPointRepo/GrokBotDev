## What it does

Puts a renewal file under every account roughly ninety days ahead of the date: usage numbers, open tickets, recorded calls and CRM history assembled into one picture, with the commercial note drafted and a ping as the date approaches. Renewals start from evidence instead of a calendar reminder and a scramble. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DenisLabelle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
