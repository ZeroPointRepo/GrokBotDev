## What it does

Point it at a Drive folder where scanned pages pile up, backlog included, and it works out what each document is before renaming and refiling it. Hashing stops the same page being stored twice, so once the copies are safe you can recycle the originals. For anyone whose scanner emits Untitled files. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
