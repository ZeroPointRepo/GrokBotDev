## What it does

Reads the bots, instruction sets and routines you have built up and turns the overlap into one document covering your profile, your fleet and your routines. It finishes with a diff of what it found, then stops and waits. Nothing changes until you approve the proposal, and it does not carry changes out itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @the_Arow_H shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
