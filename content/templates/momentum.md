## What it does

Keeps a group of cooperating bots from stalling. It watches each lane, spots the specialist that has gone quiet, and prompts that bot to pick its thread back up before the work piles up. Alongside each nudge it hands you a shortlist of next moves that cost nothing to make. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
