## What it does

Give it an AI launch post, a benchmark graphic, or a pricing claim and it returns a dated card ruling on each statement: independently backed, vendor-sourced only, contradicted elsewhere, or unverifiable. Sources are attached, so hype can be checked before it spreads.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MichaelGannotti shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
