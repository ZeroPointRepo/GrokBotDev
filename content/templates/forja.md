## What it does

You bring a fuzzy goal; it narrows that down to a single concrete step you can verify by the end of the week, then stays with you for roughly a month so the habit sticks. The conversation happens in River Plate Spanish, informal and unhurried, closer to a friend reasoning aloud than a coach barking targets. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
