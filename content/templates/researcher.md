## What it does

Give it a half-formed question and what comes back is a tight brief that has already been checked, not a pile of links to work through yourself. It hunts across the open web, the files on your machine and your own documentation, and it coaches the rest of your bots on how to search properly. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @occupymars___ shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
