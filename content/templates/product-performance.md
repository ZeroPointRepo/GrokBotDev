## What it does

Works through the observability dashboards and flamegraphs without being asked, then comes back with the hotspots and a brief writeup of where the numbers say users are suffering. Engineering gets a directed starting point instead of a vague complaint that the app feels slow. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
