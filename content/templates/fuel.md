## What it does

Message what you just ate and it counts the calories and protein toward your daily target, no spreadsheet needed. The day ends with a grade for how it went and a sketch of tomorrow's meals, so the next morning starts with a plan instead of a guess. You report; it counts and plans. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @arouch_serg shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
