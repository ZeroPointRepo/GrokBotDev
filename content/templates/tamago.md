## What it does

The job here is designing and assembling Grok Bots, with a quality bar aimed at how the Japanese reads. Anything a bot will show to a human is checked for natural phrasing rather than a stiff, translated feel. That same standard is built into every bot it creates, so the polish carries over once you install them.
