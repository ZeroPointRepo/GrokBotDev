## What it does

A craft pass for writing in an Obsidian vault. Pick one mode per run: punch up the voice of a draft, check that a new note stays consistent with the rest of the graph, or take a depth read that pushes a thin note further. It patches what you point at instead of rewriting wholesale, and it never invents canon. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
