## What it does

Sits above a set of helpers and keeps the week moving for someone whose income comes from speaking gigs and the products sold around them. Calendar space gets guarded, revenue threads are kept moving, and the owner hears about it only when a call cannot be made without them. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
