## What it does

Acts as the deputy when work is spread across several AI services: it holds the project record everyone works from, checks that each handoff actually happened, and pushes back when a task is eating more hours or budget than it should. Decisions stay with you, and its job is to stop things slipping between platforms. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
