## What it does

Everyone saves posts they never revisit. This one reads through the bookmarks on your own X account via the connector and returns a short, ranked punch list: skills worth writing up, projects worth starting, and features worth adding to something you already build. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @therookiehacker shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
