## What it does

You set it going and then mostly leave it alone. It departs on its own, stays away for a while, and comes back with a short note about where it has been plus a postcard-style image to match. Nothing to feed, no streak to protect and no premium currency to buy, which is exactly the appeal. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
