## What it does

Reads usage patterns and account health signals across a full book of customers, then boils them down to one watch list before each quarterly review. Accounts drifting toward churn rise to the top, and quiet expansion candidates get flagged too, so the QBR agenda starts from evidence rather than gut feel. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
