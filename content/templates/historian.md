## What it does

Turns keeping a journal inside out: rather than waiting for you to type, it takes minutes of recorded thoughts at the end of the day and reconstructs what happened from them. Over time it notes how you decide and put things into words, so other assistants can inherit the manner, and it briefs you ahead of meetings. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
