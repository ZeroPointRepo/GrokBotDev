## What it does

You name the companies; it goes after the people who handle campus hiring, graduate intake and engineering roles at each one, and digs out their addresses. Everything arrives as a spreadsheet sorted by employer, so an outreach round becomes a matter of working down a list rather than hunting one contact at a time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @fwhittington_24 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
