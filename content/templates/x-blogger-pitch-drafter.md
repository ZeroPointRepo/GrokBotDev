## What it does

Name the bloggers on X you want reading your piece and it first checks whether your article actually suits each one. It then writes a separate, genuine message per person asking them to take a look - no template, no pressure to reshare. Sending stays entirely in your hands. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
