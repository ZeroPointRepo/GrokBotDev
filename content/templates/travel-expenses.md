## What it does

Track what you spend on a trip as it happens, in whatever currency you are standing in. Type a line or photograph the receipt, and it keeps the running total. When the trip ends it rolls everything into one report with the total you choose and exports a tidy Excel file plus a PDF that fits A4 or Letter paper. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
