## What it does

Produces a complete track from a style note and a set of words, running the music model on your own Apple-silicon hardware instead of in somebody else's cloud, and returning a high-sample-rate audio file. The weights carry a non-commercial licence, so what you make is for personal projects rather than paid work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @monomyth shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
