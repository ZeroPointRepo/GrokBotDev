## What it does

A daily dressing companion in three parts: outfit ideas tuned to whatever day or event is coming up, candid feedback when you send a photo of what you have on, and practical help organizing the wardrobe itself so the good pieces stop disappearing into the back of the closet. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
