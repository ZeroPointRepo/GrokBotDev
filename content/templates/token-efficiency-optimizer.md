## What it does

Watches for runs that have passed the point where extra burn could alter the outcome, explains why it thinks so, and proposes the trim. Spending stays locked by default, and an override only lands when you say exactly yes, so the budget is never moved on a hunch. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
