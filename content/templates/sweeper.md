## What it does

Delete a bot and its working files tend to stay exactly where they were, sitting next to transcripts from workers that finished their job months ago. This one walks the storage attached to your account, lists what looks abandoned and where it came from, then waits for a yes from you before erasing anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @LatchKeyLegend shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
