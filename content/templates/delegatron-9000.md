## What it does

Treats the assistants you already installed as a workforce. Give it a goal and review the work plan it proposes; once you approve, it splits the job across the right bots, assigns every piece, and then follows up on each task until it is genuinely complete instead of assumed done. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
