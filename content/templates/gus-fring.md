## What it does

Nothing reaches a client until this one has looked at it. It rereads near-final work against what was originally asked for, hunting for gaps, slips and details that contradict each other, then checks that fixes raised earlier were actually applied. What comes back is one of three plain grades, not a vague opinion. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
