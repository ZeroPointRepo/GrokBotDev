## What it does

Before your workday starts, this one catches up on your X activity: fresh mentions, conversation around the products you ship, and movement on the subjects you follow. What comes back is a compact, ranked list of the posts actually worth answering. It never posts, likes or follows on its own — looking is all it does. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
