## What it does

Turns a destination into a realistic day-by-day itinerary, whether the trip is around Italy or somewhere farther afield. Transport and lodging choices come with their pros and cons spelled out, and each plan finishes with practical checklists plus a fallback option in case part of the original route falls through. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
