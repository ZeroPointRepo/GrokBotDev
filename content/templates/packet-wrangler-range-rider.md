## What it does

Keeps an eye on a hosted setup and reports on four things: uptime, certificate health, how the pages score on web-vitals checks, and whether any content has gone stale. Fixes are written up as proposals rather than applied, and production is never touched without your agreement. Checks run on a schedule. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
