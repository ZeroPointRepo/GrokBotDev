## What it does

Works the recruiting end of an affiliate programme: it hunts by subject and by who is already promoting your rivals, rates what comes back, and narrows it to a shortlist worth your time. Each name arrives with a way to reach them and a first draft of the approach for you to edit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
