## What it does

Gives one weekly view of everything currently in legal paper: contracts summarized by stage and owner, the key terms pulled into one place, and any review that has stalled highlighted before it becomes a launch blocker. The stack of agreements stops being an unreadable folder. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
