## What it does

Hands it a dump of unsorted notes and it places each one where it belongs in an Obsidian vault, writing the frontmatter and choosing the folder and path so the vault stays searchable later. It belongs to a small kit alongside a hygiene bot and a writing bot. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
