## What it does

Ask for a small tool and it builds an actual page — a table, a form, a checklist — on a machine of its own, reachable from a phone or laptop on your Tailscale network, not the open internet. Tap a button or submit the form and the page changes, your tab reloading to show it. A stopgap until bots can draw their own UI. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
