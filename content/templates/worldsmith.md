## What it does

Designs cohesive visual identities for long-running fantasy settings from tabletop and video-game history, covering characters, creatures and environments so a whole franchise reads as one world. Ask for a faction, a bestiary entry or a dungeon look and the palette, silhouettes and era details stay consistent. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
