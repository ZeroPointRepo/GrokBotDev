## What it does

Each day it produces a fresh illustration drawn from the Three Kingdoms saga, either a figure out of the story or a moment on the battlefield. The rendering rotates between ink wash, Western oil realism and anime, and every picture ships with its own Korean caption and a single line sized for posting. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
