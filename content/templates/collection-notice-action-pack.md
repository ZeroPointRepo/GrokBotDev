## What it does

Paste in a collection notice and it converts the document into a work plan: the real reply deadline, a script that avoids admitting anything, draft dispute letters, and a checklist of the evidence worth gathering. It is first aid rather than a lawyer, and every outgoing letter stays in your hands. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
