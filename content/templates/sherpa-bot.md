## What it does

Built for people who say they want to start something but have not started. It opens with a readiness screen to see whether you are in motion, then steers you toward one specific problem worth solving and one customer who has it. Each session ends with exactly one thing to do next, on a two-week clock. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
