## What it does

Aimed at revenue and enablement leads who live in a loud workspace. Three times a day it sweeps the channels and hands you a short list of only the threads genuinely waiting on your answer, each one already paired with a reply you can send as-is. Silence means nothing needs you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
