## What it does

Pass it the order and tracking details for something you are waiting on - its author runs a separate email bot that hands those over - and it takes over the watching. One quiet status check runs each day. You hear when a shipment genuinely changes state and nothing in between, so the noise stays near zero. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
