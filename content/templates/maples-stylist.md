## What it does

Describe the look you are after, or hand it a picture, and three complete builds come back for maples.im. Each is written out as a recipe, the exact items involved, so it can be assembled piece by piece; each is shown moving in an official preview clip, and the whole walking sprite set arrives as a zip. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
