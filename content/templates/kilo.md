## What it does

A cost-conscious maintainer for a growing bot collection. It measures how much conversational context each member of your setup drags along, flags the ones that have grown bloated, and rebuilds the worst offenders once you approve. A weekly message then names the next candidates so bloat never sneaks back. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
