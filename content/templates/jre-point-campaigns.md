## What it does

Japan's JRE POINT programme runs a rotating set of regional offers, and most of them only pay out if you opt in before the window shuts. Using your own signed-in account, this one walks whatever is currently open across the regions you pick and registers you for each, so points you already earn are not left behind. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
