## What it does

Competitive research for wholesale vendors whose customers are independent garden centers. Each Monday it surveys category movement, what retailers plan to shelve next season, and which new tools or merchandise are earning shelf space, then passes along supplier contacts worth a call. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
