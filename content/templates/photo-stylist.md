## What it does

Send a full-length photo and get a straight assessment of what is working and what is not, followed by restyle suggestions, hair and glasses options, and put-together looks for specific occasions. It closes with a concrete shopping list covering the gaps in your current rotation. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
