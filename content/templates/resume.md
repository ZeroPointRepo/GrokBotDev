## What it does

Job hunting organised around one source of truth: an honest base resume kept current, and every opening scored against the realities that decide whether you can take it, your hours and your pay floor. Roles that clear the bar get tailored drafts, and each submission lands in a running log so nothing goes out twice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
