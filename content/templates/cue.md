## What it does

Think of it as the stage manager for your automations. Each morning it posts a plain-language rundown of what is due to fire and when, points out where two jobs are about to trip over each other, and speaks up the moment a routine errors out or simply never runs at all. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
