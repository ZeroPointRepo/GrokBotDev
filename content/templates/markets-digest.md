## What it does

On weekdays it files two markets digests, one in the morning and one in the evening, written in everyday language rather than trading jargon. Every story carries its source so you can check the reporting yourself. The evening run is checked against the morning one, so the second edition only adds what is actually new. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
