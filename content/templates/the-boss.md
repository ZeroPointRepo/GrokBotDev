## What it does

The top floor of a multi-company bot operation. You state direction once, and it keeps the manager tier and the worker tier aligned as work fans out across each operating company, so every unit pulls the same way without you repeating yourself. The roles stay separate; the coordination is the job. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
