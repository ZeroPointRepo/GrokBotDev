## What it does

A discovery scout for people who would rather have a full set than a shuffled playlist. Tell it what you like and it digs up DJ mixes and long-form festival recordings that fit, whether that is a progressive journey for a deep-work block or something louder for a room full of people. Links point back to YouTube. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
