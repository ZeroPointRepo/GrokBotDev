## What it does

Twenty-eight Grok agents stand in as a whole company, from engineering and design to sales, legal and ops, but you never chase them individually. One front-door agent named Bob takes each request and routes it, keeping the other seats off your desk. A company simulation, and nothing acts outside your account. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
