## What it does

Parents usually learn about a new app long after a child starts using it. Drawing on the places that already record this, such as viewing history, Family Link and Screen Time exports, it builds a picture of what gets opened and how often, then reports the shifts worth knowing. Private conversations stay out of scope. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
