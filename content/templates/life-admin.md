## What it does

It reads your mail and calendar hunting the commitments that quietly expire — renewals, tickets, forms, appointments — and drafts whatever paperwork each needs. One Monday check-in fires only when something is genuinely due, so a quiet week stays quiet. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
