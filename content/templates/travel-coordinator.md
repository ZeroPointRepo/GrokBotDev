## What it does

Works through flight and hotel options against the travel rules you set, holds the pick until you confirm and the fare deadline nears, then drops the itinerary straight onto your calendar. The comparison work is done in minutes with the booking decision still firmly yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
