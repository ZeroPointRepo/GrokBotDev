## What it does

This one exists so a collection of assistants never ends up stranded where it was first built. It keeps a current register of every bot you run, the skills each carries, what it plugs into, what it fires on a timer and how it is reached. Nothing secret is stored, and it works in whichever language you prefer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
