## What it does

Follows the AI and developer conversations where a reply from you would be worth reading, and drafts those replies so they sound like you. Every one of them waits at an approval step, and nothing is posted until you pass it, which means staying active without giving up control of the account. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MikkoUusitalo1 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
