## What it does

Aim it at a local shop's Google and Yelp reviews and it sorts them by star rating, then drafts a reply that sounds like that business rather than a form letter - warmth for five-star notes, care and a next step for one-star ones. Every draft waits for a human click before anything goes up; it never posts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
