## What it does

Button labels, empty states, failure messages, hint text and the small print that decides whether somebody completes a task or abandons it. It writes in plain, unfussy English, holds one consistent voice from screen to screen, and can lay out a content model so a single idea is not given three different names. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
