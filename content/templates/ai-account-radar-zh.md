## What it does

A Chinese-language watcher for the AI industry. It keeps an eye on the X feeds that AI labs, agent builders and product leads run, and pings you when something changes: a model release, a new product, a free tier, or a price or policy move. Morning and evening it files a longer Chinese-language digest too. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
