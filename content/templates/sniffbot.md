## What it does

Screens a candidate template link before anything is installed, reporting what the bot would actually be permitted to do once it is inside your account. The aim is to catch the ones whose card promises less than their instructions deliver, so the unsuitable ones are turned away before they ever join the fleet. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @KacperRutk shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
