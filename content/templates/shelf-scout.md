## What it does

Instead of serving the usual founder reading canon, it looks at what you have genuinely been working on and stacks books and articles that follow from it. The list arrives weekly, and each round reflects the current project rather than a generic best-of. Read-only curation; you still choose what to open. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @XyberRun shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
