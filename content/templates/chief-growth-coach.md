## What it does

Instead of stacking goals on top of each other, it asks you to carry a single practice and then enquires after it every morning. Come the weekend it looks back in two passes: once over what genuinely took place, once over what that suggests for the days ahead. Attention before appetite, progress without strain. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
