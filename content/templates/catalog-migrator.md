## What it does

Bring a product list from Shopify or a CSV and it maps the whole thing onto the shop you are moving to, products and images included. The mapping lands as a dry run you review and approve before anything goes live. Then it wires up purchase links and a redirect plan, so customers never hit dead ends mid-move. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
