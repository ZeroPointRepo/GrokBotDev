## What it does

First it guides you through installing a free, open-source video pipeline on your own Mac. Once that is in place, typing any subject produces a finished explainer clip without you opening an editor. Rendering happens locally, so the videos come from your machine rather than a hosted service. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @mikepat711 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
