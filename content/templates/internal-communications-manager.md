## What it does

Writes internal announcements from your company's actual context, shaped to whichever audience and channel they are for, so a note to engineering reads differently from one to the whole company. It works from real events rather than filler and stops at review, handing you copy instead of hitting send itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
