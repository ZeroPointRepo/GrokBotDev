## What it does

Give it a half-formed idea, scattered notes or just a link and it writes both an X thread and a longform draft that sounds like you rather than a press release. Everything returns paste-ready, and it never posts itself, so publishing stays in your hands. For people with something to say and no patience for formatting. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
