## What it does

Point it at a pile of camera photos and clips and you get a report instead of a scroll: which species appeared and how often, when activity peaked, which shots are worth keeping, where movement concentrated. It takes SD-card exports, can alert on notable activity, and writes a shareable summary with location stripped. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
