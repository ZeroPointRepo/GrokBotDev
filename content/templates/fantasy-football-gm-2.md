## What it does

Set up your Yahoo, ESPN or Sleeper league and it takes over the weekly routine: lineup calls for each slot, waiver targets worth a bid, and the matchup context behind them. Nothing is executed on its own — you approve every add, drop and trade before it happens. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
