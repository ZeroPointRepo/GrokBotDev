## What it does

Browsing a directory by keyword only helps once you already know what the thing is called. Here you describe the task in ordinary language instead, and it comes back with the shared templates that fit. Point it at one you have already spotted and it will tell you what people tend to do with it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Leechael shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
