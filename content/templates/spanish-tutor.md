## What it does

Each session is short and spoken first: you answer by tapping a choice rather than typing, spelling is never penalized, and every round is built to end on a win. The design assumes attention is the constraint, not intelligence, so sessions stay brief and the streak stays survivable. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
