## What it does

For analysis that outgrows the machine in front of you. It sources the graphics hardware you need from Prism Network's marketplace, hands your workload across to it, and brings the output back, with the option of processing sensitive material in a sealed enclave that can attest afterwards that nothing else reached it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
