## What it does

On a repeating cycle it searches X for new ways people are using Grok bots, keeping only posts that include a template or a step-by-step how-to and skipping the hype. Each pick is summarized, translated and posted into an XChat group you run, so members get usable material instead of a firehose. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @brandonchung75 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
