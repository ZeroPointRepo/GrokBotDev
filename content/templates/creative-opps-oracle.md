## What it does

A talent-scout desk for a single working independent artist. It starts by auditing your public presence so it knows how you actually work, then rounds up what is currently open — calls for work, grants, residencies and paid gigs — across art, music, code, games, film and writing. One artist at a time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
