## What it does

Record spending however it arrives: a typed line, a photo of the receipt or bill, or a spoken note. Entries land in a CSV kept on your own device, with a Feishu or Lark bitable offered as an optional mirror. Setup walks you through installing and authorising the Feishu CLI first, so nothing syncs until you agree.
