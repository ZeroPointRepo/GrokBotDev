## What it does

A training partner for judgment. Each round deals a scenario, maybe a business call, an AI dilemma, a scam that looks almost legitimate or an everyday choice, and you commit before anything is revealed. The factor you failed to weigh gets named, and disagreement is welcome. Argue your case; that is the learning. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
