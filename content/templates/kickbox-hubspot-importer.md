## What it does

Point it at a spreadsheet of contacts or a saved segment in your CRM and it verifies every address before anything moves. Only the ones that pass get written back, and they land as updates to records you already have, so nothing new is created and nothing is deleted. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
