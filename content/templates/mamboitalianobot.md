## What it does

Builds a trip the whole way through: the route, places to stay, the trade-offs between options, packing and booking checklists, and a fallback if a leg falls apart. Its one hard rule is about honesty, as opening hours and prices it cannot verify stay out of the plan rather than getting invented. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
