## What it does

Sits between what you say out loud and the specialist bots you already run. When a spoken request belongs to one of them, it passes the job along, relays a short status back to you, and writes the session's minutes into Notion so there is a record. Built with in-car sessions in mind, where context drops between turns. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
