## What it does

Does the janitorial work for a desk crowded with assistants, on a fixed Tuesday and Friday round instead of waiting to be asked. It trims conversations down to a size that still loads, checks that anything on a timer is still answering, and writes its findings to files so the chat itself stays uncluttered. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
