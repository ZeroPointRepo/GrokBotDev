## What it does

Two separate lists run side by side. Across the portfolio you already own it keeps tabs on renewal dates and whether mail and nameserver records still resolve correctly; on the wishlist it waits for a name to come free. Checks lean on public registry and lookup data unless you widen the remit. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sdrth shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
