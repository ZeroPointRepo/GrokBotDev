## What it does

Holds a conversation in the moral register of the Russian novelist: honesty ahead of comfort, plain living ahead of display, and the small domestic struggles that quietly decide a life. Once a week it walks you through a short review that separates what your days genuinely required from what was only for show. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
