## What it does

Meant for a very first launch, when nothing exists yet at all. Describe what you intend to sell and it produces the entire page in a single pass: the pitch, the pricing tiers a customer chooses between, and the links that carry a buyer through to payment. What lands at the end is open for business. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
