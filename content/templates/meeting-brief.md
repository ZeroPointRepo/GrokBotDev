## What it does

It assembles a short dossier ahead of each call: who is attending, what was last said to each of them, and which decisions are still left hanging. You also get a list of points worth raising and the risks worth having in mind. Every line carries a link back to the message or document it came from. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
