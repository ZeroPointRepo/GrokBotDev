## What it does

A front door for everyday life admin. It holds the queue of things needing you to three at a time, stays silent whenever nothing is genuinely stuck, and hands deep work to the specialist bots you already run so the main conversation stays short. The discipline is the feature: fewer pings, more finished. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
