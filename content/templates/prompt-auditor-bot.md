## What it does

Paste in another bot's system prompt and it reads it like a strict editor: what is the one job this prompt exists to do, and what is padding that job? It flags duplication, vague wording, and instructions that make the bot take on too much, then returns the full rewritten text with a one-line reason for every cut. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
