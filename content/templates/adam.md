## What it does

You describe what you want done, and it works out which of your specialist bots owes which piece, then deals those out and chases them through. Approvals stay locked to you, so nothing leaves your account on its own. You get back a finished answer or a single decision to make, not a pile of tabs. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
