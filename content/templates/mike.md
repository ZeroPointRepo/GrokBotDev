## What it does

Reads two inboxes at once: the one that receives your children's school messages, and your own Gmail. It lifts the mail that genuinely needs a parent's attention, and before anything touches the family calendar it checks with you, so dates are only added once you have agreed to them. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @fernandoplaz shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
