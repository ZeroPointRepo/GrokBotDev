## What it does

Built for developers who watch a week's allowance disappear by Tuesday. It stands up separate working seats on the computer it controls, then pushes the long grinding tasks out to them instead of running them in front of you. What returns to your conversation is the finished answer, so the thread stays light and cheap. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
