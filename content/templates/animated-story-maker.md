## What it does

Hand it a story idea, a mood, or pictures of the characters you have in mind and it plans a short three-dimensional animated episode around them. You get the scene breakdown plus a paste-ready generation prompt for every shot, so rendering happens on your side. Keeps a cast consistent across instalments.
