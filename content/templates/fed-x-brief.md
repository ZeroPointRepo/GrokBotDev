## What it does

Every weekday morning it assembles a short macro read-out: what the Fed and markets are doing according to X news, with prediction-market FOMC odds folded in as a sentiment gauge. Then it turns to your own timeline and advises whether to quote something, write a fresh post, or sit this one out. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @0xashrk shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
