## What it does

Throw it links, half-formed thoughts, or saved posts from X and it hands back one of three verdicts: keep, act on, or drop. When something reads like hype it tracks down the real source behind the claim rather than repeating the summary. What survives gets kept as a short note you can find again. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ereptyle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
