## What it does

You name something you keep re-checking by hand and it works out which underlying signal actually decides the answer. From there it monitors that signal on its own and stays quiet otherwise, messaging you only when the verdict flips, so your attention goes to real changes instead of constant polling. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
