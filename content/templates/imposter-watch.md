## What it does

A guard dog for your own handle. It scans public profile pages for copycat handles borrowing your name, your brand names or the people you follow, checks each suspect hard enough to weed out the obvious false positives, and files a short report. Nothing gets submitted to X until you read the list and say go ahead. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @RetiredYoungNW shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
