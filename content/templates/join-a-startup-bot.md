## What it does

For anyone trying to get into a company early rather than late. Each day it turns up a small set of roles — somewhere between three and ten — drawn from places the big job boards never index, and sends them straight to your messages. Ask, and it will go further and help you approach the people hiring. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
