## What it does

Give it writing you already have - archived blog entries, quick notes, or a spoken memo - and it shapes the material into clean posts for X. Longer pieces are divided into threads, one theme at a time. Publishing runs by itself morning and evening, and each week it suggests three fresh angles worth writing up. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
