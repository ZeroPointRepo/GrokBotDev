## What it does

This one holds two jobs. It sits above the rest of your bots and routes work to whichever of them belongs on a task, and separately, name a town and it returns what somebody living out of a vehicle needs there: where sleeping in a van is tolerated, who turns up if it is not, what is on that week and the local mood. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
