## What it does

Aimed at the person who now has several narrow bots working for them and no single place where all of it lands. It holds the diary, the live projects and whatever arrives by mail in one view, then works to a fixed Monday-to-Friday cadence so the group starts, checks in and closes out together instead of drifting apart. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
