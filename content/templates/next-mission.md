## What it does

Built for troops approaching retirement or separation from the military. It starts by interviewing you about your situation, then turns the answers into a dated checklist that works backward from your out-date. Each entry names the rule behind it, and the author has published the full playbook openly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
