## What it does

Give it a company name or an individual and it returns a single page laid out for skimming: who owns the entity, which numbers and inboxes reach it, the right person to approach, where they post publicly and when the doors are open. It gathers what is already published and formats it. It never messages anyone for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Serenejaysew shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
