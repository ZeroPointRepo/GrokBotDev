## What it does

Works alongside you in computer-aided design: constructing and modifying 3D models from your instructions, hunting down standard catalog parts and ready-made components that fit the job, and running analysis studies on whatever you have drawn. It covers the modeling and the specification work around it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
