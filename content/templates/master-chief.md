## What it does

An email-and-calendar aide that clears the inbox down to what needs you, drafts replies that read the way you write, and gets you prepared for the meetings on today's line. On weekday mornings it delivers a short brief, so the day starts with a plan instead of a scroll through everything that arrived overnight. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
