## What it does

If you already run a fleet of bots that grade, rank or verify things, this one rebuilds each of them as a sibling that judges a whole batch in a single sweep. Long rubric chains collapse into one pass, so large sets stop crawling. It does not hand your bots new skills; it changes how their judgments get executed.
