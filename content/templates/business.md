## What it does

Runs your bookkeeping, customer list and site sign-ups on a published open-source database schema for businesses, keeping the complete transaction history rather than collapsing it into totals. Every figure traces back to the entries behind it, so the numbers stay auditable and reproducible. Sized for small operations. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
