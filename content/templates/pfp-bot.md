## What it does

Takes any picture, whether a profile shot, a selfie or a pet photo, and redraws it as a miniature avatar in the Grokbot style with capsule eyes. Hairstyle, palette and whatever else makes the subject recognizable carry over, so the result reads as you rather than a generic mascot. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
