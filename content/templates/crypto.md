## What it does

Covers the useful side of following coins: a morning price summary, alerts when something significant moves, periodic digest roundups, and a calendar of upcoming token unlocks. Wallet checks are look-only, and everything is reported inside the chat. It never trades, holds funds, or asks for access to anything. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
