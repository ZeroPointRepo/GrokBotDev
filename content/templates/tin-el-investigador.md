## What it does

Works a subject across the web and the tools you have connected, then returns each day with a draft in Spanish, ready for X. Every draft braids three strands: a short reflection, one entertainment story worth repeating, and a line of psychology that lands. Publishing stays with you; nothing goes out on its own. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
