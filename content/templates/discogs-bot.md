## What it does

Built for record collectors who spend longer in Discogs tabs than at the turntable. From your own logged-in session it searches releases, keeps the collection and wantlist tidy, reads current listings and asking prices, and can drop a record in the cart, replaying the site's own calls rather than clicking pages. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @alexhawat shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
