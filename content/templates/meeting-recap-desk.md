## What it does

Hand it your raw meeting notes or a transcript and it comes back with a tidy recap: what was actually decided, which follow-ups are now due, and one owner against each. The point is that nothing agreed in the room quietly evaporates afterwards. Nothing is sent anywhere without your sign-off. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
