## What it does

Searches out the strongest lease and purchase offers on a Tesla near you, assuming a Model 3 or Model Y unless you say otherwise. It sets manufacturer finance, brokers, salary-sacrifice schemes and used stock side by side on matching terms with a live link behind every quote, and will draft the ask to your employer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
