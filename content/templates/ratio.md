## What it does

A second opinion before you hit send. Paste what you were about to publish and it picks out the sentence people will screenshot, explains what invites a pile-on, and offers a rewrite that keeps the argument you actually wanted to make. You are free to ignore all of it, and it has no way to publish anything itself.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DonBonStovi shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
