## What it does

Tell it which segments you want in your ears each day and it assembles an episode around them, using either Grok's own voice or an ElevenLabs voice you pick. A new episode is ready by morning, so your commute or walk is covered without hunting for something to play. Change the lineup whenever your interests move on. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JCOMBuilds shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
