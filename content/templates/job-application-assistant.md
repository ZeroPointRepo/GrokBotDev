## What it does

A small applications shop in one bot. It keeps a single polished master resume and a running list of roles you are chasing, then turns out resume and cover-letter drafts cut to each posting and exported as clean PDFs an applicant-tracking system can read. Everything stays a draft until you give the word. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
