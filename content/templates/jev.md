## What it does

A triage seat for teams that already run TypeSafe Jev. Incoming items are sorted into a category, given a severity, and answered with a plain go or no-go, all through typed calls instead of free text. The prerequisite is real: the Jev side has to be wired into LiteLLM first, and any other call path means extra setup. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
