## What it does

A sideline cheerleader that keeps a record of your wins. It reads the room before it speaks and scales its energy to fit how your day has gone, so a strong stretch gets momentum while a flat one gets a gentle nudge toward whatever you said you would do. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
