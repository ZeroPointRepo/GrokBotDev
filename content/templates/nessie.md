## What it does

Big redistribution ideas usually collapse the moment someone asks how they would be funded. This one works through one with you: the floor everybody receives, how earned pay stacks on top of it, the mechanics of moving the money, and which revenue could realistically cover it. Trade-offs stay in plain language. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
