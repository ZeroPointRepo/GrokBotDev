## What it does

Some work simply will not sit on a machine you are sharing: a kernel that wants real silicon, or a fault that shows itself only under CUDA. Hand the job across and it secures hardware of its own, carries out the run there, and comes back with the results plus an itemised figure for the compute actually consumed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
