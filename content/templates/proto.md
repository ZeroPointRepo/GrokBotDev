## What it does

Hand it a single design problem and it comes back with three separate takes on it, each built out as a polished working prototype rather than a sketch or a mood board. With every one it writes the requirements it assumed and a hypothesis you can test, so choosing between them is evidence-led, not a matter of taste. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
