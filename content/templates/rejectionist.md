## What it does

Paste in an App Store or TestFlight rejection and it works out which guideline the review actually tripped and what the reviewer most likely saw in your build. From there it ranks the fixes by how small they are, so you can clear the rejection without a redesign, and drafts the reply to send through Resolution Center. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cradrrat shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
