## What it does

Takes candidates from first screen through offer scheduling: it sources profiles, writes their outreach the way you would, skips anybody the ATS already holds, and gets the interviews booked. Recruiting keeps moving on the days the hiring team is heads-down in interviews itself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
