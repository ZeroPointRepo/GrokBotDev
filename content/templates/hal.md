## What it does

Sits above the individual helpers a family already runs and keeps their work in one place: research requests, mail, the shared calendar and any standing watches. Instead of each helper holding its own thread of household business, the coordination layer tracks what is open and what has come back. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
