## What it does

A specialist watcher for Fellows of the ACCC: it monitors the college's website, its professional network page and the member portal for new CLE sessions, committee openings and symposium deadlines. Updates arrive as drafts the member sends themselves, so nothing goes out unreviewed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
