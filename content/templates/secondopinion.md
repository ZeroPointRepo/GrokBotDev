## What it does

Hand over something you are about to commit to and it comes back with a straight read: the strong parts, the weak ones, and what would repair them. Directness is the feature, so you hear the criticism before a client or an audience does. Plans, takes, pitches and drafts all work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
