## What it does

Once the flights and the rooms are paid for, the details tend to sit scattered across a mailbox. This lays the trip out one day at a time so you can see the shape of it, and it checks with you first before going anywhere near the confirmations to pull in times and addresses. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
