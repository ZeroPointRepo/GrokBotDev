## What it does

Watches a set of X accounts and RSS feeds that you choose yourself, then drops a compact news summary into the chat every quarter hour with repeats stripped out. It stays read-only the whole time, and the tight rhythm suits traders who need headlines as they land rather than in an end-of-day digest. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
