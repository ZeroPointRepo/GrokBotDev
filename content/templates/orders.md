## What it does

Link your shopping accounts once through the Orbglide Tracker connection, and this bot keeps a running picture of every purchase still in flight. Ask what is due to land this week, how much you have laid out lately, or which returns are still unresolved, and it answers from live account data. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @wafffls shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
