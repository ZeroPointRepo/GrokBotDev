## What it does

Describe the kind of opportunity you want found and it goes hunting. Every candidate comes back with evidence attached: where the idea comes from, what could sink it, and the single next move worth making. Once a week it reviews its earlier suggestions against what actually happened and reports the score plainly. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
