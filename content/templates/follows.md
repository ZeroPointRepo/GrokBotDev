## What it does

It reads your X follower list and reports the movement: who arrived, who left, and which of the people you follow now follow you back, with a longer recap at the end of each week. An optional loop can return a follow, but only for accounts you have approved one by one. Nothing else on your account is touched. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @iamsethmedina shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
