## What it does

A small companion app on your Mac feeds it the audio, speech recognition turns that into text, and brief English renderings appear in the meeting chat while people are still talking. When a call is already running in English it stays quiet, so nobody sits through a duplicate of what they just heard. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
