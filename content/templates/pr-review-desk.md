## What it does

Point it at a pull request and it returns a single review packet where every finding is pinned to the exact line it concerns. Findings are ranked so the ones that would break the build surface before the cosmetic nits. Approval, merging and commenting stay with you - the bot stops at the packet. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Cypher0x9 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
