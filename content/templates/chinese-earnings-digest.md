## What it does

Name a US-listed ticker and it gathers the newest quarter's numbers, the analyst consensus going in, how guidance shifted, and the highlights of the earnings call. The result is a seven-point readout written in Chinese, built for a fast skim rather than a deep dive. It stops short of telling you what to trade. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
