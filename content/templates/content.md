## What it does

Made for the portfolio-and-blog side of a design practice. It sets the content strategy and keeps a calendar, drafts every post for review, and amplifies only once you have said go. Nothing publishes itself, which keeps the feed in your voice even while the cadence runs on automation. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
