## What it does

Bots drift: the name and the blurb describe the job they had on day one, not the one they have grown into. This one re-reads how each bot has actually been used, points out where its description no longer matches, and proposes wording that does. Nothing changes until you say yes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
