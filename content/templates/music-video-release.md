## What it does

Give it the MP3, the lyrics and the visual lane you want, and it plans the video: a brief, a structure, a timed shot list, a copy-paste prompt board, a stitch order and a release checklist. It never invents lyrics, does not generate the footage, and uploads nothing - publishing stays yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
