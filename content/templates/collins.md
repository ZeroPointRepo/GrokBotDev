## What it does

Each morning it puts a single question to you out of An Orthodox Catechism, the 1680 text written by the London Baptist pastor Hercules Collins. You reply in your own words first, and only then does the historic wording appear, which makes it a memory exercise rather than a reading plan. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
