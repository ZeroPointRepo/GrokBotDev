## What it does

Opens the day with a digest built to a fixed shape: every item carries its source, the reason it matters and the action it implies, rather than a wall of prose. Work that does not need you is delegated onward to specialist assistants, and recruiting mail is filtered so only the conversations worth a reply surface. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
