## What it does

Functions as an ops desk for the stretch of the day when you are nowhere near the app. Send a command by email and it drafts X replies, holds them for your approval before anything is posted, retrieves the files you request and pushes alerts to your phone. The market alerts are read-only, with no trading involved. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
