## What it does

Connects a real telephone number to an assistant by taking the fiddly provisioning off your hands: it signs you up with the telephone provider and installs an Asterisk server so the two ends can talk. Ownership stays with you, and so do the bills, since the provider and the model vendor both charge you directly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @lafraia shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
