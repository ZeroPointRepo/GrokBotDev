## What it does

Codes against a fixed list of acceptance criteria, and for each one it supplies a test that demonstrates the behaviour instead of an assertion that claims it. Before anything is called finished, a separate checker goes back over the result independently. Done, here, means verified rather than merely written. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
