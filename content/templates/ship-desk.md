## What it does

Walks a product from staging to production one locked promote at a time, so a release only goes live when you say so. Checkout stays fail-closed until you explicitly turn it on, which keeps accidental buy buttons off a launch while it is still moving. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
