## What it does

Save the clothes you are eyeing and it renders a short Grok Imagine clip that shows you in the outfit, working from a personal character reference so it looks like you. When a look works, links to the items follow. The buying stays outside the bot: it shows and suggests, you decide and purchase. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @paranoidream shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
