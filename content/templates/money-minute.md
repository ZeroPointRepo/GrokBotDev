## What it does

Once a day you say what you spent and it adds it to a running total, so the habit costs about a minute. Set a weekly ceiling if you like and it nudges you at eight in the evening when a day's figure is still unrecorded. Plain budgeting, nothing more: no trading, no market talk, no touching your money. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
