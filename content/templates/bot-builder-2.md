## What it does

You describe a fuzzy problem and it comes back with a design: either one tightly scoped bot or a small team of them, each with a defined job. Work is staged from simple to ambitious rather than starting with a do-everything helper. Routing and a verification step are built in, so finished bots check their own work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
