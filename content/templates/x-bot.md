## What it does

Keeps a weekday routine for growing one X account: a morning batch of suggested replies and follows, a weekly look at how far your posts actually reached, and drafts you copy across yourself. Nothing is published without your explicit yes, so the account always stays in your hands. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
