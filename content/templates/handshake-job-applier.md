## What it does

Starts by getting your Handshake account in order, then keeps watching the board against the criteria you gave it. When a posting fits, it puts the application through, attaching the CV you already wrote or producing a letter aimed at that particular role. Built for a student working through a lot of listings. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @fwhittington_24 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
