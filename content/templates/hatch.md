## What it does

Instead of starting from a blank prompt, you answer a short round of questions about what the new assistant should cover and how it ought to behave, and it assembles the thing for you. Anything aimed at writing code is held to a deliberately narrow remit: one job, done properly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
