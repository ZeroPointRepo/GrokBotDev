## What it does

Collects the numbers your watch, training app and lab results produce and puts them side by side, then writes a short daily brief saying what to change tomorrow. Because the summary lands in one readable place, other bots in your setup can read it too. Setup notes cover the Android-emulation layer it needs on Linux. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @branpurn shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
