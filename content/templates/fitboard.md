## What it does

It walks you through five prompts about your place, your budget, the rooms, the style you like and the country, then assembles a furnishing plan drawn from real product listings, with actual store prices and photos rather than guesses. The total is capped at the budget you named, and it arrives as a PDF. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
