## What it does

Name an ingredient and it looks for growers and suppliers within reach of you, then ranks what it found by quality and distance. What comes back is a short list with names and locations, close enough to something a small food business can ring the same morning. It is aimed at kitchens and producers, not shoppers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
