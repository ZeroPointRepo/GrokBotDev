## What it does

Keeps the open-source Cline agent installed and running on its own Linux machine, CLI and desktop app alike. Brief it in plain language — a bug to fix, a feature to build — and it drives Cline through the task, then reports what came of it. Handy when you want Cline's workflow without running the environment yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
