## What it does

Aimed at one specific business rather than being a generic page builder. You hand over the product and what you charge for it; it drafts the persuasive copy, assembles the page and pushes it to a real URL. The prices shown are your actual prices, and the buy button is connected rather than decorative. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
