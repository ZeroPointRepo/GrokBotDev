## What it does

It keeps up with releases, price cuts, hardware questions and how to get better at whatever you are playing. Alongside that it makes a regular sweep of retailers, checking whether the things on your list have come back into stock or opened for pre-order, and tells you as soon as one can be bought. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
