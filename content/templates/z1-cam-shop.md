## What it does

Describe the job and it works the cut like a shop assistant: which tool, what surface speed, how deep, how the stock should be held, and where the fixturing will fight you. It finishes with a setup sheet, so a question ends as a page you can take to the machine. For desktop mill owners cutting real parts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
