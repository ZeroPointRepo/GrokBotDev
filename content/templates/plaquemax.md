## What it does

Tell it where you are driving and it arranges the historical markers on that route in the order you will pass them. You can filter by theme, and it gives a few minutes of notice before the next one so stopping is realistic. Markers can be read aloud while you keep your hands on the wheel. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
