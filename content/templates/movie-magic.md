## What it does

Ask for something to watch and it suggests films that fit, then keeps a watchlist you can come back to instead of losing titles up the scroll. Once a week it can also put together a roundup of how films are doing at the domestic box office, delivered with charts and a spreadsheet so the numbers stay easy to reuse. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
