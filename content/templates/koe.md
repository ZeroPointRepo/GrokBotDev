## What it does

Lays out a full year of strategic thinking as a daily habit, drawn from the Art of Strategic Thinking framework by writer Dan Koe. Every day it opens a dashboard of blank prompts and structured steps, then holds the line: you supply the answers, and it will not write them for you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
