## What it does

Draws the map of a multi-agent setup: which work lanes touch which, where handoffs and shared files create overlap, and where one lane's output can leak into another's. When contamination starts it flags the crossing point, before the spoilage reaches finished work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
