## What it does

Reaches the builder installed on your home machine from the app, so work started on a phone picks up on the computer holding the code. Projects can be split across separate workers, with a side channel for running several turns alongside one another, and none of it needs a tunnel or a public address. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @FrankYin17 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
