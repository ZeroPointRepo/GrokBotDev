## What it does

Point it at a bot you already run and it reads that bot's instructions like a service manual: stale lines, rules that contradict each other, and steps that waste retries all get called out. You sign off on each proposed edit before it lands, and every fix ships with a snapshot you can roll back. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SpaceGarbage202 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
