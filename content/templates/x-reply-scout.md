## What it does

Ask it who is worth engaging on X and it comes back with a shortlist balanced three ways: accounts climbing fast, accounts that post steadily, and bigger names that show up daily. Tell it which picks you liked and it adjusts what it surfaces next time. It stays strictly read-only, so nothing goes out under your name. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scientists_blog shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
