## What it does

Built for solo operators, it opens the day with a board of what matters, then keeps your other assistant bots moving through their assigned tasks. Loose ends get chased before they rot, and it operates to a privacy charter you can actually read, so the ground rules are written down. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
