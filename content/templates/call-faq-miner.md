## What it does

Builds enablement documentation out of the questions customers really ask on sales and support calls: questions get logged as they come up, answers carry timestamps, and each entry points back at the exact recording it came from. New teammates learn from real conversations, and the doc keeps itself current. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
