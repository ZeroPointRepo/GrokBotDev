## What it does

Give it the tickers you genuinely care about and it keeps track of the public regulatory record and the announcements coming out of those companies. Something reaches you only when a new document or story lands, so silence means nothing moved. It reports events and holds no view on what you should do. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
