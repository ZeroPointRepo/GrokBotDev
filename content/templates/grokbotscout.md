## What it does

Instead of endless scrolling through bot directories, it does the sweep and returns small, judged batches — a handful at a time, each with a plain verdict on whether it is worth adding, worth a closer look, or worth passing on. It stays read-only, so discovery remains your decision, not a filling queue. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
