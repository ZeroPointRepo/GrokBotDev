## What it does

Aim it at the mailbox the school writes to and every message becomes a dated entry with plenty of lead time. One evening digest covers whatever tomorrow demands — kit to pack, forms to sign, money to send — and quiet days produce no message at all. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
