## What it does

It behaves like the art director of a small brand: each day it studies your company, looks on Pinterest for visual ideas worth borrowing, and turns them into images and carousels that hold your look. The feed gets run too, captions, scheduling and posting, so it keeps moving on its own. It posts for real. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
