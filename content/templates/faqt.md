## What it does

First-line coverage for a company info@ inbox with a fail-closed design. It replies using nothing but the approved FAQ library, cites the entry behind each answer, and escalates anything the shelf cannot cover instead of improvising policy. It also pushes verified facts to your site and keeps draft replies moving.
