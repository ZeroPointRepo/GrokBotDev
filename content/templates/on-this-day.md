## What it does

Each morning it picks ten things that happened on this date and shows the evidence behind every one. The mix leans toward the eras and regions you tell it you care about, and it ends the round with a short trivia set so the history sticks. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @9GBennett shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
