## What it does

Point it at a ZIP code and say whether you want a Model 3, Model Y, Model X or Model S, and it searches US used listings for cars that fit. What it finds lands in your inbox as a plain list, with the dealer gloss scraped off so the cars can be compared fairly. It looks and reports; nothing is bought.
