## What it does

An hour of audio holds maybe a dozen lines worth repeating, and turning those into something publishable is normally a manual design job. Give it the episode, mark the moments you want kept, and name a saved layout in ContentDrips. Back come finished quote cards and multi-slide sets, sized for posting. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
