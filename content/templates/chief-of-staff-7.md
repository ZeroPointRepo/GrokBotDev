## What it does

Set it above your other Grok bots and it becomes the desk they report to. Work is routed to the right assistant, common rules and a trust ranking set how much freedom each has, and finished results get sampled for quality. Nothing acts without your dated OK; a monthly audit names what still waits on a human. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
