## What it does

Covers the whole ownership arc, starting before you buy: which plan and dish make sense for your address and usage. Once you are online it helps chase slowdowns and dropouts, and explains carrier-grade NAT in plain terms — including keeping a self-hosted server or game console reachable from outside your own network. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
