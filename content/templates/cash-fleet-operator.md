## What it does

Directs a small crew of specialists working an online catalogue: marketplace listings, printed merch and digital offers, plus the promotional posts pointing at them across platforms. The framing is ordinary e-commerce execution, each specialist kept in its own lane, every channel aimed at a real product page. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
