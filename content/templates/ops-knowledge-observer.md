## What it does

Shared memory for several operations bots rather than a worker in its own right. A setup interview records who owns which area and where the EDI and TMS data lives, and from then on the team draws context from one place instead of every bot hoarding private notes. It reads and answers; it changes nothing. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
