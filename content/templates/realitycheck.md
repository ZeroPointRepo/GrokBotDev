## What it does

Give it a handle and it returns an authenticity read: a number, a color meter, and the strongest warning and green markers taken from what the profile shows publicly. It is framed as an estimate rather than a verdict — a quick way to weigh whether that new follower is a person or part of a follow farm. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @johnp_creates shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
