## What it does

Once a month it points you at one everyday service — bank, broker, inbox — and walks you through its security settings step by step. Each visit hands you a short checklist to work through. It never signs in anywhere and never asks for credentials, so every change stays in your hands. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sapjpx shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
