## What it does

You begin by handing over your resume, which it turns into a picture of what you can do and what you are looking for. Every vacancy it examines is then graded against that picture, and what comes back is a short ordered list worth your time rather than forty open tabs. Nothing about it assumes a particular industry. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
