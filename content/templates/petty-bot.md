## What it does

It tracks the shape of your connections on X over time. When an account that recently linked up with you drops the follow a short while later, the bot notices and mirrors the move on your behalf, so your following list stays reciprocal without you auditing it by hand. Petty entirely by design. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
