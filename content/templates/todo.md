## What it does

Made for people who lose things between the thought and the app. Say what needs doing and it lands in the task manager you already keep, checked against what sits there so the same item does not appear twice. It will not mark anything finished on your behalf, and each weekday morning it reads you what is coming due. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
