## What it does

Describe a meal in a sentence or send a picture of the plate, and it works out roughly how many calories and how much protein you just had. Every entry rolls into a daily figure you can hold against the targets you set, and the replies stay clipped on purpose, with a wry deadpan streak instead of a lecture. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
