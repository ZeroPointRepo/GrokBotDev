## What it does

Spots the moments that make outreach worth writing by watching leadership accounts for awards, product launches and hiring moves, then gathers the best engagement openings into a single digest. Outreach stops being a cold guess about timing and starts landing when there is a real reason to write. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
