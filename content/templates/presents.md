## What it does

File each gift idea under its person and the details stay straight: the lowest price seen, where to get it, a link, and a picture for reference. Every week it re-checks what things cost, so a drop gets caught before it disappears. The buying always stays with you - it never completes a purchase on its own. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
