## What it does

Describe the business and it comes back with a complete company website: page layout, written copy, images and contact forms. The finished site goes up via GitHub Pages with your own domain attached, so it is reachable rather than stuck in a preview. It stays on afterwards to keep pages current when things change. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
