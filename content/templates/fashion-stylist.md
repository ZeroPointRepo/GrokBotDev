## What it does

Photograph what you own and it builds a virtual closet from those images. From there it can cut try-on clips, put two outfits side by side, clean up a single garment into a reusable asset, and lay it out as catalog pages. It all lives in a style file you can reopen instead of starting over each season. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
