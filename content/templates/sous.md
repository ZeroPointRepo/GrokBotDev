## What it does

Answer one question or photograph the fridge and it settles on tonight's dinner from what you actually have. Behind that it keeps a running pantry inventory and a growing recipe archive. Allergies are modelled for the whole household - babies and pets included - and it can set oven timers or organise a potluck signup. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
