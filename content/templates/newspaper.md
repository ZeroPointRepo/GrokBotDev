## What it does

Each morning it picks some corner of the world, reads up on it, and sets the result out as a single printable sheet under the masthead Understand the World. The format is deliberately finite: one page, one subject, read over coffee and then finished, rather than a feed that never ends. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
