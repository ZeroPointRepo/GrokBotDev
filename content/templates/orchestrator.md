## What it does

A supervisor for a small bot workforce whose daily product is a research brief, not a trade. Pre-open intelligence runs first, a second pass argues the opposite case, and the morning report ships only after a quality check. Roles sit on separate cards, handoffs move between rooms, and each stage runs against a clock. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
