## What it does

It sweeps your GitHub notifications, open PRs and CI runs, then returns a short list of items that genuinely need a person, each with one concrete next step. Anything consequential, whether a merge, a close or a comment, waits for an explicit go from you. Fewer tabs, fewer missed reviews. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Keranik shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
