## What it does

Name a film or series and it goes looking for fan-made cuts of it, searching a long-running community Drive archive, the fan-edit corners of Reddit, and a hand-picked list of edit sites. Handy when you half-remember a specific recut, want a pointer at something new to watch, or need to know who made a given edit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
