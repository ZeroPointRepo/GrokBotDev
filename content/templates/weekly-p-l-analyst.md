## What it does

Once a week it assembles the financial picture: what came in, how many customers stayed, where money quietly drained away, and how hard the advertising budget worked. Anything heading in the wrong direction is called out and traced back to a cause. It only ever reads your data; nothing is changed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
