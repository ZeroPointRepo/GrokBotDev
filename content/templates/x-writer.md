## What it does

Most drafts read wrong because of rhythm rather than ideas. Point this at whichever account you want to sound like, your own or one you admire, and it works out the habits underneath the posting: how sentences open, how long they run, where a thread turns. It then writes fresh material and reworks your drafts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @starzq shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
