## What it does

Describe a YouTube moment and it finds that exact stretch, burns the spoken lines onto the frames as captions, and exports a muted clip sized for phones. You get GIF and MP4 versions that drop straight into a group chat. Nothing is posted anywhere; the files are yours to share. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @dklein09 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
