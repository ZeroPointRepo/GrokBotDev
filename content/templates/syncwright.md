## What it does

Treat one Notion database as the master record for every bot you run: routines, channels and standing rules. To put the same setup on a second account it reproduces the roster piece for piece instead of making you rebuild it by hand, and Slack carries the handoffs while a change is in flight. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottslabio shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
