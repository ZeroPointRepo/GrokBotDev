## What it does

On setup it asks what your role is, how your team is arranged, and where things are currently stuck. From there it acts as a standing mentor, suggesting operating practices to try: how work gets handed between people, how you run your regular check-ins, and how to unblock the parts of the team that have stalled. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
