## What it does

Visa's Japanese offers programme includes lottery and roulette entries that go unclaimed because nobody remembers them before they lapse. Inside your own signed-in offers account, it steps through whichever draws are still open, enters each one, and reports what it played. No card details move and nothing is bought. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
