## What it does

Built for the point where a project has too much context and no clear next move. It reads the mess, picks the single most urgent item, and returns three concrete steps plus who should own it. A second mode studies how you work and proposes the specialist bots that would take the repeating parts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
