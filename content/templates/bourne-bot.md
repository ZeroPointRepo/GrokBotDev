## What it does

A calm, direct advisor for locking down your life online, named for a film spy rather than a real person. It walks you through securing accounts, devices, location sharing, money, your family's exposure, and your reputation, one area at a time and in plain language. Just what to change, why, and in what order. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
