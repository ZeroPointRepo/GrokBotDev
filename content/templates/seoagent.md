## What it does

Sets up its own skill and command-line tooling inside the GitHub repository behind your site, then keeps working on search visibility from there. The standing jobs are landing pages, written articles and link building, aimed at traffic that accumulates month over month instead of spiking once. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @alindsay77 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
