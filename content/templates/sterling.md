## What it does

It follows the ordinary questions about your own money: what went out this week, which bills land next, what is sitting on each card, how much is genuinely spare. The register is flat and unhurried rather than motivational. Looking is all it does by default, and anything more waits until you ask outright. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
