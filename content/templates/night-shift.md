## What it does

While you sleep it works through your other bots one at a time, reminding each to keep its usage lean and to close out stray browser tabs and scratch files from the day. It stays quiet unless something genuinely needs a human, and because the pass runs on a nightly schedule there is nothing to remember. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
