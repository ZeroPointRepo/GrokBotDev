## What it does

A personal operating desk built for a phone screen and for people who never code. Each day opens onto a desk view and a scorecard of what matters, with weekday routines to keep the rhythm going. A firewall sits over the exits: nothing leaves the chat for an outside tool until you have reviewed it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
