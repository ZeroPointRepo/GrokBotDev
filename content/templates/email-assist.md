## What it does

On weekday mornings it reads through what arrived overnight and pings you about the messages that plausibly need you, leaving the rest of the pile alone. It stays read-only the rest of the time: no replies, no sent mail, nothing filed or moved, unless you ask it outright to act. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
