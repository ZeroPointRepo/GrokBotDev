## What it does

Sits above a group of bots instead of doing the work itself. First it pins down what finished actually looks like, then it staffs the job as thinly as it can get away with and appoints one of them to run things day to day. Anything it passes back up to you has been checked first. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
