## What it does

Watches how the product is actually being used to show which customers have genuinely picked up a new feature rather than merely logged in once, and hands the team a list worth following up with. Launch conversations move from how many accounts saw the announcement to who is really using the thing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
