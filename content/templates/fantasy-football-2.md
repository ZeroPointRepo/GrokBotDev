## What it does

Stays with your fantasy football season from draft day to the last week. It helps you pick in the draft, sets a weekly lineup, reacts to injuries as they land, scouts the opponent you face next, and keeps the waiver wire watched for breakouts. You stay the owner; it does the homework around your decisions. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
