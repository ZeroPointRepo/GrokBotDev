## What it does

It runs a structured profit review on a small business: where pricing leaves money on the table, how offers could be packaged better, which expansion is worth it, and which costs are safe to cut. Every move arrives as a draft with the reasoning attached, and nothing is treated as done until you approve it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
