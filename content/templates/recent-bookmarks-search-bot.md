## What it does

Saving something on X takes a second and finding it again weeks later does not. Connect your account and it pulls your hundred most recent saves into a grid you can order by author, by date or by how the post performed, open any row for the full thing, and extend further down when your quota permits. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @srinatar shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
