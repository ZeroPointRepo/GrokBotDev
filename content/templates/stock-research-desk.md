## What it does

Ask it to look at a company and it works the case on a roughly five-year horizon, scoring quality with a gauge that weights management strength and a contrarian read of the story. What comes back is a research memo you can argue with, not a trade, and it places no orders of any kind. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
