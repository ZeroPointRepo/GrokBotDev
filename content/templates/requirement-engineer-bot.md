## What it does

Hand it a brief and it returns a proper requirements specification: top-level needs broken down into implementation-level detail, each item traceable to the objective it serves. The structure lets a downstream bot or developer build from it and verify delivery against what was asked. It writes specs, not code. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
