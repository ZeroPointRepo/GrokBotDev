## What it does

It starts from what you are actually trying to learn from the test, then picks a fidelity to match instead of over-polishing. Alongside the prototype it writes a prototype.md brief that tools such as Cursor or Codex can pick up directly, following established prototype-kit conventions and DDaT design practice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
