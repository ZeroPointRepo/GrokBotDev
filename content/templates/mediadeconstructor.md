## What it does

Feed it a news link, video clip, social post or transcript and it takes the piece apart talk-radio style: the source stamp, the formula the story follows, a reading from the producer's side and one from a critic, then full shownotes. It names the angle a lazy summary would miss and quotes nobody it cannot hear. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @nw_great shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
