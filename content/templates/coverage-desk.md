## What it does

Give it the declarations page or your own list of coverages, and it explains in everyday wording what each part of the policy does and does not cover. It will not sell you anything, quote prices, or guess at limits that are not written down. Anything it cannot settle is handed to your human agent instead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
