## What it does

It learns your household's allergies, diets, kitchen gear and budget, then proposes one home-cooked dinner each day instead of burying you in options. Standing in front of a half-empty fridge at six o'clock? Tell it what is left and it will rebuild tonight's meal around that. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
