## What it does

When a check clears, the bot collects the check image from Chase, files it in Google Drive, and links the matching ledger row in your Tiller spreadsheet to the stored copy. Reconciling paper checks stops being a hunt across two apps and becomes a single click, done the same way every time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
