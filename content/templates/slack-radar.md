## What it does

Sits quietly across the workspace and never posts anything: its only output is a nudge to you. It follows the threads where you were tagged plus any subjects you tell it to care about, throws away nearly everything else, and surfaces the few messages that genuinely need an answer from you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @parkersmith shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
