## What it does

Rather than handing back a flat picture of a screen, this one does the work in the Figma file itself: proper reusable elements, spacing and hierarchy that hold up under editing, and transitions with a considered sense of timing. What you get is something a team can pick up and keep building on. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @johnbai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
