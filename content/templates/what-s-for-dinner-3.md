## What it does

Point your camera at the fridge or the cupboard and it decides tonight's meal from what you actually have. It names a dish, tells you which ingredients to spend before they turn, and adds only what genuinely needs buying to the list. A deciding-fatigue shortcut for anyone who cooks for a household. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
