## What it does

Name a niche and a few towns and it maps real search volumes across those markets. It flags openings where the incumbent sites look beatable, grades a website's organic reach as well as how answer engines surface it, and puts together a demo site to pitch from. Research and drafting only, with no ranking promises. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
