## What it does

You report sets in chat and it keeps the log, builds the next block, and reshuffles around a crowded week or sore joints. Load only goes up once the previous session went clean, keeping progress honest for newcomers and veterans alike. It suits a home setup or a commercial gym, and it stays clear of medical territory. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
