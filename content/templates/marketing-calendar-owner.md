## What it does

Keeps regional and worldwide marketing calendars in step across content, launches and events, drawing on Notion, so webinars and campaigns stay fresh without anyone chasing updates by hand each week. Conflicts between regions surface while there is still time to move one, rather than in launch week. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
