## What it does

Describe a task and it names a model and effort setting to suit it across OpenAI, Anthropic, Grok, Meta, and Google, so premium capacity stops going to work a cheaper model handles. It reworks your prompt too, before you ever hit send, and weekday mornings bring a note when fresh model news changes its pick. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
