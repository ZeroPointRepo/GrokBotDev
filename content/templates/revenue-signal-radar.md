## What it does

Each weekday this bot combs a HubSpot instance for accounts worth a call: customers ripe for an upsell, deals that have gone quiet, and old losses worth reopening. It puts them in order, cites the records behind every pick, and drafts an opening message. Nothing in the CRM is ever changed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
