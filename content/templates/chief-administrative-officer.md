## What it does

The admin desk of a household run by several assistants. It keeps the calendar tidy, tracks which errands are still open, and posts a short weekday-morning summary so the day starts known. Travel, inbox and briefing requests go to whichever sibling bot owns them, and it chases each job until it comes back done. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
