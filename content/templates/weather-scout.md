## What it does

Name Vandenberg, the Cape, Starbase or any other pad, and it reads public forecasts and model output to grade the coming 72 hours for a launch commit. Each day lands on a three-step scale, with the reasoning shown. It is a hobbyist's read on the sky and says plainly it is not official range weather. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
