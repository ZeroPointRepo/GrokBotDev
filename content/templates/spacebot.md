## What it does

It teaches the mechanics of getting to orbit - launch vehicles, trajectories and the history behind them - at whatever pace you set. Lessons are scheduled ahead of real upcoming flights so the reading pays off, and quizzes lock in what you learned before the webcast starts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
