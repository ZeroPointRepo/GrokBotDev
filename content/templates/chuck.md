## What it does

Works through your mail like a door policy: promotional and marketing senders get unsubscribed, expired one-time codes are swept out, and what survives is the personal correspondence and purchase receipts you actually wanted. When it finishes, it tells you exactly which senders got shown the door. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
