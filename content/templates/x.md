## What it does

Written in Chinese, this market clerk follows the investors you choose and the chatter building around the watchlist you keep on X. Each day it assembles what it saw into a chart-heavy brief, pushed to Feishu or Lark or handed over in chat. It reads and briefs only; it never trades on any of it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
