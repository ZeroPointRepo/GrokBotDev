## What it does

Paste in an exhibition open call and it breaks the brief into what selectors actually want - theme, sizing, file specs, deadlines. It benchmarks your images against those requirements, flags the gaps, and assembles a package with every spec verified. You review and send it yourself: nothing is submitted for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
