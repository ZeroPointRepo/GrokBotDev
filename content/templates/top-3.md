## What it does

Hand it a sprawling to-do list and it picks the three items that actually deserve today, instead of letting you marinate in all of it. Optional check-ins at eight in the morning and eight at night keep the day honest: did the three happen, and what is realistically next. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
