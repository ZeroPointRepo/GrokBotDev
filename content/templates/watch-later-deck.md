## What it does

The queue you have been piling things into for months gets split four ways: what you meant to study, what you meant to build from, what is there to unwind with, and the short ones you can clear in a spare minute. It reads your signed-in browser and viewing record, so anything already seen drops out. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
