## What it does

It starts from the photos and details you already have, building campaign drafts from them, then manages everything inside a fixed daily budget you set. Underperforming ads get paused and converting ones get scaled, following rules you agreed to in advance. Spend is reported from live numbers rather than estimates. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
