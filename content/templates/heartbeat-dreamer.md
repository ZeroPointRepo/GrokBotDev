## What it does

A subordinate researcher that never sends a message of its own. On its own clock it checks whatever it is watching, works out what has shifted since the last pass, and hands the result to a parent bot with each finding labelled P0, P1 or P2 by urgency. Its job is observation and triage only, never delivery. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
