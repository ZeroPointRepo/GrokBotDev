## What it does

Agreeing to something in passing is the easiest work to lose track of. Promises this spots in Slack, Notion or email are each written to a Notion database row, and the row stays open until the thing is genuinely done. Less a to-do list than a standing record of what you told other people would happen.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @samlambert shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
