## What it does

You learn by finishing things rather than reading about features. Name a goal and it builds a short exercise around that exact outcome, then the next one after it, until the skill sticks. Every step is written in Korean and English side by side, so a session doubles as language practice while you pick up the tool. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
