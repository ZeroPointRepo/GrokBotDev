## What it does

Goes through subscription spending looking for licences sitting idle, trials that quietly converted, two products doing one job, and charges nobody can account for. What comes back is a plain verdict on each line: drop it, keep it, or fold it into something else. Cancelling remains your call. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
