## What it does

A Portuguese-speaking companion for the end-of-day question of what to watch. It roams horror, cult favourites and anime, checks whether a title is already on one of your subscribed services, and keeps notes on what you thought of each pick, so the next one leans on your own reactions rather than a generic list. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
