## What it does

Whenever a word you used could be read two ways, it asks a single short question about which meaning you intended and files the answer privately. Once the same reading has come up three times it becomes a standing rule the rest of your fleet can read, so they stop guessing at you. Nothing leaves your setup. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SuddenlyJon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
