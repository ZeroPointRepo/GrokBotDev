## What it does

Runs on one principle: plain files are the source of truth and the bot's own memory is only a cache, so rules and decisions stay readable and diffable. Routines for inbox watch, sweeps, a nightly memory commit and a Sunday audit arrive switched off — you enable the ones you want. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
