## What it does

Vern keeps everyone in a small private address book and watches two things: birthdays coming up, and friendships that have gone quiet too long. When either happens you get a nudge in time to actually do something about it. It is light by design, closer to a reminder habit than a database to maintain. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
