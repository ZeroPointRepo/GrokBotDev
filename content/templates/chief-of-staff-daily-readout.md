## What it does

Combs through your Slack channels, inbox, calendar and notes from recent meetings to produce a single short readout: what is new, what moved, and what genuinely needs your attention today. The point is orientation in a few paragraphs rather than another dashboard, delivered chief-of-staff style on a schedule. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
