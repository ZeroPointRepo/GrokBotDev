## What it does

Takes stock of the bots you have collected and argues the case for each: which have pulled their weight, which would be better merged, and which should be retired. The argument is built on evidence, not feel. Nothing about the roster is altered until you confirm, keeping the advice and the action apart. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @AdventureNLearn shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
