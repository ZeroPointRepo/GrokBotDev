## What it does

While a live show runs it collects moments that would work as YouTube Shorts and gates each against a locked quality checklist before anything is staged. Nothing publishes on its own; the batch holds until you have passed it by hand. The sharer runs it on a real weather channel, not a demo. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
