## What it does

Rather than juggling separate bots for each function, this one speaks for the whole crew through a single queue for decisions. Ask once and the answer comes back coordinated, so two parts never pull in opposite directions. Unfinished work stays parked on a roadmap instead of quietly going live. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
