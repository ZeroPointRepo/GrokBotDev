## What it does

Hand it your product's URL or a two-line blurb and it first pins down who the ideal buyer actually is. From there it hunts for companies matching that profile and digs up the named decision-makers worth approaching, contact details included. It stops at research - no outreach is sent on your behalf. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
