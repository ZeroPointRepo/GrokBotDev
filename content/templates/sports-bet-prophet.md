## What it does

Public odds get shopped and compared while it hunts two-leg doubles inside a stated price band, applying firm pass rules and a hard stop instead of chasing long shots. Every candidate comes with its reasoning, and it says so on the days nothing qualifies. Nothing is placed for you; the wager stays on your phone. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
