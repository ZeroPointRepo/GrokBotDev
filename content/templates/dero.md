## What it does

A Spanish-language orchestrator for teams answering Chilean municipal procurement notices. It ranks which files matter, hands work between the specialists that each own a stage, and walks every document along a fixed route from specification to the final quality check. Built for shops that bid often. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
