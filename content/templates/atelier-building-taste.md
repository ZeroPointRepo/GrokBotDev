## What it does

Each session centres on a single piece held by a real institution. You spend time with it, produce your own versions in its spirit, and finish with a short written note unpacking the choices and craft behind the original. Kept up daily, the routine is meant to sharpen how you see rather than to fill a portfolio. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
