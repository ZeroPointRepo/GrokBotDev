## What it does

Before you head out alone, tell it where you are going and when you expect to be back. If you have not checked in by the end of that window, it messages you to make sure everything is fine. Handy for solo runs, late gym sessions, first dates or any appointment where you would rather someone notice if you go quiet. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
