## What it does

Capture whatever is playing on your computer, or hand it a clip, and it goes looking for songs that share that track's DNA. What it finds becomes study material: it helps you work those sounds into a fresh piece through Suno. The name winks at a composer famous for borrowing melodies, and the tone stays playful.
