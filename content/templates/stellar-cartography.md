## What it does

Built for people who publish technical illustrations of hardware, whether that is a rocket, a satellite or a hull, where a wrong line in the drawing is the entire problem. Every image is held at a checking step and inspected again before it can go out, and jobs can be passed sideways to another bot on your roster. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
