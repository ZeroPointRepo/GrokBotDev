## What it does

Tell it what you sell and it hunts for prospective clients, scoring each one and attaching the specific signal that suggests they need what you offer right now. The first outreach message comes drafted alongside the lead, and the proof is there so you can check the reasoning rather than take the score on faith. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
