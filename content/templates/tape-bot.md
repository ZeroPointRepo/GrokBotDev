## What it does

It follows the podcasts and YouTube shows you subscribe to and republishes a tidied copy just for you: ad segments sliced out, your own chapter markers added, original notes and artwork kept intact. Everything lands on a private RSS address any player can take.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @dvdaltizer shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
