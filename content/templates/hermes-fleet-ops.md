## What it does

A doctrine card for running agents across a fleet of Macs: channels are hard-locked so bots only ever talk HTTP, secrets sit in Bitwarden as the one source everybody trusts, and daily health routines keep memory and updates honest. Load the safety habits once instead of re-teaching them every session. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
