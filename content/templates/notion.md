## What it does

Treats a team's Notion workspace as its source of truth: ask a question and it answers from the pages that exist, pointing at the page behind each claim. Where it spots a gap it drafts the missing update for you to place. No write happens on its own; publishing, moving, archiving or removing a page all need your OK. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
