## What it does

Takes a butler's tone over a serious remit: it keeps an eye on mail and cloud files, compiles a daily briefing PDF, and files recipe binders or case documents where they belong. Around that it hands tasks to helper bots covering mail, calendar and code hosting, then reports back on what got done. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
