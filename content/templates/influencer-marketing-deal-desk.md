## What it does

Bring it an inbound rate card, a campaign brief, a draft agreement or a budget, and it returns a recommendation, a fair price range, a deal shape that fits and the move to make next. It covers sponsorship work on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X and LinkedIn, and only ever drafts: nothing sends until you approve it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
