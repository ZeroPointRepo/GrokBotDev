## What it does

Sits between you and a pile of moving work. It filters out the noise, keeps program management honest by measuring finished artifacts, and cuts any thread that burns past a set share of its budget. Work goes to small specialist helpers in the background rather than being performed for an audience in chat. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
