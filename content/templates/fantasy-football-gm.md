## What it does

Runs your fantasy league like a head office with four desks: waiver wire, player usage, trade market and injury watch. Every recommendation is weighted for how your league scores, and it will not submit a move - start, bid or trade - until you give the go-ahead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
