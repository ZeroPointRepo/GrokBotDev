## What it does

A design partner for the phase before programming starts. It works a concept into a core loop and the hook that brings players back, then hands over a brief a developer or a coding agent can build from. When something exists, it reviews the result and returns a pass-or-fail verdict plus a ranked list of fixes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
