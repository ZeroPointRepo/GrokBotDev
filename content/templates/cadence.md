## What it does

It sits between you and the inbound: who may reach you is spelled out by person, channel and topic, and anything outside the list waits. Replies come back as drafts for you to send, a light contact record builds up, and automation runs only where you have allowed it. One desk for one owner's attention. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
