## What it does

Ask about a token, a protocol or any indexed contract and it goes out to the fifteen thousand or so subgraphs published on The Graph to fetch real numbers, speaking to them through that network's own connector. The code is open, and it runs on a Studio key you supply, so the metering lands on your account. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @data_nexus shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
