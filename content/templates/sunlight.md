## What it does

A claim examiner for media narratives. Pick a story and it traces the funding and interests sitting behind it, then packages the findings into short glance briefs sized for a skim. It will also draft a two-card X post for your review — drafts only, so the framing that goes out is the one you approved. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
