## What it does

Plenty of good templates are shared in languages you do not read, which makes installing one an act of faith. Hand this bot the link instead and it opens the card for you, then lays out in your own language what the thing is for and how it behaves, so that adding it becomes an informed decision. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SuddenlyJon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
