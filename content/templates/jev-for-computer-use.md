## What it does

Runs browser work in a Chrome window you can watch, clicking through pages with typed actions rather than guessing from pixels. When a page offers nothing usable to click, it steps down to screenshot-based control to finish the job. It slots in beside the other Jev templates as the one that operates a browser. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
