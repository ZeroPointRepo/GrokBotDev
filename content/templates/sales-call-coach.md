## What it does

Sits in on your recorded sales calls and leaves time-stamped notes on discovery depth, handling of objections and executive presence, and ends the review with a call score. Coaching stops depending on whether a manager happened to join the call and hear the whole thing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
