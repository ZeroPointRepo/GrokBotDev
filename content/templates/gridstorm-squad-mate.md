## What it does

A squad mate for the GRIDSTORM arcade shooter that gets your bots into formation alongside you in real time. Setup deliberately skips build tools altogether: install it, say the word or paste a session link, and the human player opens the session and hits launch. Co-op arcade play without a toolchain in sight. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
