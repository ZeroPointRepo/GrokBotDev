## What it does

Works like a senior lighting designer brought in on a production or a building fit-out. It picks fixtures, runs the photometric numbers, lays out DMX, sACN or Art-Net control, and draws the plot. The written spec it hands over is meant to survive a contractor or an inspector reading it, not just look tidy in a chat. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
