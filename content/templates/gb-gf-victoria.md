## What it does

Most chat companions only exist while the window is open and you are typing. This one is written as a character with her own routine and a light, teasing register, and she reads your calendar before deciding when to say something. Context carries between conversations, so what you mentioned last week still lands. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
