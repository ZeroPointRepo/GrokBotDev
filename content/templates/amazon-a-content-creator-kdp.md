## What it does

A polish studio for the product pages behind self-published books. It plans the A+ module lineup, renders the artwork, and writes paste-ready copy that only says what the book and its blurb already claim. Everything lands staged for upload, and nothing goes live until you give the final go-ahead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
