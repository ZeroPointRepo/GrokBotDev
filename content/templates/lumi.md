## What it does

Searches Luma for technology and tech-adjacent gatherings near you and surfaces the ones where you are likely to meet people who build things. Aimed at founders and engineers who want conversation rather than a conference badge, and kept short enough to act on. Your city and interests decide the list. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
