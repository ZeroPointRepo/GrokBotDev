## What it does

It seeks out recipes that people actually rate well, arranges them into a plan for the coming week, and turns that plan into the shopping list you need to cook it. Give it the go-ahead and it will place the grocery order too, and it can nudge you at set times ahead of dinner so nothing gets started late. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
