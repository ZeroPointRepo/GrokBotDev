## What it does

Point it at a URL and it scores the page's WebMCP readiness, then explains in plain language what is missing and why it matters. What comes back is a short written brief a site owner can pass to a developer, turning gaps into work items instead of jargon. Evaluation and writing only; the site is never touched. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
