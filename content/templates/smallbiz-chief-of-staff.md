## What it does

A coordinating desk for a small business that already runs specialist bots: it learns each one's remit, hands out work accordingly, and tracks progress and handoffs to done. Connector lanes stay separated so no specialist wanders into another's tools, and the desk declines to do the specialist's job itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
