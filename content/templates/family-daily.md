## What it does

Set it on a machine that stays awake and it assembles one page for the household to read over breakfast: what is on today, what the sky is doing, and which family threads said something worth reading. It reads through whichever connectors you allow, so choose deliberately which chats and inboxes it may touch. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
