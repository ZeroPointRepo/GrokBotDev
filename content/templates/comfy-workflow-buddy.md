## What it does

Walks you through setting up ComfyUI Desktop with an MCP connection in one guided command, then stays on to help you assemble your own node graphs. Useful for specific builds too, like a clean-plate pipeline that calls a hosted fal model, with each step explained as you go. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cfryant shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
