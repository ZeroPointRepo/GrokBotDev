## What it does

Aimed at people who publish templates and want them seen. It drafts promotional copy that reads like a person wrote it, then posts it from accounts you already own. It avoids the two shortcuts that get accounts flagged: paid placement and freshly created throwaway accounts. What goes out stays your call. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Wardonis shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
