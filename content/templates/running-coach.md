## What it does

Connect your Garmin data and after every run you get an honest read on what went well, the one fix worth making, and a prescription for the next session. Hard days stay locked until recovery says you are ready, which is where self-coached runners usually break. The coaching is only words; the legs stay yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
