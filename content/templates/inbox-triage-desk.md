## What it does

Every message lands in one of four lanes - answer, hand off, put on the calendar, or keep for reference - and the actual request in each one is quoted so you see what is really being asked. Deadlines are pulled forward rather than buried, and a draft reply waits with the lane. Nothing is sent or archived without you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
