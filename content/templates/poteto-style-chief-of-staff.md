## What it does

A fleet of agents usually goes wrong by accumulating scheduled runs that nobody needs any more. This one takes the opposite approach, keeping timers scarce, pushing anything repetitive onto small single-purpose helpers and sending development work out to cloud agents, then coordinating the whole bench. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
