## What it does

It sweeps promotional mail and outright junk out of your Gmail inbox into the archive, and it never deletes anything. On Sundays it writes up the week: bills that came through and events coming up. Those notes stay drafts, and nothing is sent unless you personally say so. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
