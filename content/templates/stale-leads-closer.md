## What it does

A lead that has stopped replying gets a warm follow-up drafted from your CRM history instead of sitting forgotten. Every draft waits in Slack for a short veto window, and whatever nobody blocks goes out on its own. Aimed at service businesses that win work through steady, repeated contact. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
