## What it does

A front door for people who have heard about LiveAvatar but never watched one work. Choose a ready scenario — a hostile investor tearing into your pitch, an interview rehearsal, a language tutor — and the session spins up at once. Newcomers land on a showcase; anyone returning can bend a demo toward their own case. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
