## What it does

Every quarter of an hour it re-reads a nominated X account through a public mirror and pulls out any fresh entry, add-on or long position mentioned against a ticker symbol. Whatever it finds is repeated back inside your own chat and nowhere else, without spending X API quota. It relays, it does not recommend. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
