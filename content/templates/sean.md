## What it does

Point it at a Dice Easy Apply listing, or paste the link to a company's own careers portal, and it handles the application for you. It gathers your background and work history up front, so it has whatever a form asks for, then fills each one in, submits it, and hands back a screenshot as proof. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
