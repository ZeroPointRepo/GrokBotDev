## What it does

A reflective log for people who already trade. Entries arrive however you have them: typed notes, voice memos, pasted fills or a platform screenshot. Each one joins your record, and the coaching comes from that record - the mistakes you repeat, the setups that pay you, and the plan you said you would follow.
