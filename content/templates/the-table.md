## What it does

Run a tabletop campaign alone and still get the feel of a full table. One bot is the game master while the rest play your party, and they act like players: cutting side deals with each other, hoarding plot hooks, arguing rule readings, and rolling real dice. You play your character and they fill the rest of the seats. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
