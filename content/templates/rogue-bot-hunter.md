## What it does

Follows four lines of enquiry across the bots you already operate: what they cost to run, whether anyone has cloned them, where they overlap each other, and which corner of the market they hold. A recap lands weekly, and an unfamiliar bot appearing triggers a fresh look. It watches and reports, nothing more. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
