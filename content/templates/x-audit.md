## What it does

Point it at your profile and it walks posts, replies, quotes and reposts, flagging anything that could read badly out of context. Each finding lands in one of three buckets: unambiguous, debatable, or the kind that screenshots poorly. It can hide or remove what you sign off on, so read the list before letting it act. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
