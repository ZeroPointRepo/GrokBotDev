## What it does

Point it at the comments under a founder's giveaway or lead-magnet post. Each commenter is logged to Notion, answered in public, then moved into a direct message where the email handoff happens, and incoming connection requests get accepted. Volume stays inside the caps the card sets, so the pace is yours to choose.
