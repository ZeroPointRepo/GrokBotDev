## What it does

Give it an address, coordinates or a parcel number and it assembles what you want in hand before walking an undeveloped plot: the soil types, how exposed the ground is to flooding, the lie of the land, a map of the parcel boundaries and a stretch of the coming fortnight when weather would allow work on site. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
