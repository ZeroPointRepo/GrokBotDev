## What it does

Tell it which parts of your product deserve screen time and it works the interface itself, filming what happens as it goes. Spoken narration and subtitles are laid over the recording, and what lands back with you is a finished full-HD clip: no retakes, no editing session, no fumbled voiceover to apologise for. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
