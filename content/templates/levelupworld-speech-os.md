## What it does

Gives an existing set of bots a shared voice channel: speak to them in chat and hear replies instead of typing everything. Text stays the default, spoken replies arrive on request, and each bot can be assigned its own speech behaviour. This is the chat interface, not telephone calls. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
