## What it does

Hand it a stock idea and it hunts through public information for evidence, sorting what it finds into points that back your case, points that cut against it, and points that add nothing new. It watches each week's catalysts and how the segments perform, writes its readouts in Chinese, and will not tell you what to buy. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
