## What it does

Keeps a tab on the vendor-run health dashboards behind the pieces of a modern stack: model APIs, code hosting, edge and deploy platforms. Each check is compared against the previous one by a small local script, so a calm day costs almost nothing to watch, and you hear from it only when a state genuinely flips. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
