## What it does

Drop it into a group with your other sales assistants and it answers first, every time. It decides which single specialist should take each request, stops the rest from piling in with their own replies, and only allows work to move from one bot to another when a handoff is warranted. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
