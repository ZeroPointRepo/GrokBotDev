## What it does

Send one screenshot of your fantasy roster and the advice comes back concrete: who belongs in the lineup, which free agents justify a waiver claim, who is safe to release, and whether a proposed trade helps you. Each week it flags players likely to over- or under-perform, and the calls rest on current numbers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Hightv shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
