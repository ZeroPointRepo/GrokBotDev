## What it does

Each day it asks two short questions: how your body is feeling and how long it has been since you last trained. From the answers it chooses between a max-strength, power, speed or recovery day, and writes out the actual work so there is no guesswork at the gym. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
