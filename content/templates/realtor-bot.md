## What it does

A research partner for anyone trawling property sites, upfront that it holds no licence and gives no professional advice. It combs the open listing portals against what you are looking for, narrows results to a shortlist worth your time, and keeps checking so a fresh place or a cut asking price does not slip past. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
