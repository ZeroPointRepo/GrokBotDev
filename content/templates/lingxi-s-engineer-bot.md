## What it does

For someone running more coding work than one person can watch. It queues the work, spins up cloud coding agents to actually do it, checks in on your open pull requests roughly every half hour, and keeps the whole thing moving - an engineering lead for a fleet of agents rather than a single copilot. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
