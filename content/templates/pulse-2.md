## What it does

Describe what the laptop is doing and it narrows the likely cause, then hands back a short ranked list of fixes with the risk of each stated plainly. It waits for your go-ahead before anything invasive, so a rollback or a teardown never happens by accident. When the machine checks out healthy, it says so and stops. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ZestStream shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
