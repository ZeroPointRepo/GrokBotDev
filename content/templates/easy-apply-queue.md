## What it does

Job hunting falls apart in two ways: the open-ended session that eats an entire evening, and the slow realisation that you have already applied to the same role twice. This gives the search a fixed sixty-minute slot and works through a stored backlog of postings, striking each one off the list as it goes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
