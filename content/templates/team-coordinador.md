## What it does

A Spanish-language coordinator for a Grok team of specialists. Work is assigned so nobody strays into a teammate's area, one vault is kept for the whole team to read from, and the week closes with a Sunday digest. No personal data is stored, and the vault plus webhook inbox are set at import. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
