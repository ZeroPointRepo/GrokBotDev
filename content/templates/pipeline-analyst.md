## What it does

Scrubs the Salesforce and spreadsheet data, flags the deals that have stalled and the commits at risk, then opens Monday with a scoreboard that gives the week an honest pipeline view to start from. Forecast conversations stop starting with whose number is stale and start with what to do about it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
