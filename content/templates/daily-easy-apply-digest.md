## What it does

Two job boards, hundreds of postings, and most of them wrong for you. Each morning this gathers the mid-level backend openings that take a one-click application, reads every one of them against your own resume, and ranks them by how well they genuinely fit. The day starts with a shortlist, not a search. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
