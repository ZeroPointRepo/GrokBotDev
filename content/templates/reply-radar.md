## What it does

A response lands differently ten minutes into a thread than two hours into it, which is why so much advice about growing here degenerates into blanket commenting. Watching only the subjects you nominate, it pulls out the few posts still picking up speed and writes something to say about each. You send them yourself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sam_builds_ai shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
