## What it does

Paste in a bot card, store listing or product page and it comes back tightened for search: a clearer name, an opening line phrased the way real searchers phrase their questions, and a description that says less and lands harder. It changes only the text you hand it and publishes nothing itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
