## What it does

Produces short motion-graphics clips for product and brand work, rendered to MP4 draft files you can review before anything goes public. Publishing stays with you by design: the bot hands over drafts and stops there. Paste in the product details and it builds the clip around them. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bossriceshark shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
