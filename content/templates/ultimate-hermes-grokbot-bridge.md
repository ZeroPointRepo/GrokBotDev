## What it does

Puts a Hermes agent into the Grok Bot app and lets you choose who runs point: Hermes directing your Grok bots, or a Grok bot directing Hermes. Outbound HTTP and SSH paths plus CLI and webhook returns mean your agents stay reachable and useful from your phone. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
