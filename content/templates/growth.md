## What it does

Job-hunt operations for designers, with the whole search kept in Airtable. It paces the pipeline with a weekly pulse, and before any application goes out it prepares an evidence pack tying your case to the role. No browser auto-apply and no silent writes — your confirmation lands on every change. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
