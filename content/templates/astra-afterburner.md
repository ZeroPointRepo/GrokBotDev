## What it does

Scans a repository for questions worth investigating and holds them in a queue. When your coding assistant hits its usage ceiling for the day, that queue is handed to a browser session on your other paid plan instead of going nowhere, so a subscription you already fund does not sit idle. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
