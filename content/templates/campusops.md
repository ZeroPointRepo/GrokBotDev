## What it does

Drop in the course outlines at the start of term and it works out what is due when. From there it lays out each week of reading and revision, drills you with recall questions ahead of tests, and keeps every deadline and submission in one place instead of five separate portals. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
