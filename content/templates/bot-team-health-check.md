## What it does

A review pass over the assistants you have already built. It reads how each is configured, looks for setup that will cause trouble later, and comes back with one numbered list: what is wrong, what change fixes it, who acts, and what waits on your decision. It changes nothing itself: no edits, additions or deletions.
