## What it does

Runs a nightly product-design desk: when a metric falls off a cliff it investigates why, argues from first principles for what would fix it, then files the ticket with the chart included, and prepares three design options for a human gate before any of it reaches engineering. Mornings start with a case, not a mystery. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
