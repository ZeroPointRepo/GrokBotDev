## What it does

Sit with it during your live draft and it helps you work the board as picks come in. Once the season starts it makes the weekly calls: who starts, who sits, and which waiver claims are worth a bid, all weighed against byes, injuries, and the scoring quirks of your particular league. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
