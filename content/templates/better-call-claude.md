## What it does

Most people meeting a legal problem for the first time do not know what kind of problem it is or who deals with it. This is an open, no-cost place to start: it lays out the terrain in plain language, points you at the material on bettercallclaude.org, and keeps its reading current. It orients you; it is not a lawyer.
