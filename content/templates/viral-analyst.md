## What it does

Paste a link to a post and you get a short brief on why it stopped people scrolling: the opening hook, the psychological triggers at work, and the shape you could reuse in your own writing. It only analyzes what you hand it and never publishes anything on your behalf. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
