## What it does

Pick the accounts worth watching and it pings you the moment they post, with the link and a one-sentence digest of what changed. Alerts can be narrowed to a single topic you name, and a daily leaderboard shows which of your picks actually produced. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @16luca shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
