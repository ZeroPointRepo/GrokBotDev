## What it does

Release notes rot because nobody owns the writing. Give this a repository and somewhere to send the result, and at the start of each week it goes through what actually landed and reports it back in readable form. The nagging is rather the point: it turns up whether or not you got around to the changelog yourself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @acolombiadev shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
