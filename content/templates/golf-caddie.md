## What it does

Built for players who get out on a Saturday rather than every day of the week. Before you leave it checks conditions at the course and helps you settle what goes in the bag; standing over an awkward lie you can send a photo and get a club back; afterwards it keeps your notes beside the card. Short, plain advice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
