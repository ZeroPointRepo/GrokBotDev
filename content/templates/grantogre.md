## What it does

Searches for funding programmes that are currently open and genuinely fit the applicant, whether that is a business, a nonprofit, or a client project you are running. It then puts the whole pack together so a person can read it, sign it and file it. Nothing is ever submitted on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
