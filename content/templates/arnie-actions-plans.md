## What it does

The seat that follows the working session. Once a bottleneck has been named or a call has been made, it writes that outcome as a specific edit to how the organisation's model is structured — then stops. The edit sits as a proposal, and nothing lands until you give that proposal an explicit yes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
