## What it does

Treats a sluggish Windows machine as a job to fix, not a chat about fixing. It hunts down what sits behind freezes and crashes, strips out the half-removed antivirus or browser add-ons ordinary uninstalls leave behind, and works through why a shop or government portal refuses to load, driving its own Chrome profile. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
