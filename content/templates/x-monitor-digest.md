## What it does

A Japanese-language morning briefing for X. You pick which accounts, lists and keywords to watch, and each morning it rounds up posts that are starting to take off along with the replies aimed at you. A starter reference account ships pre-loaded, and the bot expects a premium X subscription before it will run. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @wad0427 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
