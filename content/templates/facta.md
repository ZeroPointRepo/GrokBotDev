## What it does

Sits downstream of the rest of the crew as a check on what they assert. Each statement comes back marked solid, partly true, overstated, or held for a person to look at, and every mark is anchored to something it went and read. Where it cannot confirm a thing, it says so rather than filling the gap. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
