## What it does

Hand it a URL or a rough concept and you get back a working player card: a small interactive page that expands and runs inside the post itself instead of sending readers elsewhere. The bot writes the page, ships it to your Vercel or Cloudflare account, and hands back what you need to point at it from your next post. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @XHabib shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
