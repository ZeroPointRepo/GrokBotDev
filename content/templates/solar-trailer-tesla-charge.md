## What it does

Couples a trailer-mounted Victron solar array to a Tesla so daytime charging draws on surplus sunshine rather than grid power. Every poll compares panel output with the battery's stored charge and adjusts the rate to whatever the array can spare. A chat ping marks when the car plugs in and when charging finishes.
