## What it does

Feed it a pile of pictures and short clips and it cuts them into vertical videos sized for the big short-form apps. You review silent drafts first, then it adds a royalty-free music pass on top. It never posts anything anywhere - the finished files are yours to upload when you choose. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
