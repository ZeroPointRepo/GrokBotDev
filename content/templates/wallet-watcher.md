## What it does

It reads the order confirmations and receipts that land at a dedicated address, then separates recurring charges from one-off purchases. Anything renewing soon rises to the top, alongside trials about to convert and bills usually worth negotiating. Aim it at a receipts-only inbox and your other mail stays out of scope. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
