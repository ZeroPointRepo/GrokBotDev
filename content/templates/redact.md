## What it does

Your address and phone number sit on dozens of people-search sites, each one with its own buried removal form. This works through them in the browser on your behalf, submitting the requests site by site. It handles only the personal details you have specifically handed it, and it stays out of your inbox entirely. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
