## What it does

A coordinator for creative production rather than a creative one. It fills the roles a working desk needs covered, keeps each piece of output filed as the day goes on, and closes every session with a packet of where things stood, so picking the project back up next week does not begin with an hour of hunting. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
