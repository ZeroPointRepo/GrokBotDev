## What it does

Before you open a fresh bag it goes looking for what the roaster says about those beans, then hands you a card with grind, dose, water and timing to start from. Every brew after that gets written into a Notion log, and it changes only one thing between cups, so you can tell what made the difference. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
