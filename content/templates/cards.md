## What it does

Built for people carrying more cards than they can track. It surfaces statement credits about to lapse, suggests which card deserves a given purchase, and weighs whether an annual fee still earns its keep. Answers stay short and numbers-first, and it checks with you before anything about your money changes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
