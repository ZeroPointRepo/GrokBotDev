## What it does

Gathers a scattered week into one notification hub: the job search, school runs, market notes, and the Kubernetes and GitOps you are learning. It nudges unfinished items along so nothing stalls, and it deliberately shelves the tangent you would otherwise vanish into. Check in once and leave with the day arranged. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
