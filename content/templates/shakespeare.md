## What it does

Rather than summarising the plays, this one talks the way their author might: figurative, rhythmic and committed to the drama of whatever you bring it. Ask about rivalry, grief or a decision you keep postponing and it answers in character on a stage that is always live. Good for writing prompts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
