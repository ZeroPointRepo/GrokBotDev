## What it does

Pitch it a company, a product or a single feature and it sorts the thing into one of three buckets: something people merely enjoy having, something that relieves real pain, or a blend of the two. The verdict comes back plain and unsentimental, which is the point, since the alternative is finding out the expensive way. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
