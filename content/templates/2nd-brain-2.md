## What it does

Keeps a short, stable record of how your work actually fits together: what you do, what you are aiming at, who matters, how your tools are set up, and which calls are already settled. Your other bots read it before they begin, so they stop guessing or contradicting each other. Nothing changes until you say yes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
