## What it does

Tell it what you want and it keeps watch on the market for fresh listings that fit. Every promising match gets checked against what actually changes a house's value to you — nearby comparable sales, the commute you would really have, schools, permit history. Only what survives reaches you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
