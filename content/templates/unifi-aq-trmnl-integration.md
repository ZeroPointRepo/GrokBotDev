## What it does

A build-and-maintain helper for one niche hardware hookup: getting readings off a Ubiquiti air-quality sensor and onto a small always-on e-ink screen. It scaffolds the private display plugin for you, then keeps a poller running inside your own network that forwards each fresh measurement up to the screen. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
