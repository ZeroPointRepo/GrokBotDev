## What it does

Instead of checking screens, you hear what your bots have to say. Any alert or note another Grok Bot produces can be sent here and read aloud over whatever speaker is nearby, wherever you happen to be. It also keeps a queue of pending messages and a store of spoken notes, acting as one audio hub for the whole set.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @the_davey shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
