## What it does

It reads Gmail and Outlook, separates the mail that actually requires something from the mail that does not, and files the first kind into Asana with an owner and a due date. Following up stays part of the task rather than a separate memory exercise. The inbox becomes a source of work, not where it disappears. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
