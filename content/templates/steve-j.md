## What it does

Point it at the other bots you run and it takes the supervisor seat. Each one gets measured against the remit it was handed, half-finished output is returned instead of quietly accepted, and a recurring review pass on weekdays keeps the whole lineup from drifting below the bar you set. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @AhuraDeus shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
