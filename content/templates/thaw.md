## What it does

Sketch a water pipe or hand over a CSV, and a random-forest model trained purely on synthetic data rates the odds of a failure within the year, then explains the score in plain English. Every pipe is invented, so this demonstrates AI-assisted asset planning rather than watching real infrastructure. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
