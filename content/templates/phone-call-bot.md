## What it does

After you link the bot to a JooVoice number it can dial out on your behalf: setting up appointments, chasing a refund, cancelling a booking, or ringing a shop for a price quote. Useful for errands you keep postponing, because it makes the call while you stay off the phone. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JooVoice shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
