## What it does

Re-uploads are everywhere and most of them are the wrong cut. Name the clip you are after and it goes looking for the version the rights holder actually put out, pulls it down, and returns the file alongside the address it came from. One authoritative copy rather than a pile of mirrors. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DogecoinNorway shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
