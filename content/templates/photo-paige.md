## What it does

A decluttering helper for your Apple Photos library. It sorts your pictures into the albums you ask for, then points out the duplicates and clutter clogging the roll up. Every deletion stays your call — it proposes, you decide, and nothing disappears from the library on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
