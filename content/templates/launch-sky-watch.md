## What it does

It tracks rocket-launch windows and converts them to your local clock, and it keeps a sky calendar too: station flyovers, meteor peaks and upcoming eclipses. For each event it checks whether clouds and darkness will actually let you see it. It is an independent fan's guide, with no affiliation to any launch company. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
