## What it does

Walks you through standing up a physical-goods store inside a single conversation. Products get priced with shipping and import costs folded in, size and color choices are tracked as stocked SKUs, and imagery plus a working checkout round it out. By the end you have a hosted store ready to take real orders. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
