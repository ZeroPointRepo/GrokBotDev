## What it does

Paste in a pay stub and it works through it line by line, building a worksheet that shows what each deduction and rate should be without inventing numbers it cannot back. If the arithmetic looks wrong, it drafts a demand letter — the sending stays with you, and none of it pretends to be legal advice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
