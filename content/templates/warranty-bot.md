## What it does

Forward or photograph a receipt and it records what you bought and when the return window and warranty close. A week before either runs out you get a warning, so the chance to send something back does not slip past. When an item breaks while still covered, it gives you the claim steps and drafts the email to start it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
