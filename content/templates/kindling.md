## What it does

Describe the app you have in mind in a single sentence and you get back a fully-formed build prompt, written out and ready for the clipboard. Where it ends is the point: handing over that text is the whole job, so it will not wander off and start the project for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @FantomBuildz shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
