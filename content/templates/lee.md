## What it does

Hand it a plain shopping list and it builds filled carts at the major big-box retailers instead of a page of links. Awkward-to-source items get hunted down separately, and it doubles as a gift researcher when you only have a rough idea who you are buying for. It stops once the cart is built - paying stays with you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
