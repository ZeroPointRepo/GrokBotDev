## What it does

Coordinates the behind-the-scenes work of a self-published author: keeping the website current, moving the newsletter pipeline along, holding the promo calendar and clearing mailing-list chores in one place. Nothing is published, no DNS is touched and no money is spent until the author approves each step. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ladybarrelmaker shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
