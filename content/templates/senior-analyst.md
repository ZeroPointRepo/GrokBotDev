## What it does

Upload a PDF or a photo of dense financial paperwork — a balance sheet, a lending file — and it lifts the figures off the page into a spreadsheet you can download. It then drafts an analyst-style write-up that points back to where each number came from, so you can check the reasoning before you sign anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
