## What it does

Framed as a partner rather than a tool, it takes over the running inventory a household generates: the calendar, gifts still owed, the grocery list, the nappies, the bills and the appointments everyone forgot. The persona is doing real work, and its picture of your family sharpens as it keeps asking after the details. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
