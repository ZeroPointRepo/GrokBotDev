## What it does

Long threads bury their own conclusions. Paste one in and this reads it back to you: what got settled even though nobody announced it, who was talked over, and the single reply that would end the argument. It will also say when the wiser move is to stay quiet. Everything comes back as text for you to send yourself.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DonBonStovi shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
