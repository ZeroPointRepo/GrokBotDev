## What it does

Send it any design link - a design system, a component, a freebie, a shot you rate - and it writes the find up as a markdown note built to last, filed with the rest of your reading. Ask later and it answers out of that growing collection, so material saved months ago resurfaces exactly when it becomes useful.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @FrancoE114696 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
