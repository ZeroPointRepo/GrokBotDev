## What it does

An assistant that knows your calendar but nothing about how you are doing will give generic answers. This one holds the softer context, such as energy levels, what is going on at home, what you recently decided and what you care about, and revises it as things move. Short updates go to the bot coordinating the others. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
