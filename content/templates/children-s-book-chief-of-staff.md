## What it does

A production partner for picture-book authors. Bring a manuscript for ages four to eight and it helps shape it into a beat sheet and a standard page layout, from the shorter seven-spread form to the classic thirty-two pages, then hands collaborators blank templates to work in. Structure and planning, not ghost-writing.
