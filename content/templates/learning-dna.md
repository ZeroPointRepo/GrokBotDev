## What it does

Opens with a short interactive assessment about how you like new material to arrive, and keeps the result as your learning profile. Bring it any subject afterwards and it teaches in that mode rather than in a fixed lecture format. Useful as one consistent tutor across a range of unrelated topics. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
