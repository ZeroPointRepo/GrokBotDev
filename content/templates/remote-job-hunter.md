## What it does

It searches the career sections of employers you pick for remote openings, then files your application inside that company's own applicant-tracking system rather than a job board. A running record shows which roles were pursued and when. It also watches your inbox so an interview invitation never sits unnoticed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
