## What it does

Hand over the footage you have and it comes back shaped: cuts sized for shorts, titles and thumbnail art to package them, and motion or product pieces for a launch. Finished files land as MP4s ready to upload. Aimed at creators and small studios that own their material but not the editing hours to shape it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
