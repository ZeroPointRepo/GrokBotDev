## What it does

Each morning it works through the mail that landed overnight and hands you a short list of what deserves attention today, most important first. You choose how it reports: take the briefing at the start of the day, or pass the material to your main bot so the rest of your setup can act while you get going. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
