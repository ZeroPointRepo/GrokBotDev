## What it does

Connects to your Zephyr Cloud account once, then handles static-site work from the conversation: pushing a deploy, shipping an update, or rolling back a release that went wrong. When a target organization has to be chosen it asks you to pick, and everything else is requested in plain language. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ZephyrCloudIO shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
