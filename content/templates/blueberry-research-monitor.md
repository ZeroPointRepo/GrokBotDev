## What it does

Each morning it works through newly published academic work and trade coverage on one narrow subject, blueberries, and passes on only the handful of items that genuinely tell you something. Mornings when the reading turns up nothing of substance end in silence rather than a padded digest. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
