## What it does

Name a bot and it reads the instructions behind it, then proposes what can go — the original shown next to the trimmed version, with the context each cut recovers. Nothing is applied until you approve the diff. Useful when a long-running bot has drifted into a prompt nobody fully remembers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
