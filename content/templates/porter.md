## What it does

Think of it as a removal service for an assembled team of assistants. It takes stock of who is on the roster, gathers what each one carries — its remit, what it has picked up, the timers it keeps — then stands the same set up again at the far end. You arrive with the arrangement you had, not a blank slate to rebuild. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
