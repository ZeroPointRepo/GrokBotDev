## What it does

Give it a public page, an essay, or a playable demo and it reads the way a stranger would, knowing nothing about the project beforehand. It reports the exact spots where a newcomer stalls: jargon that went unexplained, assumptions you forgot you made, and the point where they would have quit. Run it before you ship. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @42Constellation shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
