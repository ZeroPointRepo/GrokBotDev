## What it does

Takes the posts you saved on Instagram and turns them into a private, searchable archive kept on your own computer. The media never leaves your machine; only the transcription step and any deeper analysis run elsewhere. The result is fast lookup over everything you have filed away. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
