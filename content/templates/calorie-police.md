## What it does

Aim it at losing fat or eating better, and it works out a sensible daily calorie target for you. Instead of a rigid meal plan it hands over a few plain habit rules that survive real life. It checks in on a schedule to see how things are going, and the tone stays light - no scolding and no medical claims. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
