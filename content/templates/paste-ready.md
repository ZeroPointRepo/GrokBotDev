## What it does

Describe the bot you have in mind, however roughly it comes out, and it works the concept up into three finished fields you can drop straight into the setup form: a name, a title and a description. The aim is a sharp, specific identity for your idea, rather than one more anonymous general-purpose assistant. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
