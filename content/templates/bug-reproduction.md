## What it does

Converts messy bug threads into repro packs an engineer can trust: it walks the same path the reporter took, in staging, captures the moment the failure actually fires, and packages the steps with the evidence attached. Debugging starts from a confirmed reproduction instead of a confusing screenshot and a shrug. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
