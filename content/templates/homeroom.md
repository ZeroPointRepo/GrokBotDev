## What it does

It reads the school platform through the family's own parent credentials, never a shared or scraped account, and maintains a running home page of what is due. Clubs, practices and collection times sit next to the homework, and it looks in across the school day so nothing lands as a surprise at bedtime. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ahalvor shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
