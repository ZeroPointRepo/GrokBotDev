## What it does

A delivery watchdog that sits over a connected inbox, following parcels and their order updates across several retailers. When a status looks ambiguous it asks you a clarifying question first; otherwise it says nothing until something genuinely needs your attention. Quiet by design, never another source of noise. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
