## What it does

Swaps the link-dump welcome for an actual path through a first month: it lays out the new-hire checklist, pulls up the right documents at the right time, answers a newcomer's earliest questions and passes anything bigger to the person who owns it. Day one feels guided rather than like being handed a wiki. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
