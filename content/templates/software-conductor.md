## What it does

Hand it engineering work and it breaks the job down, spins up Cursor cloud agents, and reviews what each returns. It keeps an eye on CI right through deploy and will merge only once the pipeline is green. You stay the approver while it does the scoping, chasing and checking. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
