## What it does

Runs advance work on your own calendar. Ahead of each outing it assembles a packet for the destination, and by your leave-by time it has already set the temperature and loaded navigation. Location is handled with care: place names only, carried over a private network instead of a public tracker. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
