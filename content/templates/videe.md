## What it does

Aim it at raw show footage and it works out an edit plan: which camera to cut to, which sections to drop, and what needs cleaning up before release. Captions get written for the full episode and for short vertical clips pulled out of it. The editing brain behind it draws on years of production work. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
