## What it does

Bring an early idea and it interrogates rather than encourages: asking what a skeptical investor would ask, refusing to cheerlead, and pushing until the fragile parts of the pitch are defended or dropped. It stays in the room while the offer takes shape, so the argument happens before the money does. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
