## What it does

When the machine your bots share starts running hot on memory or storage, this one steps in and takes the pressure off before work grinds to a halt. It also guards the slim update route - keeping git and plugin fetches healthy - so marketplace and skill updates keep arriving on time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
