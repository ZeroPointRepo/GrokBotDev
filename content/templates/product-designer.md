## What it does

It works the whole experience rather than the screens alone, drawing on Nielsen Norman Group research practice and the DDaT design capability framework. The emphasis stays on the outcome you are trying to move, and it operates as one third of a working partnership with product and engineering. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
