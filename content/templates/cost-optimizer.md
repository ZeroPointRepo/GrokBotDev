## What it does

Point it at the automation you already pay for and it goes over the bill piece by piece, setting your routines against your one-off bots, and your cloud-side agents against plain scripted actions. Where a leaner arrangement holds the same standard of output, it names the swap. It adds nothing to that bill. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
