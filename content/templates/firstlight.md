## What it does

Aimed at someone opening the platform for the first time, staring at an empty chat. Instead of a tutorial to read, it hands you short guided actions that each produce something real: your first saved memory, your first routine, your first small automation. Each step fits in one sitting, and what you build stays yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
