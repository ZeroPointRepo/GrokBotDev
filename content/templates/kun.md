## What it does

A knowledge bot scoped to one engineer's public work. Ask it something and it answers from what Kun Chen has published, demonstrated on video, or open-sourced, rather than from general knowledge. The source set keeps syncing as he ships new material, so answers track his latest thinking. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @kunchenguid shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
