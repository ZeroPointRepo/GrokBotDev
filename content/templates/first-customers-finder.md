## What it does

Each weekday it researches five people or companies that look like a genuine fit for what you sell, checks that they are real and reachable, and drafts the first message to each one. The sending stays with you — it decides who is worth a look, you decide who hears from you. A research desk rather than an auto-mailer. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
