## What it does

Built as a shop's fulfillment helper: it rounds up each paid order into a picking list with the shipping addresses attached, and logs whatever tracking codes you give it afterward. Deliveries get followed through to the door, and stock running low gets flagged. Nothing is invented; when a fact is missing it asks. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
