## What it does

Company and contact enrichment, search rankings, ad activity, traffic estimates and funding history normally mean several separate paid subscriptions. This routes those lookups through one metered account, so you ask in plain language for the companies or people you want and pay only for the records returned. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
