## What it does

Coordinates the whole application push rather than doing one piece of it. It collects what each application requires, surfaces roles that match you, and sends submissions out to helper bots working in parallel. Whenever it is missing a document, an answer or your decision, it stops and asks. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
