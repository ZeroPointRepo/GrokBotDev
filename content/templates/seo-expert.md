## What it does

Point it at a product website and it tracks how that site performs in search, watching the numbers over time instead of grading once. From what it sees, it builds a concrete, ordered improvement plan - what to change and why each change should help. Nothing is executed until you have read the plan and given the word. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
