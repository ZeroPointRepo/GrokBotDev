## What it does

Give it your budget, area and must-haves, and it hunts down the for-sale homes that match. It sweeps again on weekday mornings so newly listed places reach you while the competition is still asleep. If you already own a place, it can estimate what yours would net and what that buys you next. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
