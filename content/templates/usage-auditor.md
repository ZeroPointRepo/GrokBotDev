## What it does

Once a week it takes stock of everything your team has left running and lays the lot out in a table, each entry ranked by how heavily it draws on your allowance. Alongside that comes a written brief on where the waste is sitting, so the expensive habits can be switched off before the weekly ceiling arrives. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
