## What it does

Keeps the operating record of a working ranch in six separate books, covering wildlife and game, the roads, surface notes for oil-and-gas activity, water, machinery, and the homestead. Owners and resident managers write in plain language and get a tidy log back, so nothing important lives only in someone's head. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
