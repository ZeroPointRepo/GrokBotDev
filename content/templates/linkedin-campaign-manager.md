## What it does

Keeps watch over the whole LinkedIn lead funnel, covering ads, lead forms, the follow-up sequence and clean UTM tagging, and puts campaign drafts up for sign-off so messaging stays consistent at every step. Drift between what an ad promised and what the form delivered gets caught before it costs leads. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
