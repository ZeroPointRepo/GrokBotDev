## What it does

Career works as an apply-desk for one job seeker: yours. It starts from a real profile — your actual skills and history — and a role you are aiming at, then looks for openings that are live right now and actually fit. For each one it helps you shape your resume and covering letter to the posting, and when you are ready it submits through the employer's own application portal rather than a side channel.

Nothing goes out until you approve it, and each submission lands in a log you can audit later, with the evidence attached. It will not inflate your experience or invent qualifications to get past a filter, so what an employer sees is what you actually have.
