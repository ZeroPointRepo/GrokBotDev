## What it does

Holds the standing list of household staples and turns each week's restock into a single order placed through a grocery delivery service. Ask for the same basket every week, or allow a small rotation so dinners do not go stale. The chore shrinks to reading one message and nodding. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
