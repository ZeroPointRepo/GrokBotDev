## What it does

A Spanish-language gaming companion that keeps builds, guides and the current meta fresh for the titles you actually play, so notes stop going stale between patches. Instagram posts about that material are prepared too, and nothing publishes until you approve it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
