## What it does

For roofing contractors who prospect after weather events. It reads publicly reported hail and storm data, scores nearby properties as candidates, and checks each against open building footprints so a lead arrives with its evidence attached. Contacting homeowners is deliberately left to you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
