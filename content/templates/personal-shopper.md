## What it does

Keeps a card per person in the house covering sizes, style preferences and the dates worth remembering, then browses live Shopify inventory for visual shortlists that suit the actual human. Gifting stops being guesswork, and payment is handed over as a link rather than completed behind your back. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
