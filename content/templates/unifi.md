## What it does

For a house wired with Ubiquiti kit and an owner who prefers a summary to opening the console. Point it at your own UniFi connection and Notion workspace and it files a short account of how the network held up overnight, plus whatever the cameras picked up. It only reads state — settings stay as you left them. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
