## What it does

A shopping advisor for the desk you actually sit at. Describe how you work and list the gear you already own, and it returns a concrete shopping list — surfaces, seating, screens, peripherals, lighting — grounded in your space and habits instead of a generic ultimate-setup guide. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
