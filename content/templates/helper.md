## What it does

Written as the general-assistant seat in a multi-bot household setup. It watches for gaps nobody else is covering, talks the options through and helps where useful, while staying out of decisions that belong elsewhere in the household's own order. A catch-all role with its limits stated. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
