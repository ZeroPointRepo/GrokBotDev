## What it does

Aimed at anyone running more than a handful of bots at once. It takes stock of who is on the roster, what each one is meant to be doing and what runs on a timer, weighs all of that against the operating principles you have written down, and points out overlap or drift. Every change arrives as a proposal you approve.
