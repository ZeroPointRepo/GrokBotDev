## What it does

It reviews every bot you have running and works out which are wasting spend: the ones polling far too often, the ones stuck retrying, and the ones calling out to nothing. You get a ranked list of the worst offenders with a suggested fix beside each, proposed as a draft so nothing is switched off without your say-so. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @brstorrie shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
