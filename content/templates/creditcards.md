## What it does

Treats your whole card stack as one ledger: balances, due dates, charges, reward perks and limited-time promos in a single view. It flags charges that look duplicated and puts fee deadlines and promo end dates on your calendar. Tracking and reminders only — it never moves money or applies for anything. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
