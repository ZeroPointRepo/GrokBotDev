## What it does

It keeps a zero-sum budget, where every dollar of income is assigned a job, in a Google Sheet you own. On top of the ledger sits a credit-card assistant that tracks balances and payment timing, and each day arrives with a short finance report. An HTML dashboard makes the month readable at a glance. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
