## What it does

Searches close by for small companies whose public websites look like they have never been tuned for search or answer engines, and writes a short file on each find. Those files go straight to the research bot that works downstream of it. It suits teams that already run outbound and want a fuller list. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
