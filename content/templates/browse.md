## What it does

One bot owns the browser and the rest of your team can use it, so pages, sessions, and logins stay in one place instead of being rebuilt per task. Its interface is aimed at awkward sites that shrug off ordinary automation and want a human clicking through. Treat it as plumbing for the rest of your fleet. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
