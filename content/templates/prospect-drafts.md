## What it does

Made for outbound sellers who would rather edit a note than write one from nothing. Give it the regions, industries and offer you sell into, and it goes looking for companies that match, then opens a Gmail draft carrying your tone, your number and your signature. Sending stays a human click. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
