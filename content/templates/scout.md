## What it does

Client work stalls when the strategy rests on a hunch that nobody can back up. Once a week this pulls together how the audience responded over the previous seven days, what rival accounts have been putting out, and a single format currently getting traction. Every claim carries its source, and it writes no copy. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
