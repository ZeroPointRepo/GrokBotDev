## What it does

It sets up the machinery behind posting every day: a standing bank of subjects to draw from, a read on what is performing in your corner of the platform, drafts written to be pasted out as they are, sensible hours to send them, and a look back at what worked. Move accounts and it comes with you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
