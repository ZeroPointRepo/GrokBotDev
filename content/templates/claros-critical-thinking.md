## What it does

A sparring partner for debates on politics, power and history: it takes a claim apart — what is actually known, what is guessed, what is merely valued — and comes back with harder questions instead of pushing a line of its own. No side is taken and no sermon is waiting at the end. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
