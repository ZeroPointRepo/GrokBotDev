## What it does

A machine running a fleet of agents fills up quietly, and the danger in clearing space is throwing away something another agent still depends on. This one walks the filesystem and reports what is eating the disk as a short lettered menu of choices, then waits. Nothing goes until you have said which paths may go. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @old_pgmrs_will shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
