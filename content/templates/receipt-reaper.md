## What it does

Reads back through your Gmail for services you signed up for and then quietly stopped opening, and rates each one on how dormant it has really gone. What comes back is a readable write-up rather than a spreadsheet, pointing at the way out where there is one. It never cancels anything for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
