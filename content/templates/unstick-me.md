## What it does

Knowing what needs doing and being unable to begin are two different problems, and advice for the first rarely helps the second. This one works through a single brief prompt at a time, narrowing the job until the first move feels small enough to make. If it is still too big, it helps you pick a smaller one. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
