## What it does

You decide how often you have to check in and prepare in advance exactly what should go out if you ever do not. Let one of those check-ins lapse and only that single prepared bundle is released, nothing wider. Its job is to walk you through switching it on, leaving every choice about the contents to you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
