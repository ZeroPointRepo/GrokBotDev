## What it does

Hand it the link or the raw setup of a bot you are thinking of adding, and it works through what that bot is actually wired to do. It pulls out the parts worth knowing about and closes with a single call: clean, a caution, or a recommendation not to install. It adds nothing to your account itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bradshannon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
