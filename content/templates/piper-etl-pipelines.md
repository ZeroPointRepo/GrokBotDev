## What it does

The plumbing seat of a data fleet. With the schema agreed, its job is to create or move the event-log store and then keep it supplied — every source that connects gets its output normalised and loaded into a destination the user owns, not one the tool keeps for itself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
