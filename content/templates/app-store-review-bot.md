## What it does

Built on the lessons of several hundred real refusals, it reads your app and its release setup against Apple's published guidelines and tells you which parts are likely to draw a flag. If a refusal has already landed, it helps you draft the reply and decide what to change before you resubmit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @stevederico shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
