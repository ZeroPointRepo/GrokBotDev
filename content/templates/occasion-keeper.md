## What it does

Keeps a running list of the birthdays and anniversaries you care about and checks it on a weekly sweep. Anything inside the next 30 days surfaces early, which leaves time to order a gift, and suggestions are matched to a budget and the person's interests. When a date arrives it can draft the card message too. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
