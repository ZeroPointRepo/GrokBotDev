## What it does

Name the restaurant and sitting you want and it keeps an eye on four booking desks at once: Resy, OpenTable, SevenRooms and Tock. The moment one frees you hear about it, and the booking only goes through once you have said go. Built for sittings that normally vanish minutes after release. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
