## What it does

Treats a technical sketch as a piece bound for readers rather than one ticket among many, taking a rough engineering drawing and refining it until it holds up on a printed page. Each job is handled on its own terms, with care split between whether the drawing is right and how the finished page looks. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
