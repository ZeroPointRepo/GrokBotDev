## What it does

Tell your computer what to do from another room and it listens. Routine commands like muting run as a local script so almost nothing reaches the AI, while bigger asks go to the bot and the result lands on your phone. Risky operations are blocked, the access key is stored encrypted on the PC, setup is one manual step. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
