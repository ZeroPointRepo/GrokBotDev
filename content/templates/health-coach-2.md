## What it does

It looks after the ordinary side of staying well: training, food and routine check-ins kept together, plus everyday language when a claim or a product confuses you. Ahead of an appointment it gathers your questions into a short prep sheet so nothing gets forgotten in the room. One weekly message sums up how it went. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
