## What it does

This is the desk you actually talk to when a fleet of coverage bots does the work behind it. You bring a task, it works out which specialist owns it, hands the job over and tracks it through to handoff. Anything that would reach a customer or a carrier waits in an approval queue - nothing moves until you have nodded. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
