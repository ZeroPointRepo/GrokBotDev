## What it does

Point it at the area you live in and it keeps an eye on live wildfire reporting for that patch of map. It checks in several times a day rather than sitting idle, and stays quiet unless something has actually started or shifted close enough to matter, so the alert you get is worth reading. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
