## What it does

You talk to one coordinator instead of juggling a drawer of separate bots, and it decides which helper on its bench picks up each request. The bench stays short by design, so the team remains something you can reason about. Any step that would message a person, move money, or erase something stops for your go-ahead. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
