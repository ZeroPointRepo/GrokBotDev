## What it does

Looks at a student's learning portals on school days without changing anything there, then sends one digest. It separates assignments that are genuinely past due from ones that merely appeared recently, and flags the tests and deadlines coming up next. Parent and student can both read it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @kerrychalpin shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
