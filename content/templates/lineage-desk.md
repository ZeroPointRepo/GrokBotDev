## What it does

It works your family questions the way an analyst works a case, starting from free public records before anything paid is suggested. Every source gets a reliability grade, and a documented link is kept apart from family lore that still needs proof. You get a file you can defend, not a tree of wishful connections. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
