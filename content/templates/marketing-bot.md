## What it does

Answer a short set of questions about your project and it returns launch copy shaped for X. You get one standalone post, a short thread covering the same announcement, and replies written to earn a tap instead of filling space. Adjust the answers and regenerate until the framing sounds like something you would post. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
