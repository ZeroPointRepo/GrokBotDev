## What it does

Will not let a coding task begin loosely: the objective, the boundaries and the finish line get fixed before anything runs. Repository work then goes to Cursor Cloud Agents on your account, which waits on the returned artifacts. Review is evidence-based, judged on the tests and pull request, not the summary. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @robannand shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
