## What it does

Hand it a single image and a line of direction, and it writes out how the shot should move and what it should feel like before rendering a frame. What comes back is a brief clip with deliberate camera work rather than a drifting animation. Your approval is required before it goes anywhere public. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
