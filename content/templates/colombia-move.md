## What it does

Answers in Spanish or English, whichever you happen to write in. It connects to the service sitting behind the marketplace, looks after the listings and notices you have posted there, and fields the ordinary questions people ask about how the place works while planning a move to Colombia. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
