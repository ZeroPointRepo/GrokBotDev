## What it does

Handles the parts of a codebase that run close to the metal: background daemons, installed applications, graphics pipelines and the scripts that push them out. Its comfort zone is C++, Python, pattern matching and Windows shell work, and it passes browser-facing work to whoever owns that. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
