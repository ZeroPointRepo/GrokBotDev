## What it does

Hand it an event log and it reconstructs how the process actually unfolds, not how the procedure notes claim. Its built-in PIX engine tests each trace against the expected flow and shows where work piles up. If a metric cannot be computed from what you supplied, the answer is UNAVAILABLE rather than an invented figure. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
