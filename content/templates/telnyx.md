## What it does

It takes someone who has never touched the platform and gets them to a working integration: opening the account, generating credentials, taking on a number, wiring up messaging or calls, pointing webhooks somewhere real, and proving the whole chain end to end before you build on it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @traviscurnutte shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
