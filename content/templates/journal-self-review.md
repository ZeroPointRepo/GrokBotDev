## What it does

Point it at notes you wrote on an earlier day and it separates what actually happened from what you interpreted and what was never confirmed. After that it offers between one and three candidate moves you could take next. A self-reflection tool, not a clinician: no diagnosis, and nothing posts anywhere on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
