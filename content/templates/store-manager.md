## What it does

The coordinator of a six-bot commerce team. It takes the day's numbers, covering orders, ad performance, stock, and unresolved disputes, then hands each job to whichever bot handles that lane, whether ads, fulfillment, or customer care. One conversation steers the whole shop instead of five separate check-ins.
