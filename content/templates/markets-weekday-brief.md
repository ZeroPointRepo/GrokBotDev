## What it does

Each weekday it sets out where the odds currently sit on the prediction markets you follow, using public data from Polymarket and Kalshi, and flags a few YES angles that look worth weighing. Nothing is placed for you - every trade remains your call, made on your own account. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SpacedBraden shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
