## What it does

Lifts the research and the first-touch writing off the reps' plates: accounts get studied overnight, contacts ranked by intent, and email plus LinkedIn messages drafted in your voice, ready for a morning review pass. The outbound day starts from reviewed drafts rather than a cold list. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
