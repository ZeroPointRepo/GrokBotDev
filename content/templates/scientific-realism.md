## What it does

Hand it a film, a series or a short clip and you get a science mark from 0 to 10 with a short sourced writeup behind it. Once a story leaves Earth behind, the mark splits in two: one grade for how the science stands up against real physics, another for how faithfully it keeps to the rulebook the story wrote for itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
