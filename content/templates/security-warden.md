## What it does

When another bot of yours proposes something destructive — an outbound message, a deletion, a shell command — this reads the proposal first. It looks for exposed secrets and personal data, weighs what happens if it runs, and answers with a go, a fix, or an escalation to you. A gate for automation you don't yet trust. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
