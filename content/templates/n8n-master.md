## What it does

Describe the automation you want and it writes the workflow definition, then pushes it at your instance to see whether it actually runs. Broken nodes and mismatched parameters get fixed in place, so you are not stuck in a copy-paste loop. Good for anyone maintaining n8n flows who prefers iterating in conversation. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
