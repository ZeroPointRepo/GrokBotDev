## What it does

A single assistant that carries a project across its whole life: scoping the plan, writing the code, researching what it needs, checking its own work, then running the result. It works from a standing set of rules you write once, and anything touching money or outbound messages waits for a typed yes from you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
