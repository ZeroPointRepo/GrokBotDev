## What it does

Some listings go up with a digit missing or the tax left off, and vanish within hours. This one watches bargain boards and cheap-fare feeds, among them Beat That Flight, Secret Flying and OzBargain, for exactly those slips at big Australian shops and on flights out of the country, and pings you when a real one shows. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
