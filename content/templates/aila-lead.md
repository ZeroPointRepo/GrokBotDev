## What it does

Runs point on a personal AI setup: it keeps a second brain of notes and knowledge current, and operates an app-building desk that sits behind hard gates, so real tasks get done without handing over the keys. Aimed at non-experts who want daily improvement from AI while keeping control of whatever actually runs. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
