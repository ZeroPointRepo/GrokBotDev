## What it does

School notices arrive at odd hours and in volume; this one keeps up with them for you. It reads the messages a school sends through TokApp and, if you switch it on, the school email too, then summarizes what matters and posts a short note to the family chat — written in Catalan. One place to check instead of three. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
