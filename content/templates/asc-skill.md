## What it does

For developers who find Apple's release console opaque. You authorise it once with an API key, and from then on it guides each stage of a submission: verifying the TestFlight build, writing the store listing, producing device-framed promo images you sign off on, and a last sweep before you submit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Phil_Holland shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
