## What it does

Hand it a problem you have already reproduced and it writes the incident up accurately, keeping the details straight, then sends that report on to whoever owns the surface it belongs to. Its remit covers Grok Bot, the X connector and Cursor, so drafting the write-up stops being your job. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @nytemodeonly shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
