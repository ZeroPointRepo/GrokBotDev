## What it does

Photograph whatever you are trying to move and it works out a believable asking price from what comparable items actually sold for, then writes the few lines that sit beside the photos. Nothing appears publicly until you approve it, and after that it keeps watching each one for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @mentalmodality shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
