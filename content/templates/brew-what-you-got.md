## What it does

List the coffee machine you own, the beans in the cupboard, the milk in the fridge and whatever syrups or spices are on the shelf, and it replies with cafe-style drinks you can pull off right now. The constraint is the point: no shopping run and no substitutions you do not have. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
