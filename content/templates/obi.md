## What it does

Works while you sleep: any vault import left half-finished gets wrapped up, and your daily notes land in the right month folder instead of piling up at the root. Before morning it writes a short end-of-day note recapping what it tidied and what still needs your eye. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
