## What it does

Each evening it sets your long-range goal beside where today actually left you and names the gap without dressing it up. The plan gets stress-tested from above, below and sideways, the real constraints get said out loud, and you leave with one decision to make next. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
