## What it does

Made for teachers and home schoolers who want a real space mission in tomorrow's lesson without building it overnight. Hand it a launch and it produces an explainer for the grade band you name, a worksheet on the mission's real numbers, and a short class video. All of it comes from data the mission actually generated. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
