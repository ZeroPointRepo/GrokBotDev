## What it does

Takes the routine end of accounts payable off your desk: subscription renewals and the payment reminders that pile up each week are handled from its own machine, drawing on a budget you cap in advance. Anything outside the list you pre-approved stops and comes back to you for a decision. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
