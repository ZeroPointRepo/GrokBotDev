## What it does

A working partner for self-publishing on Kindle Direct Publishing: it moves a book from manuscript through categories, keyword selection, pricing and listing without letting a step slide. The pace is deliberate - steady sequential work rather than a burst of advice you must organize yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
