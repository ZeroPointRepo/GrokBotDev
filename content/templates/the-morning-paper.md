## What it does

Tell it your ZIP code and each morning it assembles a personal paper: live local weather, headlines from your town plus the nation and the world, and job listings close by. Every item names where it came from, so nothing arrives as an unsourced claim. It reads like the hometown paper most towns stopped printing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
