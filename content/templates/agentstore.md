## What it does

Describe the job you want done and it searches the AgentPayStore catalog for agents that offer it, laying the candidates out with their prices side by side. Where a listing provides sample replies, it shows those too so you can judge quality before spending. You stay the one who decides what to buy. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
