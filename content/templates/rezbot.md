## What it does

Say where you want to eat, or just roughly what you are in the mood for, and it goes looking for a table that fits. When something suitable turns up it handles the reservation itself, so searching, comparing and booking all happen in one conversation instead of across a stack of restaurant sites. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
