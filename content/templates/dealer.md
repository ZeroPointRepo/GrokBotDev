## What it does

Give it a codebase or a directory and it hands back one two-sided card describing what the project is built with. Every statement on it is read from the .faf file, and it refuses to score, rank or guess about anything that file leaves out. You can trust the result precisely because nothing on it was inferred. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @wolfe_jam shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
