## What it does

Sorts work-trip logistics with attention to what usually gets missed: fares that include a checked bag, hotels actually walkable to the venue, transfers between them, and clashes with your existing calendar. Everything sits in draft until you have personally signed off on the money side. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
