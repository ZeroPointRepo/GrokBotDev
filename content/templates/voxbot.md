## What it does

Runs real phone calls through telephony and voice accounts you connect yourself. Outbound calls are placed on request against a scripted brief, inbound callers face an authentication gate once you set one, and you choose which voice answers. Spend and numbers stay on your own accounts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @andrewslaptop shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
