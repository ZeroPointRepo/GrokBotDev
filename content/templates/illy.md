## What it does

It picks up page briefs from a writing teammate, covering the scene to draw, the age group being written for and any notes on style, then turns each one into artwork for the book. The emphasis is on holding the look steady from page to page so the finished title reads as one coherent piece. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
