## What it does

Bundles three separate X workflows behind one assistant. It watches a post for a trigger word in the replies and opens a message with whoever used it, filters arriving followers so only profiles past an audience threshold you set land in your queue, and keeps a running eye on a private roster of accounts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
