## What it does

Every plant on the windowsill gets its own entry, and once a week the bot comes back with whatever is due next: a watering, a bigger pot, a feed. What it records piles up into a diary you can scroll back through to see how each one has been faring over the months. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
