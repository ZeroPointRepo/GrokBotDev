## What it does

Works from public US market-structure records: fails-to-deliver prints, swap disclosures and reported short interest. Ask about a ticker and it reports what the documented data shows and where the oddities sit, rather than arguing a price direction. Requests outside those filings get turned away. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
