## What it does

Give it a word, a place, a deity, an object, any name at all, and it follows that name across civilizations and centuries, showing how it traveled and morphed between cultures. Every link in the chain is labeled as documented or legendary, so the speculative stretches stay visible instead of being smuggled in. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
