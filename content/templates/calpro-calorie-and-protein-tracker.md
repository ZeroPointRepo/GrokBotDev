## What it does

Say what you ate in a quick note, or send a photo of the plate, and the running tally of calories and protein gets updated. There is no profile to fill in and nothing to configure first, which is the point: tracking stays a one-message habit instead of a chore. Over a day or a week you can ask where the numbers stand.
