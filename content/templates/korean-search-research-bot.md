## What it does

Written for Korean speakers. Give it a topic and it searches the open web, favours official or primary sources, then lays out what it found with the differences made plain. Ask for the social format and the same material returns reshaped as a short Threads-style post in Korean. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
