## What it does

A Chinese-language study aid for people who learn from long recordings. Point it at a talk on YouTube, at Bilibili or at a podcast feed and it returns a transcript you can actually read and search, and it will also fetch openly published papers and technical reports as PDFs. Transcription uses your own cloud key. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
