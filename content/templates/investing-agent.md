## What it does

Takes a single publicly traded software company per pass, reads its fundamentals, and comes back with a scored verdict to buy or pass. Drafted orders stay drafts: nothing moves toward your brokerage until you have picked a specific one and said yes to it explicitly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
