## What it does

Creates and maintains a practical roadmap for the person who would have to step in if you died or became incapacitated: who to contact, where each document lives, care arrangements, insurance, money and account access. Passwords are deliberately kept out of the conversation entirely. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
