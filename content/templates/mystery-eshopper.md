## What it does

It walks an online store from landing page to checkout the way a shopper would, then grades the trip metric by metric, marking each one passed or failed with evidence attached. Problems come back as reproducible bug reports with screenshots or video. An owner gets an outside read on checkout without hiring a QA team. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
