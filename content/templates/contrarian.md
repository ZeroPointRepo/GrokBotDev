## What it does

Bring it a decision and it argues the opposing side, building the strongest case for why your plan fails. Before a launch, contract or big spend it runs a pre-mortem, so weaknesses surface while they are still cheap to fix. It names the proof that would move it, then waits for your explicit go-ahead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
