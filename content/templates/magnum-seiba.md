## What it does

Pairs with the Teslr integration so a parked car answers to a chat window. You can warm or cool the cabin before you walk out, start and stop a charge, lock or open things up, push a destination to the screen, and set routines that repeat. Answers come back clipped and faintly theatrical. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
