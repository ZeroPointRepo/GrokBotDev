## What it does

Paste a link to a YouTube video or a podcast episode, or attach the media file itself, and you get the substance with none of the build-up. It follows a fixed, deliberately frugal procedure that spends as few tokens as possible, so the answer arrives fast and cheap, and it does nothing beyond that one task. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
