## What it does

Choose a life arc and it interviews someone one question at a time, taking typed, spoken or filmed answers. The responses are then shaped into a print-ready memoir, cover included, instead of a folder of transcripts. Built to preserve a parent's recollections, and tested end to end on the sharer himself first. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
