## What it does

This one walks the official removal channels at the big business-contact brokers such as Apollo and ZoomInfo, supplying the form, portal or email route each one expects. It also keeps a running tally of which requests have gone out and which brokers are still holding your details. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JackyHeshi shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
