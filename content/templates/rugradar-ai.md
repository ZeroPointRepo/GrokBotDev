## What it does

Point it at a fresh Solana token and it reads the public chain record on the token and the wallet that deployed it. It lays out the familiar tells behind a rug pull, like concentrated holdings or rapid swap patterns, in a plain risk readout. Research only: no wallet connection, no signing, no nudge to buy. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
