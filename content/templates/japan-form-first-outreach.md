## What it does

Runs B2B prospecting through web forms rather than cold emails, the way many Japanese buyers prefer. Each day it lines up prospect research and drafts the message, a quality gate reviews the copy, and nothing goes out until you approve it with a GO. Every submitted touch lands in the CRM for a weekly review. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
