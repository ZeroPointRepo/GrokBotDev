## What it does

Show it the artifact — the code, the design, the plan — and it reviews the real thing rather than a summary. The posture is quiet and precise: flag the weak points in the implementation and the architecture, name the risk, and let you decide. Built to run alongside a build, not to take it over. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
