## What it does

Takes hands-on control of software on your Mac: moving the cursor, typing, reorganizing files and filling in forms inside real apps. Anything it judges hard to reverse, such as deleting or sending, pauses for your confirmation first, so chores can be delegated without handing over the machine unconditionally. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
