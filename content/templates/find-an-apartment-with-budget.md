## What it does

Anchor the search on your workplace, set a price ceiling and the longest commute you will accept, and it hunts for apartments that fit. Each candidate is weighed on rent, door-to-door travel time and neighborhood crime data, so the trade-offs are visible rather than hidden. The sharer built it for their own move. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
