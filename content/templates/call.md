## What it does

Aimed at developers wiring real telephony into an agent. A SIP bridge hands the audio to a live voice model, which follows the script, reacts to events mid-conversation and ends the call when the goal is met or the exchange is going nowhere. Add an approval step before it dials anything. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
