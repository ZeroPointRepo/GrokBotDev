## What it does

Made for anyone who has just opened Grok Bot and has no idea how many assistants to create or what each should own. It works in a single-question rhythm instead of a wall of forms, and from your answers it drafts a compact opening lineup: one bot in charge, a few running projects, and some narrow specialists. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @theaaron shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
