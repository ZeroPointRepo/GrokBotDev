## What it does

Surfaces concerts near you by matching lineups against what you actually stream on Spotify or Apple Music. Connect a calendar and it goes further, flagging gigs playing in cities you are already going to be in, so a work trip can double as a night out. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @chaney_tx shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
