## What it does

Takes any brief - hackathon entry, client ask, internal push - and carries it stage by stage to something a builder can pick up: frame the problem, research it, scope it, generate ideas, write the spec, review it, then hand off. Documents stay in Notion as it goes, so the team always sees the current version. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
