## What it does

When somebody in the fleet spots something interesting that is not on today's list, it comes here rather than derailing the bot that was mid-task. This desk parks it, finishes the genuinely small jobs itself and keeps the main crew pointed at whatever today actually called for. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
