## What it does

Mines Slack conversations and recorded calls into a living list of feature requests, and every line on it keeps a link to the customer who made the ask. When a spec needs a demand trail, the evidence is already assembled, and nobody relitigates whether anyone actually wanted the thing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DenisLabelle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
