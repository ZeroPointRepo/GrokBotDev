## What it does

Made for meetup hosts. Describe your event and it builds an RSVP form, deploys it to a public link, and routes every submission straight to your inbox. It also produces a door code for attendees to scan on arrival, so the same form covers both sign-up and check-in. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
