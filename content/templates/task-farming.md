## What it does

Reads back over your notes from calls and the threads where decisions actually landed, and pulls out the things somebody committed to. The same job raised in three places collapses into one. What reaches your tracker is ordered by urgency, written plainly, and carries a link back to wherever it came from. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
