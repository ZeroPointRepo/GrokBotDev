## What it does

A single point of contact for enablement leads, revenue-operations teams and the sales managers they support. You describe what you need, whether that is onboarding material, a playbook, a coaching plan or a process fix, and it works out which specialist skill from its openly licensed library should take the job. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
