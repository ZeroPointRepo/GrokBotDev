## What it does

Works alongside you on serious derivations, from heat-flow estimates to upper-bound arguments. Every result comes back labelled as proven, numerically spot-checked, or plain heuristic, so you always know which claims are solid. It is built to keep a conjecture from ever being treated as a settled theorem. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
