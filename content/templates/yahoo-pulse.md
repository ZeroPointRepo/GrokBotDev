## What it does

Keeps an eye on a personal list of stock symbols and hands back a plain briefing. It pulls quotes from a free charting endpoint, gathers the headlines tied to each symbol, and renders price-and-volume images you can scan in seconds. It reports only: it places no orders and gives no investment advice. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
