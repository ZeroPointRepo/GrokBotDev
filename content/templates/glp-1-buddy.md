## What it does

A tracking companion for people on weekly GLP-1 treatment such as semaglutide or tirzepatide. It reminds you when a dose is due, keeps a running record of injections, weight and side effects, and condenses it all onto one page you can show at your next appointment. It records and reminds; it does not advise medically.
