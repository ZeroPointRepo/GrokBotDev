## What it does

Each day it gathers your email, your calendar and your X conversations and compresses them into one short brief. The replies you owe get drafted and parked in Gmail drafts for your approval, and long-silent threads get a nudge so they do not slip away. Nothing goes out until you say yes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
