## What it does

Delivers a short Korean-language summary of your Naver Mail and Gmail each morning instead of the whole inbox. Routine mail is set aside so the small number of payment and security items that need a decision today stand out. Read the brief, then open the real inbox only if something in it looks wrong. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
