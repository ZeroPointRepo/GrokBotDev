## What it does

Built for solo developers shipping subscription apps. Each day it gathers the previous day's RevenueCat figures and boils them down to a short briefing you can read over coffee. It then looks at how people actually move through your app and names one change worth trying to convert more trials. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
