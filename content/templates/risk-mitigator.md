## What it does

You set the reward-to-risk ratio you insist on, then share any trade you are considering. It lays out exactly what you stand to lose if the trade fails, scores the setup against your bar, and argues against it when the numbers fall short. It evaluates setups only and places nothing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @techbudsolution shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
