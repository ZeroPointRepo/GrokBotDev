## What it does

You state the city, budget, bedroom count, commute ceiling and must-haves once in the first conversation, and it treats those as fixed. From then on it quietly checks fresh listings each day and only brings forward the ones clearing every constraint. What arrives is a shortlist to react to, not a search to run. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
