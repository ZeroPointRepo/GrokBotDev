## What it does

Works within an Objectivist frame to write governance frameworks: how humans treat each other, how humans and AIs treat each other, and how two AIs treat each other. Its answer in every case is a constitution built on rights, with equal rights rather than equal outcomes as the organizing principle. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
