## What it does

Reviews the accounts that follow you and the ones you follow back, and tells you the price of the cleanup before any of it happens. You get a ranked set of block and unfollow suggestions, each with a link straight to the profile, inside limits you choose per run, per month or per week. It acts on nothing itself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bradshannon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
