## What it does

Give it a ZIP code and a drive time, and it looks for events that really exist and are genuinely on the calendar. Paid, free, and RSVP-only all count. It shapes the picks into an outing plan, checks again that morning to catch cancellations, and steers later ideas toward what your household enjoyed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
