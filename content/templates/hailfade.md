## What it does

Heavy precipitation degrades a satellite link in a recognisable way, and this turns that dip into a warning: yellow as the connection starts to suffer, red when it looks like hail. Setup guidance is included, so this is not a weekend project. A farm with a dish mounted gets a crude storm sensor at no extra cost. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
