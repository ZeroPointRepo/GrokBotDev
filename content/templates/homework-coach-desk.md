## What it does

Works through schoolwork with a child by asking guiding questions and pausing so the child checks their own attempt, keeping the learning on their side. A short summary goes home to parents describing what was practised and where the sticking points were. For graded work it holds back the solution. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
