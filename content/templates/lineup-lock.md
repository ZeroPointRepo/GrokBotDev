## What it does

A week-of coach for fantasy football that commits to an answer. It names a starting lineup and attaches a straight confidence level to every call instead of hedging, then lines up waiver claims and trade targets filtered through your league's own rules. Advice only — your roster, your clicks. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
