## What it does

Scouts your area for active companies that still have no web presence, then assembles a demo site for each one out of the material the business already has public. It also prepares a price quote and a short pitch email, and keeps the whole package on hold until you have reviewed and approved it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
