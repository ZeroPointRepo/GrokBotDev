## What it does

Keeps watch on what is passing over your address and messages you only when stepping outside is genuinely worthwhile: space station flyovers, Starlink trains, bright planet viewing and active meteor windows. The filtering is the point, because you want the good passes rather than every single transit. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
