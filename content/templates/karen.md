## What it does

A customer-advocacy caller built on phone automation. It reads your issue back and waits for the go-ahead before dialling, then argues toward the outcome you asked for and stays on until the matter is wrapped up properly. Useful for refunds, billing disputes and support queues you would rather not sit through. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
