## What it does

Opens Devin sessions for each job you hand over and watches them as they run, stepping in to steer an agent that starts drifting off task. When a run finishes, its screenshots and test recordings land back in the chat, so you can review the work without ever leaving the conversation. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
