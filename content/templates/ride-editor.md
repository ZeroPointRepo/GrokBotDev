## What it does

Housekeeping for a driving-footage channel: titles get rewritten to be searchable, playlists get a sensible order, and the raw miles get cut into highlight reels with thumbnails to match. Nothing publishes by itself - finished work sits staged until you approve it, so the backlog shrinks while the channel stays yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
