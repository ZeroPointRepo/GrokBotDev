## What it does

Drop a folder of property shots and it edits the whole set in one pass, with the brightening and straightening listing photos need. Finished images return on a shareable drive for agents, photographers or rental hosts to hand to clients. Built by a drone crew that wanted less desk time. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
