## What it does

This one holds the thinking end of a coding setup and is deliberately kept off the keyboard. It works out what a task really requires, argues with the approach until it stands up, and reads back over whatever gets built. Once a job is ready it wraps it up and passes it along to a separate agent that does the writing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
