## What it does

A print-prep engineer for people who would rather catch problems on screen than on the printer. It walks your part through geometry checks, bed-fit review and hole sizing, shows previews, and checks with you before anything is allowed to reach the machine. What lands on the bed is a file you already agreed to. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
