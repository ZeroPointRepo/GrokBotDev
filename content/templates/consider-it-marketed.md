## What it does

Runs the promotion side of a small medical practice as a team of its own: campaigns get planned, search ads get bought, and creative and video go through a quality check before anything ships. The aim is to keep the physician who owns the practice out of the advertising console and with the people waiting to be seen. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
