## What it does

A small, focused Linux desktop utility. It installs the Hyprland droplet pointer theme on an Omarchy setup at whatever size you pick between 24 and 48 pixels, pulling the artwork from the project's own pixel set instead of third-party cursor packs so nothing unvetted lands on your machine. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
