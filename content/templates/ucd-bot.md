## What it does

Built on the practices set out by Nielsen Norman Group, the government service standard used across UK digital teams, and the DDaT capability framework. It carries a project through every phase, producing first-draft artefacts as it goes and keeping a traceable record of the evidence each decision rested on. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
