## What it does

Built for procurement portals with no usable API: it walks the same click-path weekly for renewals, seats and purchasing, and comes back only when a human decision is needed. The portal keeps working the way it always has while the routine part stops costing anyone an hour. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
