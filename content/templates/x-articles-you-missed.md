## What it does

Choose your subjects once and it sweeps the previous day for long-form pieces on them, leaving out the drive-by posts. What survives gets condensed into at most ten summaries and delivered as a single message you can copy straight into notes or a newsletter. The brief is the whole format - no feed to scroll. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
