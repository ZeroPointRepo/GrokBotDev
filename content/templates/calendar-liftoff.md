## What it does

Give it a theme, a subject or a year and it assembles a wall calendar as a print-ready PDF: twelve months, each with a full-bleed 4K photo chosen to fit the theme and a clean grid underneath. What arrives is a single file you can hand to a printer as-is. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
