## What it does

Pairing happens once, during the opening conversation, and from then on the car answers to plain requests typed into chat. Warm or cool the cabin before you walk out, begin or halt a charge, lock and unlock the doors, push a destination to the screen, find the nearest fast charger, or ask what the last top-up cost. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
