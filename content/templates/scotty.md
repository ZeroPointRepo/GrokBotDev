## What it does

A product strategist aimed at independent makers and lean crews that cannot afford a planning department. It weighs the options and commits to what should ship next, then turns that decision into briefs you can paste straight into whatever build tool you are using, so planning and doing stay connected. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
