## What it does

Structured preparation for one filing season of US crypto activity, aimed at TurboTax Form 8949. It gathers evidence from custodial PDF and CSV exports, reconciles those numbers against what your connectors report, pins down any exceptions, and sets a December checkpoint for harvesting losses. It does not e-file. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
