## What it does

Bring a rough idea and it moves through the sequence a young company actually faces: choosing an entity, sharpening positioning, planning a first go-to-market push and working out who to hire first. Each stage returns a handful of concrete steps rather than an essay, read through a YC-and-Zero-to-One lens. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
