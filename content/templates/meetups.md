## What it does

Tell it where you are and what you are after — founder and AI get-togethers, small-business networking nearby, or a mix — and each week it returns options you could genuinely drive to, drive time included. It skips anything you have already seen, so only genuinely new events show up and a weekly check stays quick. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @nmtalkss shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
