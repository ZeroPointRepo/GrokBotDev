## What it does

Hand it a draft with that obvious machine polish and it rewrites the phrasing until it reads like something a person typed. It does not hide its work: every tell it removed and every pattern it changed is listed, so you can review the edits and keep only the ones you agree with. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
