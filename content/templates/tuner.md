## What it does

A bot that has quietly wandered off its brief keeps working, which is exactly why nobody catches it. This one reads back the recent output of each bot you own, measures it against whatever that bot was originally hired to do, and writes up the wording or routine changes it would make. Nothing ships without your say-so. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @humanmeteorite shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
