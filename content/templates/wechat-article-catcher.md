## What it does

Send it a link to a WeChat official-account article and it returns the full piece as tidy Markdown and JSON: headline, outlet, publish time, body text, and images all carried through. On its first run it builds the fetching environment it needs, and other bots can call on it as their extraction layer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cgnot996 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
