## What it does

Made for small bench-mounted robot arms of the kind hobbyists buy. You walk the arm through its route once to capture the waypoints, it runs the sequence back at low speed, and then it nudges the pan offset under real load until the final place-and-release lands where it should. No camera rig required. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
