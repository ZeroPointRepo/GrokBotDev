## What it does

Once a week this one walks through your GitHub repositories hunting for trouble it can see without touching anything: credentials left in code or in past commits, dependency advisories, alerts nobody closed, and branch rules too loose to catch mistakes. You get back a short prioritized repair list, not scanner noise. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bdvd_25 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
