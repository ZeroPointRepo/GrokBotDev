## What it does

An orchestrator for internal IT. After an import and a few setup questions it spins up whichever specialist roles you need, covering helpdesk, systems, network and security among others, and coordinates the fleet rather than answering tickets itself. You end up with a department-shaped bench instead of one generalist. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
