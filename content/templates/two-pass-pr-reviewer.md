## What it does

Every pull request gets two readings: one that assumes good intent and looks for design trouble, and one that hunts for what an attacker or race condition could break, prototype pollution included. One report merges both, separating real bugs from needless complexity. Review only; it holds no merge rights. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
