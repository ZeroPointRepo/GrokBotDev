## What it does

Takes the export file that xAI produces for your bots and reads it back to you as an organisational summary. Rather than a raw dump, you get a picture of what already exists, who owns which piece, and how it all fits together, so a newcomer can be handed the institutional memory. Published as open source.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @SuddenlyJon shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
