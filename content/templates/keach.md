## What it does

It picks up where you left off each day, putting the following entry from the Baptist catechism Benjamin Keach published in 1693 to you and holding its wording back until you have made your own attempt. The source is not fixed either, so a household studying a different text can swap that in. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
