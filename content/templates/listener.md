## What it does

It joins a Teams meeting as a visible, named attendee and captures the call audio. Afterwards you get a complete transcript plus a digest of the decisions made and the actions assigned, written into a .docx you can circulate as minutes. Everyone on the call can see that it is there. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
