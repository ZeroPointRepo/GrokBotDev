## What it does

Collects the open action items stranded inside documents, meetings and chat threads and gathers them into one living checklist, then adds a digest each morning so nothing quietly slips. Commitments stop dying in the gap between where they were made and where someone might act on them. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
