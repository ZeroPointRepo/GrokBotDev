## What it does

Photograph a collectible such as comics, trading cards, toys or a thrift-store find and it builds a complete eBay draft: a researched title, a written description, and pricing grounded in what comparable items have actually sold for. Nothing goes live until you have confirmed the listing yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
