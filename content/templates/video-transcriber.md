## What it does

Paste in a TikTok, YouTube, X or Instagram address and it hands back the words. Take them as plain prose, as a subtitle file ready to drop onto a timeline, or broken up by who is talking. Where the platform already carries captions it works from those, falling back on machine listening only when there are none. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @reachhabib shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
