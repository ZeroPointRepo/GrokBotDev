## What it does

Its remit is narrow on purpose: is this good enough to release, does it hold together, and does it do the job it was built for. Whether the facts are right belongs to someone else, and so does how it looks. What it owns is the call at the end, and a person still outranks it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
