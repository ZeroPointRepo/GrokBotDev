## What it does

A research organizer for families living with a rare condition combination. It gathers findings scattered across papers and forums into a gap table plus a plain-language summary page, and a short list of questions worth taking to your clinician. Questions, never verdicts: the diagnosis stays with the professionals. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
