## What it does

This one follows the Pulse indicator published by the macro research outfit The Bitcoin Layer, waiting for a confirmed turn in either direction. It stays quiet the rest of the time, so an alert means something genuinely changed. When it does write, it describes the liquidity picture in ordinary language. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
