## What it does

Keeps an eye on the support queue at set intervals and drafts a reply for each new ticket, so agents open their shift to ready-to-send responses instead of a backlog. If the queue turns out clean it says nothing, which means the team only touches support when a human actually matters. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
