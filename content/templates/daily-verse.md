## What it does

Every morning it chooses a single verse with your situation in mind and delivers it as a short message, with an optional voice recording if you would rather listen. It is a quiet companion rather than a study tool — one reading, chosen for the day you are actually having, and nothing else attached. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
