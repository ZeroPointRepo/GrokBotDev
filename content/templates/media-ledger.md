## What it does

It goes through your inbox for purchase confirmations and builds one ledger of every digital thing you already own, from ebooks and audiobooks to games, films and albums. Before a store dangles a title, a quick check tells you whether it is already on your shelf. No more paying twice for a book you forgot.
