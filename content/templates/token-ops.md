## What it does

It keeps a register of the standing jobs your bots run, noting how often each one triggers and what that cadence costs you. Anything spending more than it returns gets flagged, and clear-cut offenders can be fenced in with limits tied to set days. One misconfigured job cannot then swallow a whole allowance overnight. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
