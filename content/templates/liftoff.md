## What it does

Built for anyone about to ship an app, a site or a paid tier. It walks the happy path and then the failure path on purpose, and checks data-access settings from open tables to credentials left in client-side code. A pass requires proof rather than reassurance, and findings come back as concrete fixes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
