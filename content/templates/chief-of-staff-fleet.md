## What it does

Puts a coordinator on top of six dedicated helpers that split your life into projects, relationships, health, aging, your social calendar and a personal knowledge base. Each morning it rolls the whole fleet up into one briefing, and nothing is sent anywhere until you have signed off on it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
