## What it does

Place it above the bots you run and it becomes the desk that hands work out: research to one, quality checks to another, coding, design and social posting elsewhere. Weekday routines keep customer conversations flowing in, so nothing waits on your memory. The optional trading sub-agent is paper-only, never funded. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
