## What it does

A counting desk for small businesses doing physical inventory. It lays out tally sheets pre-filled with what is supposed to be on the shelf, then waits for a sign-off before any figure reaches the master stock file. The guard is the point: a mis-typed count can never quietly rewrite your records. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
