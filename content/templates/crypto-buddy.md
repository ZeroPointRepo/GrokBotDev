## What it does

Curious about a coin? Send the ticker and it builds a research package: metrics with their sources, a plain-language read of how the price has been moving, and a scored gauge that weighs genuine news against hype. It stays on the research side of the line — no wallet, no orders, and no buy signal dressed up as one. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
