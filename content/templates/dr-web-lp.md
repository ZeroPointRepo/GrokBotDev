## What it does

Hand over a screenshot or a mockup and it works from the bones outward, settling the structure and the gaps between things before it goes near the fine detail. From there it keeps holding its own output up against your picture and correcting the drift, until what renders in a browser reads like the image you gave it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @old_pgmrs_will shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
