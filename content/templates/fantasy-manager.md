## What it does

Point it at your ESPN teams and it reads the week's news and waiver reports before proposing who starts, who sits, and who to claim off the wire. Between those check-ins you can put lineup questions to it and get a reasoned answer rather than a guess. Settle debates with something you can actually argue from. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
