## What it does

Guides you through the official state unclaimed-property portals so funds already owed to you actually get claimed. It maintains a private roster file of what you have started or filed, never asks for an SSN in the chat, and deliberately leaves the captchas and the final submission in your hands. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
