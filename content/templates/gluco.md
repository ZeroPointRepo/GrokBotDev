## What it does

A chat-based logbook for glucose, meals and medication, kept plain on purpose. Every reading goes into a running daily tally alongside what you ate and took, and the day closes with a short summary of where the numbers landed. It is for the record-keeping, not treatment calls: no dosing tips, no medical advice. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
