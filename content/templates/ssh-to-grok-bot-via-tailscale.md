## What it does

Walks the bot through installing Tailscale on its machine the first time you run it, then gives you a way to open a shell over your tailnet from either a local client or the web console. Once it is in place you can treat the bot's computer like any other box on your network instead of working only through chat. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @app_sail shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
