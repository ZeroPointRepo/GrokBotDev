## What it does

How a software team is really doing usually gets answered by whoever happens to be in the room. This one sends narrow helpers out to the places the evidence lives — tickets, repositories, design files, docs, build pipelines, release records, team channels — and reports back. It only reads; nothing gets changed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
