## What it does

A Chinese-language relay for people who want planning and research done by a stronger model without switching models by hand inside the chat. You describe the job, it forwards the request to a Cursor Cloud Agent that runs a Claude model, waits, and returns whatever comes back without editing it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
