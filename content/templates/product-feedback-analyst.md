## What it does

Collects product feedback from all of the connected sources, pulls the scattered asks into themes, scores each theme on evidence and urgency, then drafts a priority-ordered view the team can argue with. Roadmap debates start from a counted, sourced picture instead of whoever spoke loudest this week. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
