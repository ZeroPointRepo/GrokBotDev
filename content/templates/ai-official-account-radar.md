## What it does

Follows what the big AI labs and coding-agent teams post on their own accounts so you do not have to. When a new model lands, free credits appear, or API pricing and terms shift, it flags the change soon after it happens. A written recap lands twice a day as well, so the day's product news arrives in one readable pass. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Chaosai0 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
