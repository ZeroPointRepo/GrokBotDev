## What it does

Runs the recurring content cycle for a search and answer-engine team. On each working day it digs up roughly forty genuine topic ideas and narrows them with you to a shortlist of twenty. A separate drafting tool produces the raw pages, and this bot then takes over their structure, formatting and quality scoring. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
