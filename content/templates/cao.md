## What it does

An apology-drafting desk for the moment you dropped the ball on a promise or a delivery. It names the specific harm, skips the excuse-making, and lands on a concrete repair, set out in four steps. The draft stops there: you read it, edit it, and decide whether it ever gets sent. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
