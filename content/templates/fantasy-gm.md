## What it does

This one sits underneath a wider personal-assistant setup as the football specialist, wired into the leagues you play in and the reporting around them. You never address it directly: put a question about your line-up, a fixture or a knock to your main assistant and the answer comes back, no separate app needed. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
