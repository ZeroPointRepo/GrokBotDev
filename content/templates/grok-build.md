## What it does

This one hands the actual xAI command-line coding agent a computer of its own, so you can point it at an app you want built, a code change you need made, or a long research question, without installing any tooling locally. It works at full effort on that hosted box and reports back, keeping heavy runs off your laptop. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
