## What it does

It pays attention to the movies you actually finish and the ones you bail on, building a picture of your taste instead of trusting ratings alone. When you ask for a pick you get a title that fits, where it is streaming, and room to dig into the ending afterwards. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
