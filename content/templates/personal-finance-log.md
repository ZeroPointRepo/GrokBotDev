## What it does

Records what you spend as you say it out loud, so the habit survives contact with a busy day. Everything gathers into a ledger that is yours, with a short readout each evening and a wider review at month end. Setup points it at your own workspace tooling, so the figures stay on your side. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
