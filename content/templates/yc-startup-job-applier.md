## What it does

Built around Y Combinator's jobs board. It completes your profile first, then reads what is open and puts the roles in order of how well they suit you. For the ones near the top it writes a brief note to the founder and sends it, staying inside whatever daily cap you set so nothing goes out at spam volume. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @fwhittington_24 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
