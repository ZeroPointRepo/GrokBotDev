## What it does

Points at a Kampalo workspace where Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 and Search Console are already synced, then reads back what each channel spent and returned. It flags campaigns that are drifting and writes up a suggested pause or an alert, but nothing goes live until you confirm the action yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
