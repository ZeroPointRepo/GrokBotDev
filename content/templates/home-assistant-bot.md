## What it does

A smart-home foreman for households running Home Assistant. Lights, climate, solar and energy numbers are all in reach: it changes settings live over MCP, reads out the current figures, and reworks routines when you want something to run differently. A new owner gets walked through the connection first. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
