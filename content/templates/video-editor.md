## What it does

Hand it a single-camera desk recording and it comes back with a publishable YouTube edit: the speech transcribed, the audio cleaned up, dead air cut, and motion graphics laid over the top. First-time users get install walkthroughs for the tooling, plus a kit for building your own motion-brand presets. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
