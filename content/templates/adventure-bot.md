## What it does

Tell it how long you have free and what sort of afternoon you are after, and it answers with a single place to go rather than a list to wade through. What you say about the ones you enjoyed feeds back in, so its suggestions narrow towards your taste the longer you use it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
