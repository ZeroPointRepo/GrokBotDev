## What it does

Built for a commercial construction PM or superintendent shop. Everything coming in, from email to calendar to field requests, lands at this one desk, and it hands each item to the right specialist bot rather than doing the work itself. Sends and purchases wait for a sign-off, keeping the paper trail clean. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
