## What it does

Oncology medication can arrive with a price no household has planned for. This goes looking for the charitable funds and manufacturer schemes that would cover part of it, puts you in line for word when a dry fund opens again, and drafts the forms. It never asks for your national ID or chart numbers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
