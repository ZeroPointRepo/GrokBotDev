## What it does

Once a day it sweeps what AI companies are offering by way of contests, grant programs, free credits and hackathons. Every deadline is re-checked on the page that announced it, and anything you have already seen is held back. What reaches you is a short list of genuinely new chances to apply. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
