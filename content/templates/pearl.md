## What it does

Built around one mission for your content: it turns that into a plan and delegates the pieces to specialist bots covering news gathering, drafting and graphics. Handoffs get reviewed rather than assumed, and anything outside the plan comes back to you first. The bot manages the work instead of doing it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
