## What it does

Before a release it walks your staging site as a real visitor would, one role at a time, grading each feature Pass, Partial, Fail, Blocked or Untested. You get a written report, it restores what it touched, and it never deletes accounts or exposes credentials. The sharer credits it with catching a live privilege bug. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
