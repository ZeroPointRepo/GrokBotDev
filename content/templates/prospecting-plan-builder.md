## What it does

Builds the week's outbound book of work from scratch: it seeds the contact list, fills in missing email addresses and mobile numbers, and returns a tracker that is ready to work immediately. Reps start Monday from a list worth calling instead of an empty sheet and good intentions. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
