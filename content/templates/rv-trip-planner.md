## What it does

Built around full-time RV travel, where the route is never really finished. The whole trip is held as a working plan in the chat, overnight availability at the networks you belong to is checked while you travel, and each reservation is lined up for your own click. A booking is never completed without you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
