## What it does

Connects a Gmail inbox to a Ramp corporate-card account and works through them daily, matching receipt emails to the charges they belong to and adding any memos that are missing. The point is continuity: teams whose card program suspends plastic over unattached paperwork stop losing access mid-week. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @matthewbarge shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
