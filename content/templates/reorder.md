## What it does

A purchasing desk for a small operation. It watches finished goods and materials against the thresholds you set, projects the date each line runs out, and drafts the supplier orders before a shelf goes empty. The drafts stay drafts — a human reviews and places every order. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
