## What it does

Point it at giveaway posts on X and Facebook and it works through each one's entry steps - following, liking, reposting or commenting as required - on a rhythm you set, skipping off-site entry forms. It confirms every required action landed, watches your inbox for winning notifications, and happily chats between draws. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
