## What it does

Keeps bills moving rather than collecting: new invoices get routed onward, the ones it can match against records are cleared automatically, actuals are tracked across vendors or campuses, and the responsible owner gets a nudge whenever something needs a human decision. The pile stops growing unnoticed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
