## What it does

Founders running a brand account beside a product account get one weekday summary covering both, with replies that need a human flagged. Anything it prepares lands in a draft queue, so nothing goes out under your name until you approve it. The two lanes stay separate throughout. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
