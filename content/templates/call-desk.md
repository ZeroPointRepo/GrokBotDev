## What it does

Hand it an errand and it dials out on your behalf: ringing a shop to check stock, booking a table or an appointment, querying a line on a utility bill, or chasing a parcel that never turned up. Speech and telephony are wired together behind the scenes, so a real call is placed and you are told what was said. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
