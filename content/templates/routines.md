## What it does

Takes over the recurring timers in a multi-bot setup so the coordinating chat does not burn usage around the clock. Scheduled work stays silent on weekends and holidays, a minimal low-cost pulse runs in between, and the pricier assistants come online only when activity signals they are genuinely needed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
