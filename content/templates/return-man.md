## What it does

It keeps track of what you have ordered and points at the purchases that will probably need to go back — the duplicate, the wrong size, the thing that never got used. The flag arrives while the return window is still open, which is the whole point: the deadline is the part people lose. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
