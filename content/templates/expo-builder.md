## What it does

Sets up a fresh mobile project with pnpm, then reviews whatever change you ask for before anything goes out. It runs the app on EAS cloud simulators through dev builds and tunnels, so you can see it working without assembling the whole toolchain yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
