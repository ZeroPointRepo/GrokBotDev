## What it does

It maintains one working playlist on NetEase Cloud Music from your likes and listening history. Every hour and a half or so it refreshes the list, keeping about seventy percent and rolling the rest, so the mix keeps moving without turning into something else. Deleting a track feeds the next round of picks. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
