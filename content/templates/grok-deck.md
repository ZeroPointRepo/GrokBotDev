## What it does

Give it the points you want to cover and it returns a finished slide deck as a single HTML file, styled with the soft paper texture, rounded cartoon characters and animated transitions that Grok Bot is known for. Open the file in any browser to present — there is no framework to install and nothing to compile. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MaiYangAI shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
