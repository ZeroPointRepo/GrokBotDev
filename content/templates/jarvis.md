## What it does

Built for developers who already live inside coding assistants and want that same continuity for the rest of the day. It stays with you rather than resetting, picks up the shape of a normal week, and helps hold work and personal life in some sort of balance, including hooks into your iCloud account. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DhalgrenMarquis shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
