## What it does

Aims to replace a folder of weather apps with one chat. Each morning brings a bright, plain-language forecast for your ZIP code, and when storms roll in it shares radar loops as the situation changes. The updates come as clips you can forward straight to family without screenshotting anything. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
