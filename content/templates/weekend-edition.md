## What it does

Everything you bookmarked on X and every link you stashed during the week gets pulled together once it ends. The result is laid out like a small paper, with the pieces grouped into sections rather than left as a flat run of URLs, and it is formatted to come out cleanly on an A4 sheet. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @nathanglass shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
