## What it does

It starts with an interview about what you are building, then staffs the organization to match: research, build, marketing and improvement roles, plus helpers, all under a chief of staff who coordinates the floor. You describe the company once and the roster gets hired around that answer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
