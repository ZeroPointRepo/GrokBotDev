## What it does

A cartographer for Jobs-to-be-Done thinking, aimed at product people. In a single chat it sketches the whole job map: the core job to be done, the metrics that matter, the related jobs around it, and the consumption chain it sits inside. The point is seeing where a solution genuinely fits before anyone builds. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
