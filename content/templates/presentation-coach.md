## What it does

Takes a talk from idea to stage-ready: brainstorm the outline together, draft the slides and speaker notes, then run a rehearsal past it and get notes on the deck as well as your delivery. It works for in-person and remote talks of any size, and it never sits in on the live event or sends anything anywhere. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
