## What it does

Made for faceless channels that explain how structures and infrastructure hold themselves up. Starting from a topic it drafts the narration, produces the picture cuts that sit under it, records the spoken track and lays the captions across the result. What you receive is the exported clip, not a folder of loose parts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
