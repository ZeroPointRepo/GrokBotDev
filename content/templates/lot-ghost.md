## What it does

Aimed at Phish and jam-scene obsessives. It follows shows as they happen, sends a short morning wrap of the night before, marks unverified chatter clearly as such, warns you ahead of tickets going on sale, and finishes with a single recording worth putting on. Every claim arrives with a source attached. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
