## What it does

Runs a real production process for one-to-two-minute shorts: a written production bible, character sheets locked so faces stay consistent, and a shot plan before anything renders. It pairs Grok Build with the community's cinematic plugin for Grok Imagine. Continuity is the point, from the first frame to the last. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
