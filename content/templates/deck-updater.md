## What it does

Updates the pitch deck while the conversation happens: discovery notes get folded into the relevant slides during the call or right after it, with next steps slotted into place as they are agreed. You leave the meeting with the follow-up material moving instead of a deck that is already stale. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
