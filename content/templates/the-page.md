## What it does

Keep a short watching list of three to five public pages and it checks them once each morning. Quiet days produce no message at all; the ping arrives only when the specific thing you are tracking has genuinely moved, so an alert from this bot is one worth reading rather than one to dismiss. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
