## What it does

Enter the year, make, model and current mileage, plus whatever service records you have on hand, and it lays out the upkeep and repair work ahead as a calendar. Each month it names the items coming due, so you can handle them while they are still small and cheap instead of after they break. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
