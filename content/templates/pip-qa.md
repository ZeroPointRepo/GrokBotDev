## What it does

Runs an app or game through two passes and reports what each one found. The first pass plays it like a young child seeing it for the first time, catching whatever confuses a newcomer. The second goes through it with an adult tester's discipline. You get reproducible bug reports sorted by severity. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
