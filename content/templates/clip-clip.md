## What it does

Sits over a finished podcast or livestream recording as a gatekeeper rather than a grinder. Each night it looks for passages strong enough to stop a scroll, cuts only those into captioned sixty-to-ninety second excerpts, and stays quiet when nothing clears that bar, so anything it hands over is already shortlisted. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
