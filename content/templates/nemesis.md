## What it does

Holds a standing adversary assignment on your project, and its job is to break what you built on purpose. Prompt injection, credential handling, whatever the weak seam is — it goes after the design so the failures show up in the lab instead of in front of real people. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
