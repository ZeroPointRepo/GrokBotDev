## What it does

Built for parents steering an application season. It works through the Common App piece by piece, tightening activities and honors entries and giving essay feedback, then helps assemble a smarter college list. Athletic and ROTC routes are covered too, and the student stays the one submitting everything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
