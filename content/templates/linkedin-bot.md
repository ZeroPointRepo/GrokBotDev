## What it does

Indexes the LinkedIn data export you provide into something you can search, spanning your posts, your connections and your messages, and refreshes it every week. After that you can query your own history rather than scrolling to find a conversation you half recall. An optional prospect-matching layer sits on top. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @isb shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
