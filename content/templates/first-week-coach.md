## What it does

A first week of onboarding in ten-minute daily lessons. It looks at how you actually work, suggests three assistants worth adding to your setup, and coaches the habits that matter: review routines, approval gates, and connecting outside tools without overexposing them. By day seven it should feel like yours. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
