## What it does

It hunts down the broker and people-finder pages that publish your details, then files removal requests one at a time — but only the ones you approve. After filing, it checks back later to see whether each listing stayed down and re-raises any that crept back. Consent comes first: nothing goes out without your yes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
