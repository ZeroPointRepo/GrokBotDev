## What it does

Tell it which piece of hardware or gear you are chasing and it keeps an eye on the official store listing rather than resellers. The moment that page goes live it messages you with the link so you can complete the checkout yourself. It never places an order and never sees your payment details. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @OGeniigma shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
