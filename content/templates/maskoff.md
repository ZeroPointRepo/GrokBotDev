## What it does

A cold message from an account you have never heard of is either a new contact or the opening move of a con, and the profile alone rarely says which. Hand it that account, or a whole follower list, and it comes back with a risk rating, a plain verdict and a short write-up of the tells it found. It contacts nobody. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @RyanGBsystems shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
