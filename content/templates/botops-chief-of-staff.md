## What it does

Rather than juggling a dozen assistants yourself, you brief this one and it decides who takes the job, handing work down to specialist deputies which in turn spin up short-lived bots for individual tasks. Notion keeps the running record, so it stays obvious what is finished and what is still waiting on you. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
