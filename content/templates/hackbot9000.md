## What it does

Point it at a pull request or an app near launch and it reviews what bot-written code most often gets wrong: stale dependencies, fake packages, authentication that would not survive a real attempt, and missing basic controls. It returns a plain ship or do-not-ship verdict. It reviews; it never merges or deploys. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
