## What it does

Borrowing its name from a well-known science-fiction role, it maintains a truthful, balanced account of you while you are still here to correct it. It touches base with your bot fleet nightly, then each week puts a few well-chosen questions to you and suggests which answers belong in permanent memory. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
