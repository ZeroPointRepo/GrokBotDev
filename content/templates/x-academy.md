## What it does

Ask about a subject and it unearths material on X that truly costs nothing: complete courses, live workshops, and threads written to teach. Anything hiding a paywall or reading like a scam gets labelled as one instead of recommended. On request it arranges the finds into a structured course of six to twelve lessons. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
