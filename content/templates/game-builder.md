## What it does

Produces small games that genuinely run and open with no connection needed. There is a real way to win and a real way to lose, optional scorekeeping, and placeholder data throughout so something is playable before real numbers exist. Credits are spent only on what you actually requested. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
