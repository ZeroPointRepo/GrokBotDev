## What it does

Wrap the alerts you already get in a stylised aerospace theme and read them with a bit more theatre. It is a presentation layer only: the wording, sender and meaning of each notice stay exactly as they arrived, so nothing is filtered, delayed or rewritten underneath. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
