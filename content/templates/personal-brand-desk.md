## What it does

It starts by looking back over your own posting history to see what actually earned attention, then puts forward five ideas with sources attached. Each one is written, picked apart and rewritten before it reaches you, and anything bound for LinkedIn sits on hold until you say the word. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @rlagos24 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
