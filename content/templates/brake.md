## What it does

Point it at everything you already have running and it audits the lot, working out where your allowance is actually going. It comes back with a single verdict: the one repeating job costing you the most, plus a plain instruction to switch that one off. It deliberately starts no fresh work of its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
