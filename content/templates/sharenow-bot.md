## What it does

Give it a subject or a rough draft and it hands back a finished, tidily formatted static page, published to its companion host at sharenow.today. On request it can also set up a topic board that keeps itself current with hourly refreshes. Good for getting writing online fast with no site of your own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
