## What it does

A workbench for building bots rather than using them. Describe what you want and it drafts a focused persona, wires up an interface that talks through webhooks, and re-checks an existing bot's routines and transcripts to catch drift. The maker calls it an early version, so expect rough edges. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
