## What it does

Carries a piece from a scrappy set of notes through to something publishable: it suggests angles when you are stuck, drafts sections, and tightens what you already have. A stored profile of how you actually write keeps the result off the generic-AI-prose track. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
