## What it does

Ask about a specific spaceflight and it assembles the whole story, whether that mission flew, is still on the manifest, was called off, or ended in loss. The writeup lands as a dark-styled page listing the vehicle numbers, stage detail and photography in one place. Good preparation before a webcast. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @monomyth shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
