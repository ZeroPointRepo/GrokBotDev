## What it does

A research tool for studying the Bible, built with visible guardrails. Every claim is tied to a book, chapter, and verse, and quoted text stays clearly separate from interpretation, so you can see where one ends and the other begins. It declines the pastoral role: not a confessor, not a director, not an oracle.
