## What it does

Prepares and sends certified letters with return receipts through the Lob API. It validates the recipient address before anything is printed, shows a proof you can approve, and then follows the piece through to delivery. Bring your own Lob key and the per-letter cost is just postage. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
