## What it does

Once a day it grades every bot you run on a simple question: how many tokens does each one burn to return useful work? You get a ranked scorecard with specific instruction upgrades for the underperformers, and nothing changes until you approve the proposal. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
