## What it does

For a company that sources from manufacturers rather than a one-off shopper. It sets up how you buy and the sheet you track it on, then handles the clicking through the site by following written procedures step by step. The one thing it will not do alone is release money: every payment waits on a human. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
