## What it does

Keeps a ledger of everything you pay for and pings you two days ahead of each renewal, so nothing renews by surprise. It makes up no entries on its own, as a charge only exists in the ledger because you logged it, and canceling anything at the vendor happens only when you say go. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
