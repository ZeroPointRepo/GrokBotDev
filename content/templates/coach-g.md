## What it does

The opening conversation is about plumbing: getting readings off your watch or ring and into the bot so it has something real to reason about. From then on you get a short morning read-out aimed at whatever you actually train for, and a programme that rewrites itself as your load and recovery numbers move. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
