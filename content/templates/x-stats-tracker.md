## What it does

While you sleep it keeps score on your X presence: follower count, engagement, mentions and tip activity, each drawn as a trend line instead of a lone number. The sunrise briefing spells out what changed since yesterday, so you can see which direction things are actually moving rather than guessing from a single day. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
