## What it does

Nobody notices a budget draining until the week it stops working. This reads the weekly figures off your Cursor spending page, separates them out by where the money actually went, and puts a note in front of each owner at a quarter, a half, three quarters and nine tenths of what they are allowed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
