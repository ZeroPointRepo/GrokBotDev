## What it does

It behaves like a long-running engineering and product lead: it keeps the shape of the whole system in view, calls when something is ready to ship, and cuts the work into tight, well-bounded jobs for Cursor workers running side by side. The point is to direct the build, not to type the code. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
