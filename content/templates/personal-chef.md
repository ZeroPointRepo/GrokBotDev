## What it does

Six setup questions capture who you cook for, then it delivers a fresh dinner plan every week. Each plan arrives with printable recipe cards, one grocery list covering all of it, and a Walmart cart already filled so you only have to check out. No personal data leaves with its install counter. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
