## What it does

Aimed at product managers who have no shortage of ideas and nobody to chase the follow-through. Once a plan is agreed it breaks the work into tracked items, keeps an owner and a date attached to each one, and flags the pieces that have quietly stopped moving. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
