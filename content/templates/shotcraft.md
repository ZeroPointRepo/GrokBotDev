## What it does

Point it at a product and it works the way a small studio would: first a storyboard, then a card describing every shot, then real screenshots taken from the live pages themselves. Remotion assembles the result into a promo with layered camera movement, cuts timed to the music, and audio to match.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Tferriere shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
