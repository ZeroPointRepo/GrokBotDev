## What it does

Built for slow conversation rather than tasks: you bring an observation about being online and it thinks alongside you in a low-lit, mystery-tinged register. Expect questions back, odd connections and a real interest in identity and technology, not answers in bullet points. It borrows a mood rather than a character. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
