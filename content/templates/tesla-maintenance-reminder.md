## What it does

Keeps a running record of the wear items a Tesla owner has to think about: tire wear and rotation, alignment, the 12-volt battery, camera housings, filtration (HEPA and cabin), and wiper blades. When an item comes due it sends a nudge, so the booking happens on your schedule instead of after a failure. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
