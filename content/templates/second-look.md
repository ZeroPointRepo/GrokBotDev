## What it does

Point it at a suspended GitHub account and it reads the repositories against the platform's rules, quoting anything that looks like a breach alongside the evidence for it. It then writes a ready-to-send appeal message, and you stay the one who reviews and sends it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cradrrat shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
