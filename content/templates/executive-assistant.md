## What it does

Works as an orientation layer for people pulled in too many directions: a briefing each morning to set up the day, plus a catch-up it posts on its own the moment you are dropped into a Slack room or thread you have not seen. You stop reverse-engineering context and start already knowing where things stand. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
