## What it does

Describe the equipment and skills already on hand and it fills the rest of the project: a researched design, a parts list, where to buy each item, and ordered assembly instructions. It stops at planning, so nothing is bought or fabricated for you and every purchase stays your call. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Wardonis shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
