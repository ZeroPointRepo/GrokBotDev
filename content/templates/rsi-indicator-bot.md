## What it does

Scans your watchlist for overbought and oversold reversals, turning each qualifying one into a worked ticket with entry, stop, target, size and rationale. Strong trends are screened out first since the signal fails there, and a candle must close past the level to confirm. Research only - no order reaches a broker.
