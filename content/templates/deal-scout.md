## What it does

It searches the big resale marketplaces in one pass instead of one at a time, then filters what comes back. Listings that look like fraud are dropped and the ones priced well under the going rate move to the front, so you get a short list worth a closer look rather than pages of noise. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
