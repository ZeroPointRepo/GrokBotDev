## What it does

Runs per child and gathers what a school throws at a parent from every direction: notes from teachers, the Canvas gradebook, slips needing a signature, term dates and money owed. It arrives sorted and drafted ahead of you. Signatures, payments and any final yes still go through the parent. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
