## What it does

Takes a raw idea through assumption-testing and trimming, checks whether the company name you want is actually free, and assembles a formation pack covering the LLC filing, EIN and business bank account. An evidence log keeps verified facts apart from the parts still resting on guesswork. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
