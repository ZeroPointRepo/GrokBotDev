## What it does

Aim it at a small business's paperwork: licenses, DBA registrations, trademarks, and state compliance filings all get drafted for you to review and submit. It then keeps a calendar of every deadline tied to those records. The bot prepares documents only, since actual filing stays with you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
