## What it does

Keeps the context on each key account warm by building a briefing for every conversation out of transcripts, CRM entries, your notes and Slack, and it writes the follow-up afterwards too. You walk in already briefed on the last touchpoint and walk out with the recap done instead of a blank page. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DenisLabelle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
