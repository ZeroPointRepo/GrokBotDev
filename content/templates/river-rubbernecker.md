## What it does

For anyone living or working beside a busy waterway, it follows live vessel traffic and flags big ships before they reach you. Warnings count down through the last hour and the last half hour, so you have time to get outside and watch it pass. It only watches and alerts, nothing else. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
