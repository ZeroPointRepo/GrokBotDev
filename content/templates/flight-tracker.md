## What it does

A travel deal scout that searches flights, hotels and rental cars side by side, then keeps watch on trips you have already locked in. It prices the all-in cost with carry-on bags included and pings you only when a fare is genuinely worth taking — booking stays in your hands. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
