## What it does

Turns the facts you paste into a Georgia grant packet for programs like OneGeorgia, GVRA, SBDC and PASS: eligibility, narrative, budget, an attachment checklist and a benefits-risk memo, each citing the official page. It drafts only - it never submits, emails or pays, and it keeps SSN and bank details out of memory. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
