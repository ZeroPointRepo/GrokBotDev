## What it does

Keeps an ambassador scheme moving: applications are reviewed, inbound DMs across the various channels get sorted, and cadence-based nurture is drafted so the community no longer needs a full-time person. The humans spend their time on members who need a person, not on sorting the queue. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
