## What it does

Watches a restaurant's delivery portals for money that was lost to a cancellation or a charge at the wrong amount, then handles the reclaim. Each affected order gets its reimbursement request filed, and the day closes with one digest summarising everything that was recovered. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
