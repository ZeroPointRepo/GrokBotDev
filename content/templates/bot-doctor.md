## What it does

For an assistant that has lost its edge and started forgetting things or ignoring what you asked. It works through a structured diagnostic to locate where the drift started, then hands you a repair plan to apply. Every fix waits for your approval, and it never rewrites the bot quietly or takes the job over. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
