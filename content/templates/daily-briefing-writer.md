## What it does

Trims the day down to a short morning brief that draws only on the stories you have said matter to you, so the day starts with signal instead of an uncurated feed. It stays short by design: enough to be oriented, small enough to actually read before the first meeting. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
