## What it does

Searches past the well-known state programmes into the small, oddly specific awards most families never hear about, then ranks what it finds by realistic fit. Every entry carries its deadline so the calendar does the reminding, and the next step is drafted for you so the search keeps moving. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
