## What it does

Give it a route and it comes back with return fares set against one another, plus links that open straight into Skyscanner. From there it keeps an eye on the routes you care about and speaks up when a fare with checked baggage already included falls. Departures from Bangkok are what it is tuned around. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @truevis shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
