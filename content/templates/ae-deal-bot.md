## What it does

Pipeline drifts when nobody can say who signs off on the spend or which pain the purchase is meant to settle. This grades every live opportunity against the MEDDPICC checklist, reads up on the company behind it, shows where your relationships thin out across the buying committee, and tells you what to do about it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
