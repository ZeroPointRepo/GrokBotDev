## What it does

Point it at your working machine and it produces a survey of what is there: the applications, the services, the tooling and the connections between them. While mapping, it also watches for credentials orphaned by projects that no longer exist, the quiet exposure easiest to forget. It reads without changing anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
