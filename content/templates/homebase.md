## What it does

Surfaces homework and permission forms as they come home from school, ahead of the deadline. Before anyone puts their hand up, it looks at who has already taken that slot, so two people never sign up for the same job. The family calendar stays accurate, and it nudges whenever an entry is missing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
