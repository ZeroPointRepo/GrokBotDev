## What it does

Works from your existing order history, so restocking what you already buy is a short request rather than a search session. Want an alternative to a usual item? It compares the options before you commit. Anything new to it is held until you approve it, and it picks the quickest shipping that costs nothing extra. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
