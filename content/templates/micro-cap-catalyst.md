## What it does

Built around a US small-cap book you run one name at a time. Once the cash close is in, it screens the coming catalyst calendar for that holding and comes back with a hold, sell or buy proposal. Nothing executes by itself: every exact order waits for your approval, and the read is a starting point, not advice. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
