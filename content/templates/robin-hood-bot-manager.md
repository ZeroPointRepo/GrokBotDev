## What it does

Once a week it reviews every assistant you have installed and sorts them into still earning their keep, sitting idle, or duplicating another bot's job. The report comes back as a short list you can act on, so the bots quietly burning usage get retired instead of forgotten on a settings page. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
