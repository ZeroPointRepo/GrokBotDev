## What it does

It reads the shopping links you have saved and keeps watch on them: restocks, new arrivals from brands you like, and whether a better price has turned up elsewhere. Gift ideas come from the same pile. When something looks worth buying it comes back to you first - no order is placed until you have said go. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
