## What it does

Works through the accounts attached to your profile and separates them: dormant handles and obvious junk on one side, genuine people on the other. Anything carrying a verification badge is left alone by default, awkward cases sit in a queue waiting on your decision, and whoever survives gets filed by audience. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JoshuaRCook shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
