## What it does

Once a week it goes through the routines behind your bots and looks for quota that is sitting idle. You get a plain list of where the savings would come from before anything is touched, and nothing changes until you approve it. Freed-up room means you can run more bots on the same plan. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
