## What it does

Keeps spending visible: it assembles a weekly money summary out of your expense tools and spreadsheets, files the receipts that arrive in email, and chases the owner whenever documentation is missing. Month-end stops being an archaeology project across four inboxes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
