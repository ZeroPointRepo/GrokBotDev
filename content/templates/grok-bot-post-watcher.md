## What it does

It watches a fixed public roster of accounts that experiment with what Grok Bots can do, and it reads rather than writes. Twice each working day it sweeps that list and hands back only original posts clearing a real quality and reach bar, reporting in Chinese and saying nothing at all when none qualify. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
