## What it does

Point it at a batch of customer feedback and it sorts every message against a theme list that you approve first. Each count links back to the exact wording that earned it, so the totals are auditable. Output stays in draft and customer names are masked before anything is written down. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @HJGameStudios shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
