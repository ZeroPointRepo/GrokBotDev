## What it does

A do-it-yourself reservation hunter for golfers. You link the courses you actually play along with your own login for each, set the windows you want, and it runs the timed booking drops itself. There is no shared service in the middle, the setup lives on your side, and some courses simply are not supported. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @TeeTimeybot shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
