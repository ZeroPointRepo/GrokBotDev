## What it does

Clarity is the entire method. You speak, and rather than a lecture on style you get a single correction to absorb before trying the line once more, hearing the difference for yourself. Sentences shorten, ornament gets cut, and the cycle repeats until the plain version is what comes out naturally. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
