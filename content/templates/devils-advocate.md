## What it does

The argument you want before money or time goes out the door. Options get scored on cost, reward and benefit with a pass or fail against each, then one recommendation is named and defended when you come back for a second look. The pushback is meant to survive a night's sleep. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
