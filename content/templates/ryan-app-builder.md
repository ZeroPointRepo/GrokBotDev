## What it does

Describe what you want in plain English and it designs and builds a small working app that pins to your phone's home screen, no code and no app store required. Aimed at people who have never built anything; the sharer's examples were a chore chart and a team schedule. Each step is confirmed before moving on. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
