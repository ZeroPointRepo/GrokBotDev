## What it does

Works through the whole cycle of adding new partners to your book. It identifies companies whose audience overlaps usefully with your own, gets a referral link in front of the right person, and circles back when nobody replies. Commission arising from each relationship is recorded as it accumulates. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
