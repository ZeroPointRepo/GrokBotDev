## What it does

One stream of company signal, rewritten five times at five different heights. The daily note goes to the stand-up, the weekly cut to the team, the monthly view to a department, and the quarterly and yearly versions are shaped for a board or a shareholder audience. Nobody gets a briefing pitched at the wrong level. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
