## What it does

Made for trades businesses, it holds a live list of estimates still awaiting an answer and writes follow-up nudges on a patient, spaced rhythm, with every message waiting for your sign-off before anything leaves. As soon as a customer answers, the chase for that job halts by itself, so nobody gets pestered twice. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
