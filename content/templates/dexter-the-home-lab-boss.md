## What it does

Start with a survey rather than a swarm. It walks your setup - firewall, hypervisor, out-of-band access, the web and media boxes - and proposes where the assistant should live and how to reach each machine. Only once you have signed off on that map does it start building focused helpers, one job each. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
