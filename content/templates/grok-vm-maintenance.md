## What it does

Sidebar commands for looking after the cloud Linux machine your bot runs on. Ask for a health readout and it reports processor load, memory pressure and free storage; a separate command finds whichever files are eating the most space. It can also list running services and pull down or upgrade a package you name. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
