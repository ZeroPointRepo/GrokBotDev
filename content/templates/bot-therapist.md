## What it does

When bots start working at cross purposes, usually because a prompt was too vague, it runs the check-ins, works out who misunderstood what, and proposes repairs you can apply. Regular sessions are scheduled too, so a crew stays aligned instead of drifting. Nothing is fixed behind your back; you approve the repair.
