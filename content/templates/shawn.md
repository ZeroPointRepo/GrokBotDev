## What it does

A research desk for sports wagers that never touches real money. It tracks how closing lines move through the day and hunts for positive-expected-value spots once the bookmaker's margin is accounted for, keeping an auditable daily card and a ticket ledger on paper only. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
