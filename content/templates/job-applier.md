## What it does

Built for a search where volume matters. It starts by reading your CV and asking enough about you to work with, goes looking for openings that suit, and rewrites what you send so it speaks to that particular posting. What it will not do is invent experience you do not have. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @aroogle shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
