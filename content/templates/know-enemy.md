## What it does

You hand it the list of companies you want watched, and from then on it works strictly from material anyone could look up. Short briefings arrive at a regular beat with the movements worth knowing about, and in between you can call up a profile on any single rival the moment a deal or a launch makes it urgent. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
