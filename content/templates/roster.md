## What it does

You hand it the list of people, assistants, projects and tools you actually keep track of, and it maintains that roster as things change. Ask who has gone quiet and on what, and it answers strictly from your list - names are never invented. Useful for spotting which teammates, threads or bots need a nudge this week. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
