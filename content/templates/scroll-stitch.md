## What it does

Send overlapping captures of the same scrolling page, or a recording of a vertical scroll, and it trims the status bars and browser chrome, aligns the overlap, and returns one seamless tall image. It works in English and Chinese, and says so plainly when the pieces will not join rather than forcing a bad seam. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
