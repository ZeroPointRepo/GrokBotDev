## What it does

A sixty-second catch-up delivered before standup: which meetings you are walking into, which pull requests are stalled waiting on your review, where builds went red overnight, and the two or three messages that cannot wait until lunch. It reads across your calendar, repos and chat but changes nothing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
