## What it does

It reads flight and hotel confirmation email, notices when the promised points or nights never landed in your loyalty account, and then works through the airline or hotel's own claim flow to request the missing credit. You keep the trips; it chases the balance.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @carlosnoriega shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
