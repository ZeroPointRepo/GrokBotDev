## What it does

When one of your agents finishes a task, this one writes a receipt for it and files it away, recording what ran, when, and on which model or connector stack. Anything that speaks MCP works with it. Each evening it rolls the day up into one short skim — bookkeeping for agent work, not another dashboard to watch. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
