## What it does

Hand it a link to an episode or interview on YouTube, X or Spotify and it does the listening for you, coming back with a quick gist up top, a short list of concrete lessons you can actually put to work, and longer notes on the parts worth revisiting. Recaps can be filed straight into Notion. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @theadvisorbtc shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
