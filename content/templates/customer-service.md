## What it does

An order-desk assistant for small sellers who live in Gmail. It finds the orders still waiting to ship or needing a reship, then drafts the reply to each customer for your sign-off, so the awkward ones get answered the same day instead of compounding. You review and send; it keeps the queue from quietly rotting.
