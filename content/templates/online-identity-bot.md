## What it does

Once a day it looks for you the way a stranger would, querying the big search engines for your name, phone number, home address and email, then sorts anything newly surfaced into categories and sends back a tally. It only reads and records — it never signs into a removal service or files takedowns itself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
