## What it does

Paste a table or upload a CSV and it picks the form that truly fits the data, then makes the finding itself the headline. Already have a chart you half-distrust? Hand it over and it critiques it with a ranked list of fixes. It re-checks its own numbers, so the result holds up in a meeting. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
