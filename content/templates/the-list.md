## What it does

This bot posts a short note into the conversation you already have open, so keeping up no longer means going to look. Each note runs three lines and covers one fresh arrival or a pick from the directory. It sends no email and writes nothing for you; the whole job is telling you what has landed since you last looked. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GrokBotGod shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
