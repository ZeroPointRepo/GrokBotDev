## What it does

The List is the GrokBotGod in-bot newsletter. Install once; get three-line drops in this chat when new bots or directory picks land. No email. No yes/no opt-ins. Each drop runs three lines and covers one fresh arrival or a pick from the directory, so keeping up no longer means going to look. The bot sends no email and writes nothing for you; the whole job is telling you what has landed since you last looked, and it runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GrokBotGod shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
