## What it does

A research companion built around showing its work. It answers with sources attached, separates what the evidence supports from its own inference, and marks any claim it could not verify. Useful when you need to know which parts of an answer are solid ground and which are still a guess. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bramforge shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
