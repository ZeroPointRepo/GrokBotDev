## What it does

A month-end bookkeeper for freelancers and solo operators. Upload transactions, receipts and invoices and it matches documents to entries, flags duplicates and transfers that should not count twice, and hands back a clean ledger plus drafts of open invoices — everything a draft, nothing sent on its own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
