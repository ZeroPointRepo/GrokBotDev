## What it does

Each morning it walks GitHub's trending page and keeps only the projects touching the languages and tools you actually work in. What arrives is a short read: a line or two per repository on what it does and why someone with your setup would care, so you skim a page instead of scrolling hundreds. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Hussein_M_Ragab shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
