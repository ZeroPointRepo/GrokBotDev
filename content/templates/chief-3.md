## What it does

Made for founders running several projects at once. You bring it whatever lands first, and it either handles the item, sends it to one of your other bots, or keeps both sides moving until the job is done. The point is one starting place each day instead of five separate chats.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @franperez_fp shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
