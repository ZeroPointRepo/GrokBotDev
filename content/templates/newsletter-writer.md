## What it does

Puts together each month's newsletter out of the launches, wins and calendar highlights worth sharing, written the way you write, then leaves it parked as a draft so the final edit and the send remain yours. The recurring excuse that there was nothing to say stops working, because the material is already gathered. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
