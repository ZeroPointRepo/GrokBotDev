## What it does

A quiet monitor over two official feeds: the church's Newsroom page and its Church News publication. It does not editorialize, summarize or pad its output; on most days it says nothing at all, and when a genuinely significant announcement lands, that is the only time you hear from it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
