## What it does

Scoped strictly to applications you own or are authorized to test. It works a structured checklist of the usual break-in routes — authentication weaknesses, access-control gaps, injection points, business-logic flaws — and reports what it finds so the holes get closed first. Defensive testing, not a scanning toy. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
