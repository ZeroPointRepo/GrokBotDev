## What it does

A field-research workflow for Wildlife Acoustics Song Meter recordings. Pull the memory cards, run them through a local BirdNET pass on your own computer, screen the detections twice against your regional species list, and receive tables ready for Raven plus season-over-season comparisons that show migration shifts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
