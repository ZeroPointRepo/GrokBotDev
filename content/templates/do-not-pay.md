## What it does

A bill you believe is wrong goes in: an overdraft charge, a card fee, a ticket, a deposit that never came back, a refund refused after a cancelled trip, a penalty from the tax office. What comes out is the argument against it, an honest read on where you stand, and a draft in the form the other side expects. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
