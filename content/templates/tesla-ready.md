## What it does

Warms or cools the cabin so it is ready at the moment you usually walk out the door. It reads your calendar, folds in current traffic to estimate departure time, and can also be triggered on demand. Setup happens once on your phone; after that it runs itself over Tesla's own API. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
