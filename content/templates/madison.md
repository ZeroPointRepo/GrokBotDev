## What it does

Built for people pushing on public institutions, and for the assistants acting on their behalf. It covers records requests of the FOIA kind, court processes, school districts and benefits portals, laying out the coverage patterns those systems tend to follow so you know what to ask for and when. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
