## What it does

A setup companion for people who have never wired an API: it leads you through connecting a Bland-powered phone helper to your bot in plain-words steps. Once connected, that helper places errand calls and sends texts on your behalf. It closes with a guided test call so you hear it working before you rely on it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
