## What it does

Paste in a resume and the listing you are targeting, and get the resume reworked for that specific role, with the phrasing applicant-tracking systems look for. It also flags the gaps between what the posting asks for and what your resume actually shows, and it will not invent experience or numbers to fill them. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
