## What it does

Takes instructions or prompts that read badly and rewrites them under a published simplified-English standard. Along the way it names the specific phrases that are ambiguous, and where a concept is genuinely hard to put in words it adds a plain visual explanation instead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
