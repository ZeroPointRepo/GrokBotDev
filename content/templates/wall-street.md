## What it does

Twice each weekday it sits down with the open market, decides what an imaginary portfolio would hold and in what size, and writes the reasoning down beside it. Nothing is ever sent to a broker and no actual funds are at stake. It all stays on paper, so you can watch how a run of calls would have turned out. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
