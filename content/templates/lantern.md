## What it does

A companion for end-of-life planning that pairs with a free self-hosted 1Password utility. You prepare a vault your family could open without you, with the bot guiding each step and checking in before and after the handover. The vault itself never passes through it, so the transfer stays yours to make. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @sjcobs shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
