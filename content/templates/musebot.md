## What it does

Point it at an agent directory and it works out who is reachable, sets up the command-line tooling to talk to them, and opens the first contact. Every message or paid call stops for your approval before it goes out, so nothing gets signed or spent on the bot's own judgment. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @clawddevs shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
