## What it does

Instead of opening with colour and layout, it maps the path somebody takes through a service and pins down what each screen must do at every moment: at rest, focused, still loading, holding nothing yet, or gone wrong. It then specifies the small movements between them and keeps accessible patterns in from the outset. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
