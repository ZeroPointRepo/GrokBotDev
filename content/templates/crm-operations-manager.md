## What it does

Handles the unglamorous upkeep around every meeting: contact and org-chart records get refreshed before you dial in and tidied again afterwards, without anyone booking a cleanup pass. The database stays close to reality as a side effect of ordinary meetings rather than a project nobody staffs. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
