## What it does

Takes whatever life admin is stuck on your desk — a double-booked week, an unpaid bill, a trip that needs booking, a form that needs filling, a question that needs answering — and hands back one clear next step plus the drafts to carry it out. Nothing is sent, spent, deleted or published until you say yes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
