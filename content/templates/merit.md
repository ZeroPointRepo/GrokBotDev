## What it does

Once a week it sweeps a wide territory — civic claims, science, UAP and the paranormal — and puts each one through a scoring rubric instead of a vibe check. A running list of unresolved claims carries forward, and the whole thing lands as a single weekly brief for readers who would rather verify than believe. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
