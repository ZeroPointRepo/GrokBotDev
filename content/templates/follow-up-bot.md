## What it does

After a conference or a busy event week, hand it the contacts you collected and it drafts a personal follow-up for each one, by email or X direct message. Nothing goes out until you approve the wording. If a thread sits unanswered for a while, it nudges you with a short list of conversations worth picking back up.
