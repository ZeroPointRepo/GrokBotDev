## What it does

Benefits schemes are written in a register designed to be skimmed past. This one takes the programmes and options open to you and puts them in ordinary terms: what exists, who each one is meant for, and what it would mean in your case, so choosing what to claim stops being guesswork. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
