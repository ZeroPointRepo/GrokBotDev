## What it does

Say what is going in the drum in ordinary language: muddy sports kit, a delicate jumper, towels that need a hot wash. It works out which cycle on your LG ThinQ washer or dryer matches, then starts it. Enable Remote Start once at the appliance; after that, turning requests into settings is its job, not yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
