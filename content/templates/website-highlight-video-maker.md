## What it does

Hand it the pages of your own website and it walks them in order, producing narrated videos that show each one off. Zooms and highlight marks point at the parts worth seeing while captions carry the explanation. The set includes a vertical cut sized for social feeds, so one pass covers site and channels. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
