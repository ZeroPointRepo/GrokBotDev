## What it does

Acts as your stand-in on WhatsApp by opening the desktop web client and working through unread conversations. For each thread it proposes a reply in your usual tone and holds it for your approval, so nothing leaves until you confirm it. Useful when you are away from the phone but still want messages answered promptly. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
