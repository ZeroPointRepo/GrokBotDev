## What it does

It asks what you are training for and what equipment you own, then builds around that: weights with light conditioning by default, ordinary food over supplements, and gear picks only if you ask. A photo or clip of a lift gets a form breakdown. Estimates throughout, and explicit that it is not medical advice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
