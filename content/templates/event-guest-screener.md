## What it does

Grades each event applicant against the customer profile you define, approving the strongest fits in bulk inside the invite tool itself, so the room ends up full of the right kind of attendee. The review that used to eat an afternoon before a launch becomes a short check of the edge cases. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
