## What it does

An assembly seat inside Figma for product designers. It builds new frames and tidies existing ones with the library components already in the file, and it reads autolayout and spacing from the inspected properties rather than eyeballing them, so what comes back matches the file's own system. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
