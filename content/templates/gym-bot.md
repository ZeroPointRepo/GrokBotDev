## What it does

Log your training and your meals each day and it keeps the running picture alongside recovery numbers from your wearable. It renders a daily card stack so the whole week reads at a glance, suggests meals that fit what you are doing, and calls out the pattern when takeaway quietly becomes a habit. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
