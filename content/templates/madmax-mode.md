## What it does

Building a bot badly means a bloated brief and responsibilities scattered anywhere they happen to fit. This writes a short, sharp character for each new one and sorts every duty deliberately into a skill, a routine or a memory. When an action carries consequences it stops and waits rather than pressing on. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
