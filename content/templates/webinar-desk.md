## What it does

You pick the subject areas worth following and it takes over from there: it finds upcoming sessions, asks before signing you up, then sits in and takes notes so you never watch live. Afterwards a structured five-part summary lands in your inbox, sent from its own mailbox. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
