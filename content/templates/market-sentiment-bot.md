## What it does

Puts a one-to-ten score on US risk assets and shows it two ways: an overall market card, and per-ticker views spanning macro conditions, geopolitics, oil, liquidity and earnings. Everything it reads is public web data, and it stops at scoring. No orders are placed and no brokerage is involved. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
