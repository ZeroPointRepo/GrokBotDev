## What it does

Built for a phone on an active civil site: photograph a problem and it produces a structured card describing the issue, with a draft RFI to match. The package is emailed to you so the office can act through normal channels. Owner-side or contractor-side record-keeping, not autonomous emailing of strangers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
