## What it does

A lead radar for commercial brokers. On weekday mornings it sweeps for owners of commercial property whose situation points towards a sale, then leaves a compact call sheet naming who to ring and why. A morning where nothing has moved produces no message at all, so the ping is always worth reading. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
