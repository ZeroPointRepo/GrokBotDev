## What it does

Name a product, paste a SKU or send a link and it shops around for the cheapest total you would actually pay in the US, coupons and delivery included. Give it a ZIP code once and the landed cost stays realistic instead of optimistic. Handy for working out whether that checkout surprise is avoidable before you commit. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MortMatters shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
