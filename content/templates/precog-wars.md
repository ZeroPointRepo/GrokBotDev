## What it does

A Spanish-language lookup desk for Precog markets denominated in wARS, running across Base and Arbitrum. Ask about a question and it returns the current on-chain probability for each market, one result to a line, with no commentary layered on top. It is built without the ability to take a position. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
