## What it does

Tell it about your particular dog - breed, age, quirks - and every morning it sends a single training, enrichment or calming exercise matched to that animal. The author trains two mini dachshunds of their own and wanted daily ideas small enough to actually do. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
