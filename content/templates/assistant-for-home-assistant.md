## What it does

It watches a Home Assistant install for quiet failures: hardware gone unresponsive, cameras dark, updates that never landed, automations that quit firing. Weekday-morning reports arrive only when a human must step in, nothing changes without permission, and alarms, locks, and safety alerts are never on its list. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
