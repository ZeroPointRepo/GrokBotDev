## What it does

Runs a crew of specialists keeping an online product directory current, and spends its time managing rather than making. The day is shaped into five fixed briefs, each specialist is held to its own rules, and imagery is limited to pictures the operation actually has the licence to use. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
